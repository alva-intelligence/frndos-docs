# Audience Overview

**Audience** is where everyone your brand is working with lives: the contacts you uploaded or connected, and the followers pulled in from social. Instead of a customer list in one place and a follower list in another, both sit in one pillar, group into the same segments, and answer the same questions.

Everything is **scoped per brand**. The brand active in the sidebar decides whose people you're looking at.

> **Audience replaces Customer Intelligence.** If you used the old Customer Intelligence page, its URL now brings you here and the same data comes with it.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **Open the right brand** — from Home, click it under **Brands** in the sidebar. The **Audience** row sits in the brand's sidebar and is greyed out for a brand you don't have Audience access to.
2. ✅ **Access to the tab you need** — Overview, People and Segments are granted separately, so you may see some pills and not others. See [Inviting Team Members](../workspace/inviting-team-members).
3. ✅ **Enough credits** — enriching profiles is paid from your credit balance. See [Managing Workspace Credits](../workspace/managing-credits).

## The Three Tabs

Audience opens on **Overview**. The tab strip at the top carries:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | The AI summary of who this audience is, plus the headline numbers |
| **People** | Every individual — [Contacts and Followers](./people) |
| **Segments** | The groups they fall into, and the [personas](./segments) built from them |

A fourth pill, **Inbox**, is marked **Soon**. It doesn't open anything yet.

> **Each tab is its own permission.** A teammate can be given People without being given Segments. If a pill isn't in your strip, that tab wasn't granted to you for this brand, and a link straight to it lands on the first tab you can see.

**Sources is not a tab.** Where your people came from lives in **Audience Settings**, reached by the **Settings** button on Overview and People.

## The AI Summary

When the brand has people, the top of Overview is an **AI summary** headline describing who this audience is, with four figures beside it:

| Tile | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **People** | Contacts + followers together, labelled _"contacts + followers"_ |
| **Active segments** | How many segments are live, _"grouped by AI"_ |
| **Avg. lifetime value** | Average customer lifetime value, across _"customers with purchase data"_ |
| **Avg. purchase intent** | Average purchase-intent score, _"since last enrichment"_ |

A tile with nothing to report shows `—` rather than a zero.

> **Why People is one number and not two.** The question the pillar is named after is "how many people do we have", so the tile sums both sides. The two halves aren't counted identically and can't be: a contact is counted once enrichment completes, while every follower row counts, because a follower has no enrichment status.

**Refresh summary** rebuilds the narrative and the tiles. **Settings** opens Audience Settings on its Sources pane.

### Suggested actions

The summary can offer **suggested action** pills. Clicking one opens the AskFRND panel with the prompt already written, so you carry the question into chat instead of retyping it. See [Generating with Chat](../askfrnd/generating-with-chat).

## What's on the Overview Below the Summary

| Card | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Add people** | Opens the add-people flow, with a badge counting your sources |
| **Inbox** | Marked **Soon** — replies from Instagram, TikTok, email and WhatsApp, with a persona-aware draft assistant |
| **Lifecycle** | Marked **Soon** — where each cohort sits from awareness to loyalty, and which segment is stalling |
| **Reach a segment** | Marked **Soon** — turn a segment into an ad audience, a message, a journey or a survey. Lands with Growth |

The three **Soon** cards describe what's coming. They aren't links.

## Starting From Nothing

A brand with no people yet shows **No people yet**, explained as: _"Bring this brand's customers or followers in and the overview, segments and personas build from there."_ The **Add people** button beneath it is where to start. See [People](./people#adding-people).

The summary also shows a loading state while it's being generated, so a blank hero on a brand you just imported into means the write-up is still being built.

## FAQ

**Q: The Audience row in my sidebar is greyed out.**
That brand hasn't been granted Audience access for your role. Access is per brand, so another brand may still open.

**Q: I can see People but not Segments.**
The three tabs are granted separately. Ask a workspace admin to grant the tab you need.

**Q: Where did Customer Intelligence go?**
It became Audience. The old URL redirects here, and its Integrations tab is now the Sources pane in Audience Settings.

**Q: Why doesn't the People tile match the number of rows I count on the People tab?**
The tile sums contacts and followers. Contacts are counted once their enrichment has completed, so people still being processed aren't in it yet.

**Q: What is Inbox?**
A coming-soon tab. Its pill and its Overview card are both marked **Soon** and neither opens anything.
