# Calendar

**Calendar** puts your work and your meetings side by side. Tasks with due dates, campaign flights and team events from frndOS are already on it. Connect Lark Calendar and your meetings appear next to them.

> **Beta:** Calendar is rolling out. If **Calendar** isn't in your sidebar, it hasn't reached your workspace yet.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **Calendar in your sidebar**, see the note above
2. ✅ **A Lark account** (optional) to bring in your meetings

## Opening Calendar

Click **Calendar** in the sidebar (marked **Beta**). Home's **Schedule** card links here too (**Calendar →**).

## Connecting Your Calendar

The first time you open Calendar with nothing connected, a card explains: _"Your work already has dates. Bring your meetings in."_

| Option | Status |
|---|---|
| **Connect Lark Calendar**: your Lark meetings, rooms and Minutes, via the Lark plugin | Available |
| **Connect Google Calendar** | Soon |
| **Connect Microsoft 365** | Soon |

Click **Connect** on Lark and sign in. Then choose what shows (_"Choose what shows on your calendar"_) and click **Show my calendar**.

You can also click **Add without connecting** and plan with tasks, campaign dates and team events only.

> **Who sees what:** _"Only you see your personal calendars. Teammates only ever see free/busy."_

## The Side Rail

The rail has two modes, **Plan** and **Calendar**. Press **P** to switch. It follows the view (Plan in Week and Day, Calendar in Month and Agenda) until you pick one yourself.

### Plan

Plan is about today:

* **The date and one plain line**, for example **2 meetings left today**, **You're free after 17:00** or **Nothing else today**
* **Up next** (or **On now**), the meeting on now or next, with **Join** when it has a meeting link. If it's an invitation you haven't answered, it shows **Reply** instead
* **Needs a reply**, invitations waiting on you, each with **Yes** and **No**. A repeating meeting is one row; **Yes** and **No** answer the next occurrence only
* **To schedule**, tasks with no time yet. _"Drag a task onto your calendar to block time for it."_ This works in Week and Day

When the rail is on **Calendar**, a blue count on **Plan** shows how many replies and overdue tasks are waiting.

### Calendar

Calendar mode is about what you see:

* **A mini month** to jump to any date
* **Show**: switch **Meetings**, **Tasks** and **Campaigns** on or off, plus **Routine meetings**
* **Calendars**: **Team** (frndOS, shared), then your **Lark** calendars. **Manage calendars** picks which ones sync. Your primary calendar always syncs. **Add calendar** connects another account

### Sync now

The bottom of the rail shows when Lark last synced (for example **Synced 5 min ago**) and **Sync now**. Calendar also refreshes by itself when you come back to it and every couple of minutes while it's open. If you've just synced, it reads **Synced a moment ago**.

## Reading the Calendar

* **Meetings** are soft cards with the title and time. A client meeting tied to a brand is tinted with the brand's colour and shows its mark
* **Invitations that need a reply** are outlined, with **Reply**
* **Routine meetings**, short ones (30 minutes or less) that repeat daily, show as thin one-line strips in Week and Day so they don't crowd the grid. Turn them off under **Show**. In Month they're hidden by default
* **Tasks** with a due date sit in a **Tasks** lane at the top of each day, not on the hours. Tick one to complete it
* **Campaigns** run as bars across the **All day** band
* **An empty weekend** folds into one narrow column in Week view. Click it to open it

The Week view opens at 8:00, or a little earlier if your first meeting is earlier.

## A Meeting's Details

Click a meeting to see when it is, its guests and their answers (**Accepted**, **Declined**, **Maybe**, **Awaiting**), its location and description.

* **Accept**, **Maybe** or **Decline** answers an invitation (until the meeting has ended)
* **Prep with frnd** opens AskFRND with a request to prepare you for the meeting: agenda, context and the last deck
* **Open in Lark** opens it in Lark
* **Copy meeting link** copies the join link

## Adding an Event

Click **New event** (or press **N**), or drag across the grid. The popover has two tabs:

* **Quick**: type a sentence and press **Enter**, for example _"KV review thu 2pm 1h @alex #acme-q4"_. **@** adds a guest and **#** links a project. The popover shows when it lands and whether everyone is free
* **Detailed**: every field, including title, start and end, **All day**, project, location and meeting link. Switching from Quick carries over what you typed

## When Something Goes Wrong

| Notice | What to do |
|---|---|
| _"Couldn't load your calendar."_ | Click **Try again**. What was already loaded stays on screen |
| _"Lark Calendar disconnected. Reconnect to see new meetings."_ | Click **Reconnect**. Your meetings are still there |

## FAQ

**Q: Can I connect Google Calendar or Outlook?**
Not yet. Both are marked **Soon**. Lark Calendar is available now.

**Q: Can my teammates see my meetings?**
No. They only ever see free/busy. Your personal calendars are visible to you alone.

**Q: I accepted a meeting in Lark and it still says it needs a reply.**
Click **Sync now** at the bottom of the rail.

**Q: Why do some meetings show as thin strips?**
They're routine meetings: 30 minutes or less, repeating daily. Turn **Routine meetings** off under **Show** to hide them.

**Q: How do I block time for a task?**
In Week or Day, drag the task from **To schedule** onto the grid.

**Q: My weekend column is narrow.**
An empty weekend folds to save room. Click it to open it.
