# Tasks

**Tasks** is one page for the work your team owes: what's due, who has it, and where it stands. Tasks come from project rooms, from agents handing work back, from proposal scorecards, and from Lark or Google Tasks, and you can add your own.

> **Beta:** Tasks is rolling out. If **Tasks** isn't in your sidebar, it hasn't reached your workspace yet.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **Tasks in your sidebar** — see the note above
2. ✅ **Access to the brands and projects** the tasks belong to

## Opening Tasks

Click **Tasks** in the sidebar (marked **Beta**).

The header has **Search tasks** and **New task**. Below it, the toolbar keeps to three controls:

* **Scope tabs**: **All**, **Mine**, **Today**, **Overdue**. Each shows a count when it has tasks; Overdue's count is red
* **Filter**: narrow by brand and project. It reads **Filter · 1** when one is on
* **View**: pick **List** or **Board**, how the list is grouped (**Due date**, **Brand**, **Project** or **Status**), and the **Show done** and **Show archived** switches. The button names what you're looking at, for example **View · Due date**

The view you pick is remembered and kept in the page link, so sharing it shows the same list.

### Reading a row

A row only says what's true. The line under the title shows a status other than the default, the due date with how late it is (for example **12 Jun · 16 wk late** in red), where the task came from, and its checklist steps. A task with no assignee shows no "Unassigned" label. Its **•••** menu appears when you point at the row.

### Keeping Tasks in sync

Tasks refresh by themselves when you come back to the page, and every couple of minutes while it's open.

When your tasks come from Lark, the bottom of the **View** menu shows when they were last synced (for example **Synced 2 min ago**) and **Sync now**. Click it to pull your Lark tasks straight away. If you've just synced, it reads **Synced a moment ago**.

## The Board

**Board** shows a column per status:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Later** | Put down on purpose |
| **Not started** | To do |
| **Doing** | In progress |
| **Blocked** | Stuck on something |
| **Waiting for review** | An agent finished a step and handed the work back to you |
| **Done** | Finished |

Drag a card to another column to change its status. You can't drop a card on **Waiting for review**; agents put work there.

**Keyboard:** focus a card and press **Space** to pick it up, **←** / **→** to choose a column, then **Space** to drop it or **Esc** to cancel. **Enter** opens the card.

A column with no cards folds into a narrow strip so the board stays readable. It still takes a dropped card, and opens while you drag over it.

Each column has **+** to add a task straight into it. The **Done** column's menu has **Archive all done**.

## A Task's Details

Click a task to open its details on the right:

| Field | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Where it stands |
| **Due** | When it's due. Type a date in plain words, such as _"tomorrow"_, or pick a day |
| **Assignee** | A person or an agent. Suggested agents for the step are listed first |
| **Brand** / **Project** | Where the task belongs |
| **Source** | Where it came from, such as **Lark** or **Google Tasks** |

Below the fields are the **Description**, a checklist, and the task's **Discussion**.

* **Mark complete** (or **Reopen**) changes it in one click
* **Work on this task with frnd** opens AskFRND on the task
* The **⋯** menu has **Copy link**, **Archive task** and **Delete task**

Edits save when you leave a field, not on every keystroke.

### Checklists

**Add a step…** adds a checklist step, and a step can be assigned to an agent. **Work the list** starts the first unfinished step that has an agent, in a chat beside the task. It stops after that step: ticking it off is what starts the next one, so a stray click costs one step, not the whole list. The checklist shows its progress.

## Archiving

**Archive all done** puts every done task in the current view away, after confirming how many: _"They leave the board and stay in Show archived, where you can restore them."_ You can also archive a single task from its menu.

Turn on **Show archived** to see archived tasks and restore them.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I move a task into Waiting for review?**
No. That column is where agents hand work back for you to check.

**Q: Will changes sync back to Lark or Google Tasks?**
Tasks are kept in sync with Lark when your Lark account is connected; a task that came from Lark or Google Tasks says so under **Source**.

**Q: I just added a task in Lark and it isn't here yet.**
Open **View** and click **Sync now**.

**Q: Where did the old project-room checklist go?**
A project room's Tasks section still works, and its tasks also appear on this page.

**Q: I archived a task by mistake.**
Turn on **Show archived** and restore it.
