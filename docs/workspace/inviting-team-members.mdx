# Inviting Team Members

Invitations are sent from **Workspace Settings → People**. You enter one or more email addresses, choose whether each person joins as **Member** or **Admin**, and — for Members — pick which brands they can open and with what permission role.

This is the proactive path. The other way in is [Approving Join Requests](./approving-join-requests), where people find the workspace themselves and ask to join.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **You can manage the team** — the **Invite** button only appears if your role has full access to workspace team management. If you only see the member list without the button, ask an owner or admin.
2. ✅ **You're in an organization workspace** — **People** is not available in a personal workspace; opening workspace settings there sends you to **Credit Usage** instead.
3. ✅ **You have the email addresses** of the people you want to invite.
4. ✅ **Brands already exist** (optional) — brand access is assigned during the invite, so create the brand first if you want the invitee to land with access. See [Managing Multiple Brands](../brand-setup/multiple-brands).

## Step 1 — Open the People Page

1. Open **Workspace Settings** from your profile menu
2. In the left settings sidebar, under **Workspace**, click **People**

The page header is **People**. You'll see the member table, a **Search** box, and — if you can manage the team — the **Invite** button.

## Step 2 — Send the Invitation

Click **Invite**. A dialog opens titled **Invite to \{your workspace name\}**.

### Enter the email addresses

1. Type an address into the **Email Address** field — the placeholder reads `emails, comma separated`
2. Press **Enter**, or type a comma, to turn it into a chip
3. Repeat for as many people as you want — every chip has an **×** to remove it

Invalid input is caught before sending:

- `Please enter a valid email address` — the address isn't a valid email format
- `This email has already been added` — that address is already a chip in this dialog

### Choose the role

Under the email field you'll see a line like **You're inviting 3 guests as [Member ▾]**. The dropdown has exactly two options:

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| **Member** | Workspace access, plus whatever per-brand access you assign in the next step |
| **Admin** | Full workspace management — and full access to every brand automatically |

Pick **Admin** and the dialog states it directly: _Admins have full access to every brand in this workspace._ There is no brand picker for Admins because there's nothing to narrow down.

> **Owner isn't an invite option.** The **Owner** role exists in the member list (the person who created the workspace) but can't be assigned from the invite dialog.

### Assign brand access (Member only)

With **Member** selected, the line continues **with brand access [No brand access ▾]**. Open it and you get a two-column picker:

1. **Left column** — every brand in the workspace. Brands you've already given a role to are listed first with their role underneath; the rest follow.
2. **Right column** — the permission roles available in this workspace. Pick one to assign it, or choose **Remove access** to take the brand back off the list.

The button label summarizes your picks: **No brand access**, the brand's name when there's one, or **N brands** when there are several.

> **A member holds one role per brand.** Assigning a second role to the same brand replaces the first.

### Send

Click **Send Invite** (it reads **Sending Invite…** while it works). On success you get the toast **Invitation sent successfully!** and the dialog closes.

Two rejections come back from the server, named per address:

- `<email> is already a member of this workspace`
- `<email> already has a pending invitation to this workspace`

## Step 3 — What the Invited Person Does

The invitation email links to an invitation page in frndOS. What happens next depends on whether they already have an account.

### They already have a frndOS account

1. If they're not signed in, they're sent to the login page first, then back to the invitation
2. Once signed in, they see the workspace logo and **\{Inviter\} invited you to join \{workspace\}** — or **You're invited to join \{workspace\}** if the inviter's name isn't available
3. The screen explains: _Accept to join the workspace and switch into it, or reject to keep your current workspace._
4. **Accept invitation** → toast **Invitation accepted**, and they land on Home already switched into your workspace
5. **Reject invitation** → toast **Invitation declined**, and they stay in their current workspace

### They don't have an account yet

1. The invitation page detects there's no account and sends them straight to the sign-up form
2. The email field is **pre-filled and locked** — it can't be edited, so the invitation stays tied to the address you invited
3. After signing up, they're taken back to the invitation to accept it

> **Why is the email locked?** The invitation is bound to that address. Accepting from a different account returns the error _This invitation is for a different email address._

## Step 4 — Track and Manage Invitations

Back on **People**, the table has these columns:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Member** | Avatar and email address |
| **Status** | **Owner**, **Invited**, **Expired**, or **Active** |
| **Role** | Owner, Admin, or Member |
| **Brand access** | The brand name, **N+ access** for several brands, or **—** for none |
| **Last Active** | Relative time since their last activity |

Click any row to open the member detail panel — **Status**, **Role**, **Brand access**, **Job title**, **Last active**, **Joined date**, and **Remaining credits** when a credit allocation exists.

From that panel, if you can manage the team and the row isn't the Owner:

- **Resend invitation** — shown for **Invited** and **Expired** rows. Resending an expired invitation issues a fresh link.
- **Remove member** — opens the **Remove Team Member** confirmation. The dialog warns that the action can't be undone; confirm with **Remove Member**.

Role, brand access, and job title are editable in this panel for **Active** members who aren't the Owner.

> **Invitations expire after 24 hours.** After that the row flips to **Expired** and the old link stops working — the invitee sees _Your invitation link has either expired or is invalid._ Use **Resend invitation** to issue a new one.

## Assigning Brand Access to Several Members at Once

If people are already in the workspace, you don't have to edit them one by one:

1. On **People**, click **Assign brand access**
2. Tick the members you want — only **Active** members that aren't the Owner can be selected. The header checkbox selects all of them.
3. Pick a role from the **Role…** dropdown
4. Pick one or more brands from the **Brands…** dropdown
5. Click **Assign**

The bar shows **N members selected** as you go. Close it with the **×** on the right.

## Permission Roles for Brands

The roles you assign per brand are defined in **Workspace Settings → Permission Role** (visible to admins who can write to team settings). The page describes them as _permission sets that can be assigned to workspace members per brand_, split into two groups:

| Group | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **System Roles** | Built-in, fixed permissions. Marked **Locked** — you can view them but not edit or delete them. |
| **Workspace Roles** | Available in this workspace, and editable or deletable by admins. |

Every workspace starts with **Manager** and **Viewer** as locked system roles, plus **Data Analyst** and **Creative** as editable workspace roles. **Manager** grants full access across the brand; **Viewer** is view-only everywhere; **Data Analyst** covers the Insights surfaces; **Creative** covers the Studio generators.

To build your own, click **New Role**, give it a name (description optional), and set each brand feature to **No access**, **View only**, or **Full access** in the permission matrix. A workspace can hold up to **20 custom roles** — at the limit the **New Role** button is disabled.

> **Deleting a role removes access.** If the role is assigned, the confirmation tells you how many members across how many brands are affected — including members who haven't accepted their invitation yet.

## Inviting Someone to a Single Brand

You don't have to grant workspace-wide reach:

- **At invite time** — invite as **Member** and assign a role for just that one brand.
- **After they've joined** — open the brand's **Settings → Members** page. It has **Brand Members** and **Workspace Admins** tabs, and a **+ Add Member** button that pairs an existing workspace member with a permission role for that brand.

Workspace admins and owners always show as **All brands (Manager)** — the tooltip states _Workspace admins always have Manager access to all brands_ — so they can't be narrowed to a subset.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I invite several people at once?**
Yes. Add as many email chips as you need in one dialog. Everyone in that batch gets the same role and the same brand access.

**Q: Can I give different people different brand access in one go?**
No. One invite dialog sends one role plus one set of brand assignments. Send a separate invite for a different combination, or adjust brand access afterwards from the member's detail panel.

**Q: The person I invited already has an account somewhere else — do they need a new one?**
No. The invitation is tied to the email address. They sign in with their existing account and accept, and accepting switches them into your workspace.

**Q: Can I cancel an invitation before it's accepted?**
Yes. Open the **Invited** row and use **Remove member** — that clears the pending invitation.

**Q: How long is an invitation valid?**
24 hours. After that the row shows **Expired**; use **Resend invitation** to send a fresh link.

**Q: They never got the email.**
Check the address on the **Invited** row, have them look in spam, then use **Resend invitation**. If their mail server blocks it, invite a different address instead.

**Q: Can I change someone's role after they join?**
Yes. Click their row on **People** and change **Role** (Member ↔ Admin) or **Brand access** in the detail panel. This is limited to **Active** members other than the Owner.

**Q: What about credits for new members?**
Credit allocation is handled separately, in the credits section of workspace settings. See [Managing Workspace Credits](./managing-credits).
