# Notification Settings

Following brands and views is only useful if the alerts arrive the way you want them. **Notifications** is where you set how much reaches you, through which channel, and when frndOS should leave you alone.

> **Personal, not administrative:** these are **your own** preferences for the things **you** follow, scoped to the current workspace. Everyone sees this screen, including in a personal workspace — you don't need admin rights, and changing it doesn't affect anyone else.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **Something you follow** — a brand or a saved view; see [Collaborating on Insights](../insights/collaborating-on-insights)

## Opening the Page

**Workspace Settings → Notifications**, under the **You** section of the settings sidebar.

## How Much You Hear

Three levels, from loudest to quietest:

| Level | What gets through |
|---|---|
| **Everything** | _"Including routine movement. Most of it lands in your digest."_ |
| **Worth knowing** | _"Changes worth seeing today. The default."_ |
| **Only urgent** | _"Budget overruns and deadlines. Nothing else reaches you."_ |

## Where It Reaches You

Toggle each channel on or off:

| Channel | What it is |
|---|---|
| **In-app inbox** | _"Always kept — this is the record you can come back to."_ |
| **Browser push** | _"A notification while you're away from the tab."_ |
| **Digest** | _"One roll-up instead of a stream."_ |
| **Lark** | Marked **Soon** — _"Not connected yet."_ |
| **Desktop app** | Marked **Soon** — _"Not connected yet."_ |

> **Heads-up:** **Lark** and **Desktop app** are shown but disabled. They're listed so you can see what's coming, and can't be switched on into silence.

## Digest

A digest is _"one roll-up of everything that moved on what you follow. Routine changes only ever appear here — they never interrupt you."_

Pick a cadence:

| Cadence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Daily** | One roll-up a day |
| **Weekly** | One roll-up a week |
| **Off** | No digest |

> **Tip:** if you want to stay informed without being interrupted, set **How much you hear** to **Everything** and rely on the digest — routine movement never interrupts you, it only lands there.

## Quiet Hours

Turn on **Quiet hours** and set a **From** and **To** time. Your timezone is shown beside the times.

> _"Nothing interrupts you between these times. Your inbox and digest still collect everything — quiet hours defer, they don't discard."_

Defaults are **22:00** to **07:00** when you first switch it on.

## Saving

Changes save as you make them. If one doesn't go through, a message says so: _"That change didn't save. Your previous settings are still active."_

## Reading a Notification From Another Workspace

If you're in more than one workspace, the inbox shows them all — and each row **says which workspace it belongs to** with a small chip on its bottom line, next to the category and the time.

Opening a row from a different workspace **switches you there first**, then lands you on the item. A toast confirms it: **Switched to workspace &lt;name&gt;**.

> **Why the switch matters:** everything in frndOS is scoped to your active workspace, so a link into another one would simply not resolve without it. Rows from your current workspace navigate directly, with no switch and no toast.

If the workspace has since been deleted, or your membership was removed, nothing navigates and you get **Workspace no longer available**.

Rows that belong to no workspace — system notifications — carry no chip.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these settings affect my teammates?**
No. They're your personal preferences for the current workspace.

**Q: Do I need to be an admin?**
No. Everyone has notification preferences, in shared and personal workspaces alike.

**Q: Will quiet hours make me miss something?**
No. Your inbox and digest still collect everything — quiet hours only defer the interruption.

**Q: Why can't I switch on Lark or the desktop app?**
Neither is connected yet. They're shown disabled rather than hidden so you can see they're on the way.

**Q: Where do the in-app notifications appear?**
In the bell popover in the sidebar header.

**Q: I clicked a notification and my whole workspace changed.**
That's intentional. The item lives in another workspace, and frndOS scopes everything by the active one — so it switches you there and tells you it did.

**Q: How do I tell which workspace a notification is from before clicking?**
The chip on the row's bottom line names it. No chip means it's a system notification with no workspace.

**Q: A notification says "Workspace no longer available".**
The workspace was deleted, or you're no longer a member. Nothing is opened.

**Q: I turned everything on and it's still quiet.**
Check what you actually follow. Notifications come from brands and views you've followed — see [Collaborating on Insights](../insights/collaborating-on-insights).
