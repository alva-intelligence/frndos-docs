# Apps: Choosing What Your Workspace Uses

In frndOS, the brand tools you open from the sidebar are **apps**: Insights, Radar, Research, Audience and more. Admins choose which apps the workspace uses, and each member chooses which of those to keep in their own sidebar.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ You are in a **team workspace**. Personal workspaces have no Apps settings.
2. ✅ To turn apps on or off you need permission to change workspace settings (owners and admins). Everyone else can pin and unpin.

## The Apps Section in the Sidebar

The sidebar's **Modules** section is called **Apps**. It lists the apps you have pinned, for the brand you're working on. Each row opens that app on the first tab you can read.

* An app your role doesn't grant for the current brand is greyed out. Ask a workspace admin for access.
* The app you're in always has a row, even if you haven't pinned it.
* **Research** is marked **Beta**, and shows how many studies are ready.

Until you pin anything yourself, every app that is on shows in your sidebar.

## All Apps

**See all** on the **Apps** header opens **All apps**. On the collapsed sidebar, the same panel opens from the **All apps** button.

* Every app that is on appears as an icon. Click it to open it.
* The pin in an icon's corner adds the app to your sidebar, or removes it (**Pin … to your sidebar** and **Unpin …**). There is no limit on how many you pin.
* The header counts them, for example **3 of 4 pinned**, and the footer reminds you **Pinned apps show in your sidebar.**
* Apps an admin has turned off are not listed.
* Admins also see **Manage apps**, which opens the settings page below.

If saving your pins fails, a notice says **Couldn't update your pins** and your sidebar goes back to how it was.

## Turning Apps On or Off (Admins)

Open **Workspace Settings → Apps**. The page reads _"Choose the apps this workspace uses. Turning one off hides it for everyone; its data stays, and turning it back on brings everything back."_

Each row shows the app, a one-line description, and who last changed it and when, for example **On since 3 Nov 2026 · turned on by** a name, or **Off · hidden from everyone**. A counter such as **5 of 7 on** sits at the top of the list.

**Turning an app on** takes effect at once. A notice reads **Insights is on** (with the app's own name), and everyone in the workspace can open it again.

**Turning an app off** asks first, in a dialog titled **Turn off** followed by the app's name and **for this workspace?** that says _"Nothing is deleted: turn it back on to pick up where you left off."_ It also tells you what the change touches, for example how many members lose the app from their sidebar and All apps. Choose **Turn off** with the app's name to confirm, or **Cancel**.

### What happens to a member who opens an app that is off

They see a page titled with the app's name, then **is off in** and your workspace's name, who turned it off and when, and that everything is still there. An admin gets **Turn on** with the app's name, and **Manage apps**. A member is told **Ask a workspace admin to turn it back on.** and gets **Go to Home**.

The same applies behind the scenes: AskFRND tools and the frndOS MCP tools for an app that is off are refused too.

### Studio

**Studio** has no page in the sidebar. Its doors are the create menus (the **Canvas**, KV and **Resize** entries). Turning Studio off removes those rows. The KV files themselves stay in **Files**.

### Always on

Some areas are not apps and can't be turned off: **Tasks**, **Home**, **Files**, **Brands** and **Chats**. **Calendar**, **myFRND** and **Customize** stay in the sidebar as they are.

## FAQ

**Q: I can't find an app I used to have.**
Check **All apps**. If it isn't there, an admin turned it off. If it's there but unpinned, pin it.

**Q: Does turning an app off delete anything?**
No. Data is kept and comes back when the app is turned on again. The dialog tells you what pauses in the meantime.

**Q: I don't see Apps in Workspace Settings.**
You need permission to change workspace settings, and it isn't shown in a personal workspace.

**Q: Do my pins affect other people?**
No. Pins are per person. Turning an app on or off is workspace-wide.
