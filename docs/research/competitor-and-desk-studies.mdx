# Competitor and Desk Studies

Two Research methods send a **research agent** to find the answer for you, with every claim cited:

| Method | Use it to | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Size up a competitor** (Competitor) | Compare your brand with a competitor on positioning, pricing, channels and more | A cited side-by-side |
| **Find out what's known** (Desk research) | Answer a question from what's already out there | Cited findings from the web |

Both open with the answer, then show how they got there.

> **Rolling out:** the answer-first results page may reach workspaces gradually. If your study looks different, refresh the page.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **Research access** for the brand, see [Research Overview](./overview)
2. ✅ **Credits.** Agent studies draw credits

## Step 1 — Start the Study

On the brand's **Research** Overview, pick the method under **Or choose a method**, or type your question in **What do you want to find out?** and let Research suggest one. The **Size up a competitor** starter fills in a ready-made competitor question.

The study walks through four steps: **Question**, **Design**, **Who answers** and **Review**.

## Step 2 — Design It

| Method | What you fill in |
|---|---|
| **Size up a competitor** | The competitor: a brand name, a URL, or a comparison such as _"Aqua vs. Le Minerale in Jakarta"_. You can also paste a link or drop a file with what you already know |
| **Find out what's known** | Your **Research question** |

Under **Where to look**, pick what the agent may search: **Web**, **Brand IQ**, **Insights data**, **Listening corpus** and **Past studies**. A source that isn't connected yet says so and offers to connect it.

Under **Web search** you can set:

* **Search depth**: **Quick** (fewer, faster searches, for a known landscape) or **Thorough** (deeper search, more sources per query, takes longer)
* **Market**: a country, or **Anywhere**
* **Prefer these domains**: sites the agent looks at first. Other sources can still appear
* **Exclude these domains**: sites to leave out

**Who answers** needs nothing here: agent methods don't recruit a panel. On **Review**, check the study name and click **Launch study**.

## While It Runs

The study page shows the agent's real steps as it works: **Planned the search**, **Searched the web and your sources**, **Reading sources** (with the pages it's reading), writing, and **Checking every citation**.

You can leave the page. If you launched the study, you get a notification when it's ready or if it didn't finish. See [Knowing when a study is done](./overview#knowing-when-a-study-is-done).

## Reading the Results

The results read top to bottom:

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **The answer** | The answer in plain words, with how confident it is: **Confident**, **Fairly confident** or **Low confidence** |
| **Side by side** (Competitor) | A comparison table. _"Green marks who leads"_, with how many sources back each row |
| **Key facts** (Desk research) | The facts that matter. _"Each one links to its source"_ |
| **What we found** | Each finding with its confidence and the sources behind it. Point at a source to see its title, site and the passage quoted |
| **What to do next** | Suggested next steps |
| **Sources** | Every source the research found, grouped: **Web**, your connected sources, and **Other sources** |
| **How this was researched** | The search depth, when it finished, and how the agent worked |

Each finding has **Save to Brand IQ**, which saves it to the brand's knowledge with its citation.

Confidence reflects how many independent sources agree. Every citation is checked against the sources the study found, and anything that can't be traced is left out.

## Asking FRND About the Study

Under the answer, chips open AskFRND on the study's own chat: **Shorter**, **For a client**, **What should we do first?** and **What does this mean for us?** Further down, **Ask FRND about this study** takes questions, a look-up, or (for a competitor study) another competitor to compare.

## FAQ

**Q: What does Low confidence mean?**
Few independent sources agreed. Read the sources behind each finding before acting on it.

**Q: Can I point the agent at sites I trust?**
Yes. Add them under **Prefer these domains**. To keep a site out, add it under **Exclude these domains**.

**Q: Do I need to stay on the page while it runs?**
No. You're notified when it's ready if you launched it.

**Q: Can I use a finding elsewhere in frndOS?**
Click **Save to Brand IQ** on the finding. AskFRND and other tools then read it with the brand's knowledge.
