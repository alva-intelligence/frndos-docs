# Research Overview

**Research** is where you get to know your audience — before you launch a campaign, or afterwards to measure how it landed. Instead of guessing, you gather real signal and feed it back into your brand work.

Research runs **studies**: surveys with a real panel, scorecards that review your work before it runs, and research agents that find the answer on the web and in your connected sources.

> **Looking for Customer Intelligence?** It became the [Audience](../audience/overview) pillar, with its own sidebar row. Contacts, followers, segments and personas all live there now.

## Where to Find Research

1. Log in to **frndOS**
2. Open **Research** under **Modules** in the sidebar. It opens for the brand in the page title
3. To work on another brand, click the brand name in the page title and pick it

> **Why select the brand first?** Research tools are brand-aware: every study you run is filed under the brand in the page title. Picking the right brand before you start keeps your results with the correct brand.

Research has two tabs at the top: **Overview** and **Studies**. The gear beside them opens [Research sources](#research-sources).

> **Rolling out:** the Overview described here may reach workspaces gradually. If yours looks different, refresh the page.

## Starting With a Question

The Overview opens with **What do you want to find out?** _"Ask a question or drop a file. We suggest the right study and show the time and cost before anything runs."_

Type your question in the box (_"Ask about your brand, a competitor, an audience or a creative…"_), add **Who should answer?** if you know, and click **Suggest a study**. Research suggests a method and shows who answers, how long it takes and the credits. You can change it before anything runs.

Starter buttons under the box fill in a ready-made question: **Size up a competitor**, **Test a KV or pack**, **What are people saying about us?**, and **Import past research**, which attaches a file.

> **Dropped a proposal?** If you drop or attach one deck or document that reads like a proposal, a line under the box offers _"Looks like a proposal. Review it with the Proposal Scorecard?"_ Click **Review it** to open the scorecard with the file already in it. Dropping a proposal anywhere else in the brand offers **Review this proposal** too. See [Proposal Scorecard](./proposal-scorecard).

### Your studies on the Overview

Under the box, **Your studies** lists the brand's latest studies with one line of stats. **See all studies** opens the **Studies** tab. On your first visit it shows **How research works** instead: ask or drop a file, check the suggestion, read the findings.

### Choosing a method

**Or choose a method** lists every method as a card, with what you get and how long it takes:

| Card | Method | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Test a proposal before it runs** | Proposal Scorecard | A score and what to fix, in about 3 minutes. See [Proposal Scorecard](./proposal-scorecard) |
| **Ask the market** | Survey | Answers from a real panel, in 2–5 days. See [Creating a General Survey](./creating-a-general-survey) |
| **Understand a group of people** | Persona generator | Personas from your audience. Opens in [Audience](../audience/segments) |
| **Test a concept, pack or KV** | Creative Lab | Reactions from synthetic personas |
| **Size up a competitor** | Competitor | A cited side-by-side. See [Competitor and Desk Studies](./competitor-and-desk-studies) |
| **Find out what's known** | Desk research | Cited findings from the web. See [Competitor and Desk Studies](./competitor-and-desk-studies) |
| **Hear what people are saying** | Conversation deep-dive | Themes from social talk |
| **Track brand health** | Tracker | Marked **Soon**. Click **Notify me** to hear when it's ready |

A method you can't use for your role isn't offered. **Understand a group of people** needs Audience access.

## The Studies Tab

The **Studies** tab at the top of Research lists every study for the brand in one table. The line under the title sums it up, for example _"12 studies · 2 running · 340 credits this month"_.

Above the table, quick filters narrow the list by status, each with its count: **All**, **Ready**, **Running**, **Drafts**, **Archived**. Next to them are **Search studies** and a filter menu for **Method** and **Owner**.

The table has five columns:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Study** | The study's name, with its method underneath |
| **Status** | **Running**, **Ready**, **Draft** or **Archived**. A running survey also shows its progress, for example `12/30` |
| **Owner** | Who started it |
| **Credits** | What it cost, left blank when there's nothing to show |
| **Updated** | When it last changed |

Point at a row for **Open**. A running study that frndOS researches on its own also gets a **⋯** menu with **Stop study**, which asks before it stops. Click a column heading to sort by it.

### Knowing when a study is done

You don't need to keep a study open while it runs. Whoever launches a study gets one notification when it's ready (**Study ready**), or when it didn't finish (**Study didn't finish**), with a link straight to it.

While a study runs, its page shows _"You'll be notified when it's ready"_. Click **Turn off** to stop the notification, or **Notify me when it's ready** to turn it back on. Surveys follow their own response count and don't send this notification.

## Research Sources

The gear on the Research tabs opens **Research sources**: _"What this brand's studies can look at."_

| Section | What it lists |
|---|---|
| **Always used** | **Web search**: searches and reads the open web, and cites what it reads |
| **Your plugins** | Plugins you've connected that cover research |
| **Suggested for research** | A few more plugins worth connecting, each with **Connect** (or **Finish connecting**). The rest are in [myFRND](../getting-started/myfrnd) |
| **Survey panel** | **Populix**: _"Real people answer your surveys. Uses credits."_ |

> **Where did Research Settings go?** Its Sources page is now this panel. Old links to it open the panel.

## Per-Brand & Team-Only

Research is **scoped per brand** and only available in a **team workspace**:

- Every survey belongs to a brand — the **Client** column on the Surveys list shows which one.
- Research isn't available in a **Personal** workspace. If you're in one, frndOS sends you back to Home.
- If you handle multiple brands, switch between them by clicking the brand name in the page title.

See: [Working with Multiple Brands](../brand-setup/multiple-brands)

## Getting Started with Surveys

To run your first survey for a brand:

1. ✅ **Open the right brand** and go to **Research** → [Creating a General Survey](./creating-a-general-survey)
2. ✅ **Track it** as responses come in → [Managing & Tracking Your Surveys](./managing-surveys)

> **Heads-up:** Surveys are paid from your credit balance, and you'll need survey access on your role. See [Managing Workspace Credits](../workspace/managing-credits) and [Inviting Team Members](../workspace/inviting-team-members).

## Understanding Your Audience

Your brand's contacts, followers, segments and personas are no longer part of Research. They live in the **Audience** pillar:

1. ✅ **Meet the pillar** and its three tabs → [Audience Overview](../audience/overview)
2. ✅ **Bring people in** and enrich them → [People — Contacts and Followers](../audience/people)
3. ✅ **Group them** and build personas → [Segments and Personas](../audience/segments)

## FAQ

**Q: I don't see Research, or the tools are greyed out.**
Research is team-only and permission-gated. Make sure you're in a team workspace (not Personal), and that your role has survey access — ask a workspace admin if unsure.

**Q: I left a study running. How will I know it finished?**
You get one notification when it's ready or if it didn't finish, if you launched it. See [Knowing when a study is done](#knowing-when-a-study-is-done).

**Q: Which brand do my surveys belong to?**
The brand that was active when you created them. You can reassign a survey to another brand later. See [Assigning a Brand & Sharing Surveys](./assigning-brand-and-sharing).

**Q: When is the Tracker coming?**
**Track brand health** is marked **Soon**. Click **Notify me** on its card to hear when it's ready.

**Q: Do I have to pick a method myself?**
No. Type your question in **What do you want to find out?** and click **Suggest a study**. Research suggests the method, and you can change it.

**Q: Where did Customer Intelligence and Audience Personas go?**
They became the [Audience](../audience/overview) pillar. Personas now sit on the Segments tab there.
