# Audience Personas — From Followers to People

**Audience Personas** takes the people who actually follow a brand's Instagram account and turns them into something you can use: grouped segments, and named personas with real characteristics behind them. You can also write a persona from scratch when you have someone specific in mind.

> **Beta feature:** Audience Personas is gated per workspace. If **Audience Personas** isn't available in Research, it isn't enabled yet — see [Turning On Experimental Features](../settings/experimental-features).

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **A brand with an Instagram account** — connected, or one you can name
2. ✅ **The feature enabled for your workspace** — see the note above
3. ✅ **A customer list** (optional) — customers who are also followers give a richer signal

## Opening It

Inside a brand: **Research → Audience Personas**.

Three tabs:

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Audience Table** | Every follower analysed, one row each |
| **Segments** | The groups the analysis found |
| **Audience Personas** | The personas built from those groups |

## Generating Personas From Followers

The generator is headed **Generate audience personas** — _"Analyze the people who follow an Instagram account and turn them into audience personas."_

1. Check the **Instagram account** field. If the brand has a connected account it's filled in for you (_"Prefilled from the brand's connected Instagram account."_); otherwise type the handle (_"No connected Instagram account — enter the account to analyze."_)
2. Decide on **Include customer list** — _"Followers who are also customers contribute richer signal."_
3. Click **Generate personas**

> **Heads-up:** the handle accepts letters, numbers, dots and underscores only, up to 30 characters. An invalid handle blocks the button.

### While it runs

The progress panel names the stage it's in:

| Stage | Message |
|---|---|
| Fetching | **Fetching and enriching followers…** |
| Enriching | **Enriching follower profiles…** |
| Building | **Building personas from enriched followers…** |

When it finishes you get **Audience segments updated**. If your existing segments already covered the new audience, it says so — _"Existing segments absorbed the new audience — personas kept as-is."_

## Writing a Custom Persona

Have someone specific in mind? Click **Create persona** to open **Create a custom persona** — _"Describe the person you have in mind and generate a single persona from it — no follower analysis involved."_

Describe them in plain language. The box suggests the level of detail:

> _"e.g. A Gen Z college student in Bandung who games after class, follows K-pop comebacks, and buys snacks at the minimarket almost daily"_

You'll see **Custom persona queued** — _"It will appear in the list below once generated (~1 minute)."_ A toast tells you when it's ready: **Custom persona generated**.

## Working With the Personas

Two counters sit at the top: **Segments** and **Personas**.

Below them, controls for the grid:

| Control | Options |
|---|---|
| Columns | How many personas per row |
| Filter | **All personas** · **From followers** · **Custom** |
| Sort | **Newest** · **Name (A–Z)** |

Follower-derived personas are grouped under the segment they came from, ordered by how much of the audience each segment represents. Custom personas are collected in their own **Custom personas** section.

Click a persona card to open its detail. Once a persona is finished you can also **chat with it** — ask it questions as if it were the person.

> **Note:** a persona still generating shows in place, so you can see it's coming. A failed one tells you why.

## Segments

The **Segments** tab lists the **AI Generated Segments** the analysis found, each with its follower count and a **View Segment** action that opens the people inside it.

Empty until you've run a generation: _"Generate audience personas from the brand's Instagram followers to create segments."_

## The Audience Table

The **Audience Table** is every analysed person, searchable by name, username, or source.

Columns: **Name**, **Platform**, **Username**, **Source**, **Description**, **Followers**, **Passion Points**, **Hobbies**, **SES**, **AI Segment**, **Intent**, **Verification**, **Created**.

The **Source** column tells you where the person came from:

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Follower** | Found through the Instagram analysis |
| **Customer** | From your customer list |
| **Follower + Customer** | Both — the richest signal |

## FAQ

**Q: I don't see Audience Personas in Research.**
It's gated per workspace. Ask an admin — see [Turning On Experimental Features](../settings/experimental-features).

**Q: Do I need a connected Instagram account?**
No. If one is connected the handle is prefilled; otherwise type the account you want analysed.

**Q: What does "Include customer list" change?**
Followers who are also customers contribute richer signal to the analysis. It needs a customer list — see [Importing Customers](./importing-customers).

**Q: How long does it take?**
Follower analysis runs in stages and reports each one. A single custom persona takes about a minute.

**Q: I re-ran it and no new segments appeared.**
Your existing segments absorbed the new audience, and your personas were kept as they were. frndOS says so when that happens.

**Q: What's the difference between a follower persona and a custom one?**
A follower persona is derived from real people the analysis found and belongs to a segment. A custom persona is one you described, with no follower analysis behind it.

**Q: Can I ask a persona questions?**
Yes — once it's finished generating, open it and chat with it.
