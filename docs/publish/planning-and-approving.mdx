# Planning & Approving Posts

**Every post in Publish is planned, then approved, before it can go out.** This guide covers building a post, choosing where it goes, handing the creative to a designer, and how approval works for your brand.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **You can edit the brand's Publish plan.** See [Publish Overview](./overview#before-you-start).
2. ✅ **The brand has at least one connected account**, so posts have somewhere to go. Planning works without one.

## Step 1 — Add a Post

Add a row to the plan, or press **New post** on an empty plan. You can also ask frnd in the plan's chat to draft posts; on an empty plan, **Plan the week with FRND** drafts a week for you.

Open the post and fill in what it needs:

* **Post** — a working title
* **Date** — the day it's planned for
* **Format** — Feed post, Carousel, Reel, Story, Video, Short or Text only
* **Owner** — who the post belongs to
* **Creative** — the image, video or carousel files
* **Caption** — the words that go out

The post's **Checks** tab lists what's still missing (a creative, a caption, a date, an owner) and reads **Ready to go** when nothing is.

## Step 2 — Choose the Accounts

A post goes to one or more of the brand's accounts, up to 10.

* **In the post:** press **Add an account** and pick one. When the brand has one account that fits, the button reads **Add** followed by its name.
* **In the Table:** once a post has accounts, its **Channel** cell lists them. Click it to tick more accounts or untick one.

Each account can have its own caption. Until you write one, an account's caption reads **Caption · same as Main** and uses the post's main caption. Once adapted, edits to Main won't overwrite it; **Use Main's** goes back to the main caption.

> **Heads-up:** one approval covers every account on the post, so adapted captions are approved together with the rest.

If the brand has no accounts connected yet, the post shows **Connect an account**, which takes you to the brand's account connections in Insights.

## Step 3 (optional) — Hand the Creative to a Designer

When someone else makes the creative, press **Hand to a designer** beside the post's creative and fill in:

* **Brief** — what to make, the look, what to avoid
* **References** — links, each with a note on what to take from it
* **Sizes** — Feed 4:5, Feed 1:1, Story 9:16, Reel 9:16, Landscape 16:9
* **Text on the image** — headlines, prices or logos, each as an editable layer
* **Designer** and **Due**

Press **Hand to** followed by the designer's name. You stay the owner and the post stays where it is. The designer gets a task linked to the post, and the post shows who it's with and when it's due.

When the designer delivers, the creative is on the post. Press **Use this** to keep it, or **Send back** with notes pinned on the picture (or on the whole design). **Take it back** or **Hand to someone else** changes the hand-off while it's open.

## Step 4 — Send for Approval

When the post is ready, press **Send for approval** at the top right of the post.

1. **Approver**: leave it on **Anyone who can approve**, or pick one person to ask.
2. **They'll be told** shows who gets notified.
3. Add a note if you want one.
4. Press **Send for approval**.

The post moves to **Needs review**. You can keep editing, but a change after it's approved needs approval again.

> **Not every post needs this step.** If the brand's approval settings make a kind of post optional, its button reads **Approve** instead, and the owner can approve it directly. See [Approval Settings](#approval-settings).

## Step 5 — Approve or Request Changes

Open the **Approvals** tab. It has two lists:

* **Waiting on me** — posts you can approve
* **Sent by me** — posts you sent that are still waiting

Pick a post to see it, then:

* **Approve** — moves it to **Approved**, ready to schedule
* **Request changes** — write what should change and press **Send back**. The post goes back to **Drafting**, and whoever sent it is told, with your note.
* **Suggest edit** — propose a new caption. Someone who can edit the plan can **Accept** or **Reject** it.

You can also approve from the post itself: **Approve** and **Request changes** sit at the top right while the post waits for you.

### Who can approve a post

A post that needs approval is approved by someone other than:

* the person who sent it for approval
* its owner
* anyone who worked on it since it was last approved

If that's you, the post explains why and someone else approves it. People who can only view the plan can still suggest edits.

## Approval Settings

Open **Approval settings** from the **Approvals** tab, or from the plan's **•••** menu. For each kind of post, choose **Needs approval** or **Optional**:

| Kind of post | Covers | Starts as |
|---|---|---|
| **Campaign posts** | Posts made for a campaign | Needs approval |
| **FRND drafts** | Posts FRND drafted in chat or planned for you | Needs approval |
| **Made by hand** | Posts someone on the team made themselves | Optional |

**Creators can approve their own posts** is for small teams: when it's on, whoever made, sent or owns a post may approve it. Approval is still a step before it's scheduled.

Only brand managers change these settings (the **Publish settings** row of their brand role). Everyone else with access sees them read-only.

## Editing After Approval

Edits after approval send the post back for approval. If the post was already scheduled, changing its creative or caption takes it off the schedule; moving its day keeps its approval. When an edit took a post off the schedule, approving it again puts it back on, at its time if that's still ahead.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I approve several posts at once?**
Yes. Select them in the plan and press **Approve** in the selection bar. Posts that must be sent for approval first go to **Needs review** instead.

**Q: Why can't I approve a post that's waiting?**
You sent it, own it, or worked on it since it was last approved, so someone else approves it. The post says which. A brand manager can turn on **Creators can approve their own posts** under **Approval settings**.

**Q: What happens to a rejected post?**
It needs to go **Back to drafting** before it can be sent for approval again.
