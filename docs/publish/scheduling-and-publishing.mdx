# Scheduling & Publishing Posts

**Once a post is approved, frndOS can send it to its accounts for you.** Schedule it for its date, or publish it now. Every send goes through a short check first, so nothing goes out unseen.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **The post is Approved.** See [Planning & Approving Posts](./planning-and-approving).
2. ✅ **It has at least one account.** A post with none says **Open the post to add accounts** in its Publish cell.
3. ✅ **The plan's time zone is right** for the brand. See [Setting the Plan's Time Zone](#setting-the-plans-time-zone).

## When a Post Goes Out

A scheduled post goes out on its **Date**, at a time picked for each account:

1. A time set for this post on that account, if you set one.
2. Otherwise, the account's posting slot for that weekday. Each account starts with 12:00 Monday to Thursday, 18:30 on Friday and 10:00 at the weekend.
3. Otherwise, the default time.

The check shows each account's time and where it comes from: **Set for this post**, the account's weekday slot (for example **Its Thursday slot**), or **Default time**. Click a time to change it for that account only. **Use its usual time** puts it back.

> **The date moves the whole post. A time changes only its account.** So one post can go out on Instagram at noon and on Facebook in the evening of the same day.

## Setting the Plan's Time Zone

Every time in the plan is read in one time zone. A new plan uses Jakarta (WIB).

1. Switch the plan to **Table**.
2. Click the **Publish** column's name (or choose **Settings** from its column menu).
3. Under **Time zone**, pick the brand's zone. Indonesia's three zones come first, followed by others across the region and the world.
4. Save.

Each post is scheduled for its date and posting time in this zone. Posts already scheduled keep their time. In a zone other than Jakarta, times show their offset, for example `GMT+8`.

## Step 1 — Open the Check

Open the post and press the button at its top right:

* **Schedule** with its time, when the post's date is still ahead
* **Publish now**, when it has no date or its date has passed

In the **Table**, the post's **Publish** cell has the same button. Either way, the post's **Publish** tab opens on the check.

## Step 2 — Read the Check

The check shows everything that's about to happen:

* **When** — the post's date. Change it here to move the whole post.
* **Where** — each account, with its time and where that time comes from
* **What goes out** — the creative and caption as they'll be sent
* **Approved by** — who approved it

Below that, it lists anything to know before sending:

* **Warnings** you can go ahead past, such as a picture on a Reel (it goes out as a photo post) or a post with no image (it goes out as words only).
* **Blockers** that stop the post, such as an account that has no caption or no creative, or a caption over that platform's limit.

When some accounts can't take the post and others can, the post goes to the others only. A button such as **Send to the others only** takes the stopped accounts off so the rest go as approved, and keeps the approval.

## Step 3 — Schedule or Publish Now

* **Schedule for** (followed by the time) puts the post on its accounts' schedules. Its status becomes **Scheduled**. You can change or unschedule it until then.
* **Publish now** sends it straight away, on all its accounts.

### A post dated today whose time has passed

If the post is dated today but its time is already gone, the check says so and suggests a time later today, for example *Today's 12:00 has passed, so we suggest 14:00*. Press **Schedule · today** with that time, or pick another time first. If nothing is left today, pick another day or publish it now.

### Scheduling several posts at once

1. Select posts in the plan.
2. In the selection bar, press **Schedule** or **Publish now**.
3. A check lists the posts that will go, with their times, and the ones that **Won't go** and why (not approved yet, no account yet, no date yet, its date has passed).
4. Confirm.

Only approved posts with accounts go out; **Schedule** also needs a date ahead.

## Step 4 — Follow Each Account's Result

After a send, the post's **Publish** cell shows each account's logo with a dot for how it went, and a line summing it up, such as **Scheduled** with the time, **Live**, **Failed**, or a count like *1 live · 2 scheduled*. Hover a logo for that account's result. A live account's logo links to its post.

While a post is on its way:

| You'll see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Sending…** | The send is in progress |
| **Waiting for the live link** | Sent to go out now. The link usually arrives within a few minutes. Don't send it again |
| **Should have gone out** | Its time has passed and frndOS is checking with its accounts |
| **We didn't hear back** | The platform didn't answer, so it may or may not have gone out. frndOS keeps looking, and nothing posts again unless someone presses |

### frndOS confirms scheduled posts went live

You don't have to check each account yourself. Once a scheduled post's time passes, frndOS confirms it with the platform:

* **Live** — the account turns live with its link, and the post becomes **Published** once every account is live.
* **Gone or cancelled** — if the post was deleted or cancelled before it went out, the account is marked **Failed** with the reason.
* **Still not out a day later** — the account is marked **Failed** so someone looks. If it goes out after all within the next week, frndOS still turns it live, so nobody posts it twice.

## Retrying What Failed

When an account fails, the post's button reads **Try again** (in the Table, **Retry failed**). The check shows why each account failed. Fix what it says, then send again: only the accounts that failed go, and accounts that already went out don't go twice.

If frndOS couldn't confirm whether a post went out, the check asks you to look at the account first. If the post is there, use **Mark as published** instead. See [Marking Posts Published & Results](./marking-published-and-results).

## Changing a Scheduled Post

Open the post. Its **Publish** tab shows **Scheduled**, with:

* **Move to another day** — change the date. Moving the day keeps its approval.
* **Unschedule** — takes it off the schedule and back to **Approved**.
* **Publish now** — sends it now instead.

> **Heads-up:** changing the image, video or caption takes the post off the schedule. It needs approval again before it goes back on.

## Approved but Not Scheduled

An approved post that nobody scheduled won't go out on its own. If it's planned for today or tomorrow, its check warns *it isn't scheduled, so it won't go out*, and its owner (or whoever approved it) is told once for that day.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I schedule a post that's still in review?**
No. Only approved posts go out. A post waiting for approval shows a disabled **Publish** that says why when you hover it.

**Q: An account lost its connection. What happens to its posts?**
The plan shows a warning with **Reconnect**. Posts scheduled to that account won't go out until it's back.

**Q: Some accounts say they can't post from frndOS yet. What do I do?**
Post it on that platform yourself, then use **Mark as published** with the live link. See [Marking Posts Published & Results](./marking-published-and-results).
