# Publish Overview

**Publish** is where a brand's social posts go from idea to live. Every brand has one **plan**: each row is a post, with its date, the accounts it goes to, its creative and caption. Posts move through approval, get scheduled or published to the brand's accounts, and once they're out, their results come back into the same plan.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **You have access to Publish for the brand.** Brand roles have two Publish rows: **Publish** (the plan) and **Publish settings** (the approval rules). The **Manager** role has both, and **Viewer** can look. For other roles, an admin sets these rows in the brand role editor. See [Inviting Team Members](../workspace/inviting-team-members).
2. ✅ **The brand has accounts connected** if you want frndOS to post for you. You can plan without any. See [Connecting Your Accounts](#connecting-your-accounts).

## Opening Publish

1. Open the brand you're working on (switch brands from the page title).
2. In the sidebar, under **Modules**, click **Publish**.

The page title reads the brand's name, then **Publish**. If Publish isn't in your sidebar, your role probably doesn't include it for this brand.

The first time someone who can edit Publish opens the page, frndOS creates the brand's plan. Until then, the page says the brand has no Publish plan yet.

## The Plan

The plan is a table of posts. Its main fields:

* **Post** — the post's working title
* **Date** — the day it's planned for
* **Channel** — the accounts it goes to (a post can go to up to 10)
* **Format** — how it goes out: Feed post, Carousel, Reel, Story, Video, Short or Text only
* **Owner** — who the post belongs to
* **Design by** — the designer, when the creative is handed off
* **Creative** — the image, video or carousel files
* **Caption** — the words that go out with it
* **Publish** — where the post stands with its accounts, and the button that sends it
* **Pillar / Objective** — the content pillar, and what the post is for
* **Live link** — where it went live
* **Reach, Views, Eng. rate, Comments** — results, filled in by frndOS after the post goes out

**Pillar** offers the brand's own Content Pillar labels, the same ones Insights uses to label posts. **Objective** offers the brand's objective labels, or Awareness, Engagement, Traffic, Conversion and Community when the brand has none. Results can't be typed in; see [Marking Posts Published & Results](./marking-published-and-results).

## Post Statuses

Every post has a status:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Drafting** | Being written. Every new post starts here |
| **Needs review** | Sent for approval and waiting for someone to approve it |
| **Approved** | Approved and ready to schedule or publish |
| **Scheduled** | Sent to its accounts to go out at a set time |
| **Published** | Live |
| **Rejected** | Turned down. It goes back to Drafting before it can be approved again |

You can't set **Scheduled** or **Published** by hand. A post becomes Scheduled when you schedule it to its accounts, and Published when frndOS confirms it's live or you mark it published with its link.

## Views

The tabs at the top of the plan show the same posts in different ways:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Table** | Every post as a row, with all its fields |
| **Board** | Posts as cards in lanes. Publish opens here |
| **Gallery** | Posts as cards with their creative |
| **Calendar** | Posts on their dates |
| **Approvals** | Posts waiting for approval. The tab shows a count when some are waiting on you |

The views menu (it reads **Default view** until you pick another) also holds ready-made views every plan starts with:

* **Pipeline** — a Board of everything not yet published
* **Calendar** — every post on its date, leaving out rejected ones
* **Social** — a Table of social posts still in the works
* **Articles** — a Table of posts whose Channel is Blog
* **Published** — what went out, best reach first, with its results

> **Heads-up:** a view you change is yours until you press **Save view**, which saves it for everyone who opens the plan.

## Opening a Post

Click a post to open it. It opens beside the plan; **Open full screen** gives it the whole page. Two of its tabs matter most for getting a post out:

* **Publish** — the next step for this post, and the check before it goes out
* **Checks** — what's still missing, such as a creative, a caption, a date or an owner

The other tabs hold the post itself (its fields, accounts, captions and creative), the team's comments and its activity.

The button at the top right of the post always says the next step: **Send for approval**, **Approve**, **Schedule** (with its time), **Publish now**, **Try again**, or **View post** once it's live.

## Connecting Your Accounts

frndOS posts to the brand's connected social accounts. When none are connected, the plan says **No channels connected yet** with a link to **Connect Instagram, TikTok or Facebook**, which opens the brand's account connections in Insights. Planning works without any accounts; you just can't send posts until one is connected.

See [Connecting Owned Media](../insights/connecting-owned-media) for the sign-in steps.

If a connected account loses its connection, the plan shows a warning with **Reconnect**. Posts scheduled to that account won't go out until it's back.

## Starting an Empty Plan

A brand with nothing planned shows **Nothing planned yet** with two ways to start:

* **Plan the week with FRND** — frnd drafts a week of posts from the brand's priorities, pillars and the dates coming up that matter to it. They arrive as drafts and stay with you until you send them for approval.
* **New post** — adds an empty post and opens it.

## FAQ

**Q: Is there one plan per brand, or can I make several?**
One per brand. Every post for the brand lives in its Publish plan; use views and filters to focus on part of it.

**Q: Why can't I see Publish in the sidebar?**
Your brand role doesn't include Publish for this brand. Ask a workspace admin to set the **Publish** row for your role.

**Q: Can I move a post to Published myself?**
Only with its live link. Use **Mark as published** and frndOS sets the status. See [Marking Posts Published & Results](./marking-published-and-results).

**Q: Does Publish post to blogs?**
Not yet. Publish posts to the brand's connected social accounts.
