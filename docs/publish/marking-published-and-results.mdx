# Marking Posts Published & Results

**A post counts as published once frndOS knows where it went live.** Posts frndOS sends are marked automatically. For a post that went out another way, you give frndOS the link. From then on, its results fill in by themselves.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **The post is Approved or Scheduled.** Only an approved post can be marked published.
2. ✅ **You can edit the brand's Publish plan.**

## When to Mark a Post Published

* The post was posted by hand on the platform.
* Its account **can't post from frndOS yet**. Such a post's button reads **Mark as published**, and its check says **This one goes out by hand**.
* frndOS couldn't confirm a send, and you found the post live on the account.

## Step 1 — Open Mark as Published

Use any of these:

* **In the post:** on the **Publish** tab, press **Mark as published** (under the send buttons). When the post's next step is **Publish now**, it's also in the menu beside that button.
* **In the plan:** select one post and press **Mark as published…** in the selection bar.
* **On the Board:** move the post's card to **Published**.

Posts are marked one at a time, because each one needs its own link.

## Step 2 — Pick the Post or Paste Its Link

The **Where did it go live?** dialog opens.

* **Posts around its date** lists the brand's own posts from Insights, on the platforms the post's accounts are on, from a few days before its date to a week after. Click the one it went live as, and its link fills in.
* Or paste the link into **Live link**. You can leave off `https://`.

Press **Mark as published**. The post becomes **Published**, and its live link is saved in the **Live link** column.

> **Heads-up:** if the post was still scheduled, frndOS cancels what's scheduled first, so it can't post twice.

If the list is empty, Insights may not have seen the post yet (it can take a few hours) or can't be read right now. Paste the link instead.

## Correcting a Post Marked by Hand

Open the post. Its **Publish** tab says **Marked as published by hand**, with:

* **Change the link** — save the right link.
* **It didn't go out** — **Back to Approved** clears its live link and numbers. Nothing is scheduled for it now, so schedule it again if it still should go out.

A post frndOS sent itself can't be corrected this way. It stays as it went out.

## Results

Once a post is published, frndOS fills its result columns from Insights:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Reach** | How many people saw it |
| **Views** | Plays, for posts with a video (a picture's Views cell stays empty) |
| **Eng. rate** | Its engagement rate |
| **Comments** | How many comments it got |

* **Numbers show up a few hours after it goes out.** Until Insights has the post, frndOS uses the platform's own numbers for posts it sent, then switches to Insights' so the plan matches the Insights page.
* **A post on several accounts** shows its total: reach and comments added up, engagement rate weighted by reach. Hover an account's logo in the **Publish** cell to see that account's own numbers.
* **Stories** are read every hour while they're up. Once a Story ends after 24 hours, its last numbers stay.
* **Results can't be edited.** frndOS writes them; they're what happened after the post went out.

### Finding your best posts

* **Published · best first** (above the plan) shows only published posts, highest reach first, in the Table.
* The **Published** view does the same, with the results fields.

## The Live Post

Open a live post to see, on its **Publish** tab:

* where it went, with **View** for each account
* **How it's doing**, with its numbers
* its **Latest comments**

Live posts stay as they went out. Where the platform allows it (Facebook today), a live post's menu offers **Change the words** or **Take it down**. Instagram, TikTok and YouTube posts are changed on the platform itself.

## FAQ

**Q: Why are a post's result cells empty?**
Insights reads a post a few hours after it goes out. A picture never has a **Views** number.

**Q: I marked the wrong link. Can I fix it?**
Yes. Open the post and use **Change the link** on its **Publish** tab.

**Q: Can I mark several posts published at once?**
No. Each post needs the link where it went live, so mark them one at a time.

**Q: The post went live but frndOS shows it as failed. What now?**
If it turns out live within a week of failing for being late, frndOS turns it live on its own. Otherwise, use **Mark as published** with its link.
