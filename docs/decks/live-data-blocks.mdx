# Live Data on Slides

A slide can carry a block that stays connected to where its numbers came from. Instead of pasting a screenshot that is wrong a week later, you link the real source and refresh it when you need to.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **A deck open in the editor** — see [Decks Overview](./overview)
2. ✅ **Something to link to** — connected Insights data, an Audience segment, or a Studio file

## What You Can Put on a Slide

| Linked to | What lands |
|---|---|
| **Chart** | An Insights chart |
| **Block** | A named performance block, listed below |
| **Segment** / **Persona** | Audience data |
| **KV** / **Studio video** | A file from Studio |
| **Artifact** | Campaign work such as **4C**, **Big idea**, **GWTB**, **Campaign plan**, **KPI** or **Verbatims** |

The performance blocks available:

**Latest numbers** · **Top posts** · **Worst posts** · **Spend & pacing** · **Trend** · **Channel mix** · **Campaign scorecard** · **Voice of customer** · **Upcoming posts** · **Campaign timeline**

## The Live Chip

A linked block carries a chip reading its state, what it's linked to, and the period it covers, for example `● Live · Top posts · All time`.

| Chip state | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Live** | Linked and current |
| **Updating…** | Fetching from the source |
| **Source updated · Refresh** | The source moved on; your slide still shows the older pull |
| **Source removed** | What it pointed at is gone |

> **The chip turns amber when it's stale.** That's the signal the slide is showing numbers the source no longer agrees with.

## Changing the Window

A block covers a period, and you can change it without rebuilding the slide. The options are **Auto**, **7d**, **30d**, **90d**, **This month**, **This year**, **All time** and **Campaign**.

For a block that reads brand data, you can also switch which brand it reads.

## Refreshing, Opening and Detaching

Select the block and the right rail gives you:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Refresh** | Pulls current values from the source |
| **Open in Insights** / **Open in Studio** | Opens the source itself in a new tab |
| **Detach** | _"Keep the elements, drop the link"_ |

**Detach** is how you freeze a slide. The numbers stay exactly as they are and stop tracking the source, which is what you want for a deck that has been signed off and shouldn't move again.

### Your edits survive a refresh

If you've hand-edited values in a linked block, refreshing does not silently overwrite them. The button tells you how many it's keeping, reading **Refresh · N edited kept**, and a tooltip spells out that the edited slots are kept as they are.

> **Heads-up:** that's also the thing to check if a refresh "didn't work". An edited slot is deliberately left alone — use **Reset to source** on that field to hand it back to the source.

## Controlling How It Looks

A linked block isn't locked to one presentation. The right rail carries **Layout**, **Show** (which parts appear), **Title** and **Fit** (**Fill** or **Fit** for images). Turning the title off or rewording it doesn't break the link.

## FAQ

**Q: My slide's numbers are out of date.**
Look at the chip. **Source updated · Refresh** means the source has newer data. Click **Refresh**.

**Q: I want the numbers to stop changing.**
**Detach** the block. The values stay on the slide and no longer follow the source.

**Q: I refreshed and my manual edits are still there.**
That's intended. The button reports how many edited slots it kept. Use **Reset to source** on a field to give it back to the source.

**Q: The chip says Source removed.**
What the block pointed at has been deleted. The slide keeps what it last had; relink it or detach it.

**Q: Can I change the date range without rebuilding the slide?**
Yes. Pick a different period on the block, from **Auto** through to **All time**.
