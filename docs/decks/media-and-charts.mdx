# Video, Embeds and Charts

A slide takes more than text and images. You can paste a link and have it play in place, and you can draw a chart from your own numbers.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **A deck open in the editor** — see [Decks Overview](./overview)
2. ✅ **The link or data you want to show**

## Pasting a Link

Paste a URL and frndOS decides what to put on the slide. Four providers play inline:

**YouTube** · **Vimeo** · **Loom** · **Figma**

The editor states the rule plainly: _"YouTube · Vimeo · Loom · Figma play. Any other page becomes a link card."_

| What you paste | What lands |
|---|---|
| One of the four above | A player that runs in the editor and in **Play** |
| Any other page | A **link card** with the page's title and preview image |

> **Heads-up:** on export a player becomes a poster image that opens the link. A slide deck file can't hold a running video, so the exported version hands the viewer the link instead.

### Switching between a player and a card

A media element's menu can change your mind for you: **Convert to link card** turns a player into a card, and **Convert to player** turns it back.

Converting back only works for a link that can actually play. If it can't, the option explains why: _"Only YouTube, Vimeo, Loom and Figma links play."_

### Video controls

An embed's menu also carries:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Play** | Starts it in the editor. **Esc** stops it |
| **Autoplay in Play** | Starts it by itself when you reach the slide while presenting |
| **Replace link…** | Points it at a different URL |
| **Use poster as image** | Turns the preview frame into a plain image |
| **Lock position** | Stops it being moved by accident |

For an uploaded video there's also **Set poster from this frame**, described as: _"Play the video, pause on the frame you want, then set it."_ And **Download original**.

## Charts

**Chart** in the bottom dock draws from numbers you supply. The full set of kinds:

| Family | Kinds |
|---|---|
| **Columns and bars** | Column, Stacked column, 100% column, Bar, Stacked bar, 100% bar |
| **Lines and areas** | Line, Area, Stacked area, 100% area, Combo |
| **Parts of a whole** | Pie, Donut, **Funnel** |
| **Points** | Scatter, **Bubble** |
| **Single figures** | KPI, Gauge, Bar list |
| **Other** | Radar, Timeline |

### Funnel

A **funnel** shows a sequence narrowing at each stage, which is the natural shape for awareness through to conversion.

### Bubble — a positioning map

A **bubble** chart plots each thing as a point rather than a bar, which makes it the one to reach for when you're mapping competitors or positioning rather than comparing totals.

Each point carries:

* **x** and **y** — where it sits on the two axes you're comparing
* **size** — scales the bubble, for a third number such as spend or reach
* **label** — names the point
* **group** — buckets points so related ones share a colour

You can also label the quadrants, so the four corners of the map say what they mean instead of leaving the reader to infer it.

> **Colour follows the group, not the point.** Points in the same group share a colour, which is what makes a crowded map readable.

## FAQ

**Q: I pasted a link and got a card instead of a video.**
Only YouTube, Vimeo, Loom and Figma play. Everything else becomes a link card. Use **Convert to player** if it is one of the four and landed as a card.

**Q: What happens to my videos when I export?**
A player exports as a poster image that opens the link. Nothing is lost, but it doesn't play inside the exported file.

**Q: Can a video start on its own while presenting?**
Yes. Turn on **Autoplay in Play** on that element.

**Q: Which chart should I use for a competitor map?**
**Bubble**. It plots each competitor as a point on two axes, sizes it by a third number, and lets you label the quadrants.

**Q: How do I stop a video playing in the editor?**
Press **Esc**.
