# Decks Overview — Building Presentations in frndOS

A **deck** in frndOS is a presentation you build inside the app: slides on a canvas, a Story view for writing the narrative, an AI assistant beside you, and export to PowerPoint or PDF when you're done.

Decks live in your Library alongside Key Visuals and reports, and can be filed into a project so the rest of your team finds them in context.

> **Beta feature:** the deck editor is gated by the `decking-phase-a` flag. If deck creation isn't available in your workspace yet, it's still rolling out.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **A workspace with the deck editor enabled** — see the note above
2. ✅ **A brand or project to file the deck under** (optional) — a deck with neither is "unfiled" and still works

## Where Decks Come From

There are four entry points, and they all land in the same editor:

| Entry point | Where | What it makes |
|---|---|---|
| **Create menu → Presentation Deck** | The **+** button in the sidebar footer | Opens the **New deck** chooser |
| **Library → New file → Deck** | [Library](../library/overview) | Opens the same **New deck** chooser |
| **New file** | Inside a [project room](../projects/project-rooms) | A deck titled **Untitled deck**, filed into that project |
| **Handoff → Quick deck** | From project work | A deck scaffolded from existing work |

### The New deck chooser

Both the sidebar Create menu and the Library open a chooser titled **New deck**, described as _"Start blank, bring in a PowerPoint or PDF, or pick a published template."_

| Tile | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Start blank** | Creates an empty deck called **Untitled deck** and opens it |
| **Import a file** | Turns a `.pptx` or `.pdf` into a new deck — see [Importing a PowerPoint or PDF](./importing-a-deck) |
| **A published template** | Creates a deck pre-loaded with that template's slides |

## The Editor at a Glance

<!-- TODO: add screenshot of the deck editor — top bar, slide rail, canvas, right rail, bottom dock → /img/guides-asset/<name>.jpeg -->

| Area | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Top bar** | Deck menu, the parent link and deck title, search, who else is here, **Export**, **Share**, **Play** |
| **Slide rail** (left) | Slide thumbnails, and the **Slides** ⇄ **Story** switch |
| **Canvas** | The slide you're editing |
| **Right rail** | Three tabs — **Slide**, **Layers**, **Notes** |
| **Bottom dock** | Insert tools — **Media**, **Shape**, **Chart**, **Table**, **Embed** |

The line under the deck title tells you its state: **Private** (or **Template**), the slide count, and either **saved** or **approved**.

> **Heads-up:** **Chart** is marked Phase A and **Embed** is marked Phase B — Phase B controls are visible but not usable yet.

## Two Views: Slides and Story

The deck rail carries a **Slides** / **Story** switch, and ⌘/ flips between them.

| View | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Slides** | Laying out one slide — placing text, media, shapes, charts, and tables on the canvas |
| **Story** | Writing the narrative as prose, in chapters, before or instead of arranging boxes |

Story carries its own actions: **Read back**, **Listen**, **Rehearse**, **Present**, and **Structure**. Your current slide carries over when you switch, so you land on the same part of the deck in either view.

See [Writing the Story](./story-view) for how the narrative side works.

## Commenting on a Slide

Feedback goes **on the slide**, pinned to the exact element you mean.

<!-- TODO: add screenshot of the deck editor in comment mode — the top-bar toggle, a pin on a slide element, the Comments dock → /img/guides-asset/<name>.jpeg -->

Click the **💬 speech bubble** in the top bar, or press **C**. Then click what you want to talk about — a pin lands there and a composer opens beside it. For a note about the deck as a whole, use the composer at the bottom of the dock: _"Comment on this deck…"_.

On an empty deck the dock says: _"Press C and click the slide to leave one."_

**Keys:** **C** arms comment mode, **V** leaves it, and **Escape** steps out one level per press — composer, then open thread, then the mode.

### Finding feedback across slides

The deck shows only one slide at a time, so the dock is how you see the rest. **Slide thumbnails in the left rail carry a count** of open threads on that slide, so you can tell where feedback is waiting without clicking through. Select a thread in the dock and the editor **takes you to its slide**.

A thread whose slide or element has been deleted is marked **No longer in the deck** — _"The slide or element this thread points at has been deleted."_ It stays readable rather than disappearing with the object.

### In the dock

Search threads, filter by **All / Open / Done / @me** (plus author and "has image"), hide the pins with the 👁 eye, and per comment: **@mention**, **reply**, **react** (👍 ❤️ 🎉 😄 😢 👀), **attach an image**, **edit** or **delete** your own, and **resolve** or reopen.

> **Who you can mention:** the suggestion list for a deck is resolved from the deck's own audience — including the room it sits in — not from a brand's member list.

> **Rolling out:** deck commenting ships behind its own switch. Until it's on for your workspace, the deck has **no commenting at all** — the old per-slide Comments tab in the right rail was replaced by this, not kept alongside it.

**Desktop only.** Comment mode is aim-and-click and isn't offered on the phone layout.

## Renaming, Approving, and Version History

The **Deck menu** (top-left) holds the deck-wide actions:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Rename** | Renames the deck (you can also click the title in the top bar and type) |
| **Save as template** | Turns the current deck into a reusable template |
| **Guides & grid** (⌥G) | Shows or hides alignment guides |
| **Version history** | Opens the deck's history panel |
| **Approve deck** / **Reopen deck for edits** | Marks the whole deck approved, or reopens it |
| **Import…** | Brings a PowerPoint or PDF into this deck |
| **Import report** | Shows what happened on the last import |
| **Export…** (⇧⌘E) | Opens the export options |

When a deck is approved, the meta line under the title reads **approved** in green.

## Sharing and Exporting

**Share** and **Export** in the top bar open the same dialog, **Share & export deck**, on different tabs.

**Share** offers a **View-only preview link** — click **Enable link**, then **Copy**. **Disable** turns the link off again.

**Export** offers:

| Format | Notes |
|---|---|
| **PowerPoint (.pptx)** | _"Editable deck. Some elements may render differently."_ |
| **PDF** | _"1920×1080 print-ready pages."_ |
| **Google Slides** | Marked **Soon** — not available yet |
| **Canva** | Marked **Soon** — not available yet |

> **Heads-up:** **Comment access** and **Edit access** appear in the Share tab labelled **Phase B** — guest comments and per-link roles aren't live yet.

## Presenting

Click **Play** in the top bar to open the deck full-screen. From Story view, **Present** does the same.

## FAQ

**Q: Where do my decks show up afterwards?**
In the [Library](../library/overview), with **Deck** as the file kind. A deck created inside a project also appears in that project's **Files** section.

**Q: Can someone without a frndOS account see my deck?**
Yes — enable the **View-only preview link** in the Share tab and send that URL. It's view-only.

**Q: Can I use my brand's colours and fonts?**
Yes. Build a brand template once and every deck started from it is on brand — see [Brand Deck Templates](../brand-setup/deck-templates). Pick it in the **New deck** chooser rather than starting blank.

**Q: There's no speech bubble in the top bar and no Comments tab either.**
Deck commenting is rolled out per workspace. Until it's switched on, the deck has no commenting surface — the old right-rail Comments tab was replaced by the pinned version, not kept next to it.

**Q: How do I find a comment on another slide?**
Open the dock and select the thread — the editor jumps to its slide. Slide thumbnails also show a count of open threads.

**Q: What does "approved" mean?**
It's a deck-wide state you set from the Deck menu. It marks the deck as final; **Reopen deck for edits** undoes it.

**Q: Why can't I export to Google Slides or Canva?**
Both are listed with a **Soon** badge and are disabled. PowerPoint and PDF work today.
