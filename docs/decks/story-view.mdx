# Writing the Story

Most decks start as a story, not a layout. **Story view** is one calm page where you write that story: a card for every slide, grouped into chapters, with the slide beside the words you'll say over it. You can write first and build the slides later, or write the talk track for slides you already have.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **A deck open in the editor**, see [Decks Overview](./overview)
2. ✅ **Something to say.** Story works from the words you write, and Ask FRND can draft the rest

## Opening Story View

The editor's dock carries a **Slides** / **Story** switch. Click **Story**, or press **⌘/** to flip between the two.

Your current slide carries over, so you land on the same part of the deck either way.

## The Page

Story is one page for the whole deck:

* **Chapters** are headings across the page, each with how many slides it holds and how long it takes to say
* **Cards** sit under each chapter, one per slide. The slide is on the left and its talk track is on the right
* **The slim bar** at the top shows **Chapter B of 7** (it's a menu: pick any chapter to jump to it), how much is written, and **Find a line…**

Clicking a card, or scrolling to it, makes it the current slide. The rest of the editor follows, so switching back to **Slides** lands on that slide.

While you type, the bar fades back so the words have the page. It returns after a pause, or when you point at it.

On wide windows, a thin chapter index in the margin jumps between chapters.

## Writing a Talk Track

Each card asks _"What do you say on this slide?"_ Type the words you'll actually say. The card shows how long they take to read out.

Cards you haven't written yet are easy to find: **⌘↓** jumps to the next card without words, and **⌘↑** to the previous one.

### Adding, Moving and Removing Cards

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Add a card below the one you're in | **⌘↵**, or **Add card below** in the card's **•••** menu |
| Add a card between two others | Point at the gap between them and click **+ Add a card** |
| Start a new chapter after this one | **⌘⇧↵** |
| Move a card (across chapters too) | **⌥⇧↑** / **⌥⇧↓**, or **Move up** / **Move down** in the **•••** menu |
| Remove a card | **Remove card** in the **•••** menu, or **⌫** in an empty card |

Removing a card that has words asks first: _"Remove this card and its words?"_ Once it's gone, the toast offers **Undo**.

Click **Shortcuts** in the bar to see every writing shortcut.

## Ask FRND Drafts Your Talk Tracks

When some slides still have no words, the bar shows a pill such as **3 slides still need words**. Click it to open the talk-track panel. Ask FRND drafts what you'd say on each one.

Choose how the drafts should sound before you start:

| Option | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Tone** | **Presenter**, **Conversational**, **Formal** |
| **Length per slide** | **~20s**, **~40s**, **~60s** |
| **Language** | **Bahasa Indonesia**, **English**. The deck's own language is marked **Matches the deck** |

Click **Write N talk tracks**. Slides you've already written stay as they are; only empty ones are drafted.

A draft waits on its card, marked **Suggested by Ask FRND**, with four choices:

* **Keep** makes it your talk track
* **Edit** keeps it and lets you change it
* **Try another** writes a different version in its place
* **Dismiss** throws it away

To take every waiting draft at once, click **Keep all N** in the panel. **Redraft…** writes the waiting drafts again with the options you've just changed.

You can also draft one card at a time with **Write it with Ask FRND** on an empty card.

## Building a Slide for a Card

A card without a slide shows **No slide yet** where the slide would be.

1. Write at least a line on the card. Until then, the slot reads **Write a line first**
2. Click **Suggest a slide** in the slot. Ask FRND proposes a headline and a few layouts, drawn in the deck's look
3. Point at the suggestion to see it in full. Pick a layout, or click **Other layouts** for more
4. Click **Build this slide**, or **Not now** to put the suggestion away

A suggestion only uses figures that the card's own words state. If your talk track doesn't give a number, the slide won't invent one.

### When the Story Moves On

Edit a card's words after its slide was built, and the card shows **Story changed · Update slide**. The slide never rebuilds by itself. Click **Update slide** to rebuild it from your words.

If the slide was also changed by hand since it was built, frndOS asks first: _"This slide was changed by hand since it was built. Updating it from the story replaces those changes."_ Choose **Update anyway** or **Keep the slide**.

### Building Every Missing Slide

When several cards have no slide, the bar says how many (for example **4 cards have no slide**) and offers **Build 4 slides**. On a deck with no slides yet, it offers **Build the deck**.

If part of a build fails, an amber dot appears on the chapter menu. Open it to see which chapter wasn't built and click **Try again**.

## Peeking at a Slide

Hover a card's slide to see a larger preview beside it. Click the slide to open it full size, then use **←** / **→** to step through the story. **Open in Slides** takes you to that slide in the editor.

## Focus Mode

Click **Focus** in the dock, or press **⌘.**, to strip the page back to the writing. Everything except the card you're on fades back, and a slim bar keeps your place. Press **Esc** or **⌘.** again to leave. Focus mode is remembered for each deck.

## The Story Actions

Story view replaces the dock's insert tools with its own row:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Build** | Proposes slides from your story (see below) |
| **Read back** | Shows where your words actually go, and what to fix |
| **Listen** | Reads the story aloud |
| **Rehearse** | Times you as you talk through it |
| **Focus** | Turns focus mode on or off (**⌘.**) |
| **Present** | Opens the deck full-screen |
| **Notes** | Shows or hides notes (**⌥N**) |
| **Structure** | Shows or hides the structure outline |

### Build

Click **Build** and frndOS proposes what to create. A modal opens headed **Build N slides from your story**, with the rule stated plainly:

> _"Nothing is created until you approve. Existing slides are never replaced — new ones land after their chapter's last slide."_

Proposals are grouped by chapter. Untick anything you don't want, then click **Approve & build N slides**, or **Keep writing** to go back.

## Editing While You Write

Select any passage and a small action row appears:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Tighten** | Rewrites the selection more concisely |
| **Say it out loud** | Rewrites it the way you'd actually say it |
| **Ask about this** | Opens a question box. The answer lands in the margin, not in your text |
| **Make it the heading** | Promotes the selection to the card's heading |
| **Split into two slides** | Breaks the card in two |

**Tighten** and **Say it out loud** offer their version next to yours, so you can keep your own wording.

## Read Back and Rehearse

**Read back** shows a bar chart of where your words go, chapter by chapter, followed by at most three findings. Each finding names a chapter and offers a jump straight to it.

**Rehearse** turns the story into a teleprompter with a clock. You advance when you've finished saying a slide, and that is the measurement. When you finish, **How that run went** shows your total time, your drift against the plan, your pace in words per minute, and **What to fix first**. Then **Run it again** or **Back to writing**.

> **Note:** Rehearse never asks for your microphone. It measures by when you advance, not by listening.

A completed run sets your real speaking pace, and every time estimate in the story updates to use it.

When every slide has words, the page says **Every slide has something to say** and offers to rehearse.

## Talk Tracks in Slides View

In **Slides** view, the right rail's **Notes** tab shows the slide's **Talk track**, read-only. Click **Edit in Story ⌘/** (or **Write it in Story ⌘/** when it's empty) to write it in Story. Talk tracks are private to editors; the audience never sees them in preview or a share link.

## FAQ

**Q: When I typed fast, some letters went missing. Is that fixed?**
Yes. Every key now lands, however fast you type in a card.

**Q: Opening a deck once wiped its story. Is that fixed?**
Yes. Your story stays when you open the deck, including a deck that only has its blank first slide. On an empty deck, Story waits until your saved story has loaded before it adds a first card.

**Q: Will Ask FRND overwrite the talk tracks I wrote?**
No. Drafting only fills slides that have no words, and a draft waits for you to **Keep** it.

**Q: Will Build or Build this slide overwrite my existing slides?**
No. New slides land after their chapter's last slide. A built slide only changes when you click **Update slide**, and frndOS asks first if you changed it by hand.

**Q: Why does the slot say "Write a line first"?**
A slide is suggested from the card's words. Write at least one line, then click **Suggest a slide**.

**Q: Where did that number on the suggested slide come from?**
From your talk track. Suggestions only use figures the card states.

**Q: I removed a card by mistake.**
Click **Undo** on the toast that appears after removing it.

**Q: Does Rehearse record me?**
No. It requests no microphone access. It times you by when you advance to the next slide.
