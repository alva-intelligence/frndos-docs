# AskFrnd Overview

**AskFrnd** is your AI assistant in frndOS. It's not a separate tool — it's a persistent panel you can open anywhere in the platform to ask questions, generate visuals, or get analysis.

If you used the old standalone chat page in v1, AskFrnd is what replaces it — and it's smarter, more accessible, and aware of where you are.

## Where to Find AskFrnd — and What History You'll See

Open AskFrnd with the **Ask FRND** button in the bottom-right corner of any page, with **New chat** in the sidebar (**⌘O**), or by clicking a chat under **Chats** in the sidebar.

### Floating, Docked or Full Screen

The panel opens in one of three ways, and you can move between them from its **More options** menu:

| Mode | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Float** | A card floating over the page |
| **Dock to side** | A panel beside the page. Drag its edge to make it wider or narrower |
| **Fullscreen** | The whole window. **Exit fullscreen** goes back to the way you had it |

AskFrnd remembers whether you prefer it floating or docked, and opens that way next time. The panel's header also has **New chat** and **Chat history**. The **More options** menu adds **Generated files** (once the chat has made something) and **Chat settings**.

### Chat History

**Chat history** lists your past chats, with **Search chats...** and filters for the chat type and the module it came from.

* **Floating or docked** — a brand menu, starting on **All Brands**, so you can find a chat whichever brand it happened in
* **Full screen** — scoped to the brand you're on, or to chats with no brand when you're not on a brand page

The sidebar's **Chats** list shows your recent chats that aren't tied to a brand. **See all** opens the full history. See [Navigating frndOS — The Sidebar](../getting-started/navigating-frndos) for more on the sidebar layout.

## What Makes AskFrnd Different

### It Knows Where You Are

AskFrnd is **context-aware**. When you open the panel from inside the Insights dashboard, it knows you're looking at Insights data. When you open it from a creative canvas such as the KV Generator, it understands you're in a creative context.

This means:

* **From Insights** → AskFrnd can analyze your dashboard data ("what are my top 3 posts?", "why did this campaign outperform that one?")
* **From a creative canvas** → AskFrnd can help with creative ideation, image generation, or video generation
* **From Home** → AskFrnd works as a general-purpose assistant across your brand and projects

> **In short:** ask the same question from different places, get more relevant answers.

### Conversation History

AskFrnd **saves your chat history** — but what you see depends on your access point. See [Chat History](#chat-history) above for how the history is scoped.

### Model Choice

You can choose which **AI model** powers your conversation. Different models have different strengths — some are faster, some are better for visual generation, some are better for analysis. AskFrnd shows the available models in the panel; pick the one that fits your task.

> **Tip:** Credit cost can vary by model. See [Managing Workspace Credits](../workspace/managing-credits) for how credits work.

## What You Can Do with AskFrnd

AskFrnd is a general-purpose AI surface inside frndOS. Common uses include:

### Ask Questions About Your Data

*"What were my top 3 posts last month?"*
*"Why did the summer campaign perform better than the winter one?"*
*"Show me the engagement breakdown for owned media in Q1."*

### Generate Images

*"Create a banner for a back-to-school promo, vertical format, bright and energetic."*

See: [Generating Images & Videos via AskFrnd](./generating-with-chat)

### Generate Videos

*"Make a 5-second product reveal video, clean white background."*

### Brainstorm Ideas

*"Give me 10 social caption ideas for a Ramadan campaign for a youth banking brand."*

### Get Help with Workflow

*"How do I tag a post with multiple labels?"*
*"What's the best way to set up a new project for a client?"*

### Build a Workflow

*"Create a workflow that drafts a weekly report from our campaign data."*

AskFrnd builds it while you watch. See [Building a workflow in chat](../early-access/workflows#building-a-workflow-in-chat).

## Reading an Answer

While AskFrnd works, the answer shows _"Answering your questions…"_ and then the step it's on. When it's done, the top of the answer says how long it took, for example **Answered in 8s**. Click it to open the steps behind the answer, such as reading a page, retrieving Brand IQ or asking an agent.

Point at an answer for its actions:

* **Reply** quotes that answer in the composer, so your next message answers it specifically. Click the quote to jump back to the answer, or remove it to cancel
* **Branch from here** starts a new chat that continues from that answer, leaving the original chat as it was. The composer shows **Start new branch** until you send
* **Copy message**, and **Regenerate** on the latest answer

Your own messages show when you sent them, with **Edit message** and copy when you point at them.

### Suggested answers

Under the latest answer, AskFrnd offers a few suggested replies. Tap one to send it and keep going. In a new chat, cards such as **Summarise this page** or **What changed this week?** help you start.

## Rich Answers

AskFrnd's answers aren't only text. When it helps, an answer can include cards, charts, tables, key figures, file cards and sources, or buttons and choices you can click to carry on. Rich answers are on for everyone.

## AskFrnd vs. KV Generator

A common question: when should you use AskFrnd's image generation vs. opening the KV Generator?

| Use AskFrnd Chat When…                      | Use KV Generator When…                            |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| You want a quick concept or exploration     | You're building a finalized Key Visual            |
| You don't need fine canvas controls         | You need bounding box edits, layers, or templates |
| You're chatting and want to generate inline | You need version history and craft refinement     |
| You want flexibility in prompting           | You want repeatable, brand-aware production       |

Think of AskFrnd as the **sketchpad** and KV Generator as the **studio**. Both are valid; they're built for different moments.

## What AskFrnd Knows About Your Brand

AskFrnd has access to:

* Your **active brand** (whichever brand you currently have open — see [Navigating frndOS](../getting-started/navigating-frndos) for how brand context works)
* The **current page context** (which pillar, which data, which view)
* Your **conversation history**, scoped to your access point (see above)
* Any **brand data you @mention** — including brands other than the one you have open

### Pointing AskFrnd at Specific Brand Data

Type **@** in the composer and a picker appears. Its first section is **Brand data**.

You can mention:

| Mention | Scope |
|---|---|
| A **brand** by name | That brand's insight data as a whole |
| A brand's **subpage** — e.g. _Nike's Ads_ | Just that area |

Subpages available per brand: **Overview**, **Campaigns**, **Ads**, **Social Media**, **Influencer**, **ATL**, **Trend Signal**.

The brand you currently have open is listed first with its subpages expanded; other brands you have access to follow. Keep typing after the **@** to filter across all of them.

> **Heads-up:** you only ever see brands you actually have access to — the list is filtered on the server, and every mention is re-checked when the message is sent.

> **Note:** the same picker also lists **Agents**, **Teams**, and **Workflows** when those are enabled for your workspace. If nothing matches what you typed, it says _"No brands, agents, teams, or workflows found."_

> **Tip:** mentioning brand data depends on the **brand data** tool being on. If the **Brand data** section never appears, check **Chat Settings → Tools** — see [AskFrnd Tools](./tools).

> **Heads-up:** Brand IQ (Strategy, Identity, Tone) is currently **only fully consumed by the KV Generator**, not yet by AskFrnd's chat-based generation. This is being worked on. For now, if you need brand-perfect output, use the KV Generator or include brand specifics in your prompt.

## Attaching Files

Attach a file to a message and AskFrnd works from it.

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| **Max file size** | **20 MB** per file (raised from 10 MB) |
| **Files per message** | 5 |
| **Types** | JPG, PNG, WebP, PDF, DOC, DOCX, CSV, XLSX, TXT |

An unsupported type is refused with: _"File type tidak didukung. Gunakan JPG, PNG, WebP, PDF, DOC, DOCX, CSV, XLSX, atau TXT."_ Too large gets **File size too large. Maximum is 20MB.**

> **Video is different.** On the chat models that accept video, the cap is **200 MB** — video files are checked against that instead.

> **A message is required with an upload.** Sending a file with an empty composer says so: _"A message is required when uploading files."_

### When a file can't be read

If AskFrnd can't read an attachment, it tells you so in its reply and names the file. It no longer searches for the contents and answers as if your knowledge base were empty. Try a different copy of the file, or paste the part you need into the message.

PDF text also reads more cleanly now. A PDF with an odd character in its text used to fail as a whole; that character is now repaired, so the rest of the document comes through.

## Stopping a Response

Asked the wrong thing, or seen enough? Click the **stop** button — it replaces **Send** in the same spot while a reply is streaming.

What's already been written stays on screen. You don't lose the useful half of an answer just because you stopped the rest.

> **Note:** you can have more than one conversation working at a time. Starting a second thread doesn't discard the first one's answer.

## Queueing Follow-Ups While AskFrnd Is Answering

You don't have to wait for a reply to finish before typing the next thing. Type your follow-up and press Enter while a turn is still streaming, and instead of being dropped it's **parked as a chip** above the composer:

> _"Queued — sends when this turn finishes. Double-click to edit."_

When the current answer completes, everything you parked is **sent automatically**, in order, as one message.

### What you can do with a queued chip

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| **Edit it** | Double-click the chip (or click its edit button) — the text drops back into the composer. A draft you'd already typed is kept, with the chip's text appended on a new line. |
| **Remove it** | Click the **✕** on the chip |

### Limits

- **Files can't be queued.** Submitting with an attachment mid-stream shows: _"Files can't be queued — wait for this turn to finish or remove the file."_ Send it as its own turn instead.
- **Queues are per conversation.** A follow-up parked on one thread never drains into another one.
- **They survive closing the panel.** Parked chips are kept for the browser session, so closing AskFrnd doesn't lose them. Logging out clears them.

> **Rolling out:** the follow-up queue rides its own flag (`show-askfrnd-message-queue`) and is off until it's enabled for you. If it's switched off after you've already parked something, those chips still render and still send — nothing gets stranded.

You can also queue a follow-up **by voice** — see [Talking to AskFRND with Voice](./voice-input#speaking-while-askfrnd-is-still-answering).

## Seeing Which Conversation Is Working

Because several conversations can run at once, you need to know which one is still going. In the sidebar's **Chat** list, a thread that's still answering has its **title shimmering** — a highlight sweeps across it until the reply finishes.

That's the whole indicator: no spinner, no badge. The title itself carries the state, so the row never changes size and nothing jumps around as threads start and finish.

> **Why it matters:** without it, a conversation you walked away from mid-answer looks exactly like a finished one, and the only way to find out would be to open it.

## Privacy & History

- Your AskFrnd conversations are stored in your account
- Workspace admins can see usage analytics (how many credits, which features), but **not the content** of your conversations
- Where you can **see** a given conversation depends on how the panel is open: see [Chat History](#chat-history) above
- Open the history in the floating or docked panel and pick **All Brands** when you're not sure which brand a past conversation happened in

## FAQ

**Q: AskFrnd said it couldn't read my attachment.**
The file couldn't be read, so its contents aren't available to AskFrnd. Try another copy (for example, re-export the PDF), or paste the relevant text into the message.

**Q: Links in AskFrnd's answers were hard to read in the light theme.**
That's fixed. Link chips in chat now read clearly in **Daylight**, as they do in Midnight and Aurora.

**Q: Is AskFrnd the same as the old chat in frndOS v1?**
It evolved from that, yes — but it's redesigned. The biggest changes: it's now a persistent panel (not a separate page), and it's context-aware based on which pillar you're using.

**Q: How much does AskFrnd cost in credits?**
Credit cost depends on the model and the type of action. Image and video generation cost more than text-only conversation. Cost is shown **after** generation completes.

**Q: Can AskFrnd see my campaign data?**
Yes, when you open AskFrnd from inside the Insights pillar — it can analyze the campaign and post data visible in your dashboards.

**Q: Can I switch models mid-conversation?**
Check the model selector in the AskFrnd panel. Behavior may vary based on the specific implementation.

**Q: My AskFrnd panel is gone. Where did it go?**
Click the **Ask FRND** button in the bottom-right corner to reopen it, or **New chat** in the sidebar.

**Q: Why does my chat history look different depending on where I open it?**
Floating or docked, the history starts on **All Brands**. Full screen, it's scoped to the brand you're on. See [Chat History](#chat-history) above.

**Q: What's the difference between Reply and Branch?**
**Reply** stays in the same chat and points your next message at one answer. **Branch from here** starts a new chat from that answer, so you can try another direction without changing the original.

**Q: I can't find a chat I had last week. Where should I look?**
Open **Chat history** in the floating or docked panel, keep **All Brands**, and use **Search chats...**. It searches across all your brands at once.

**Q: How big a file can I attach?**
**20 MB** per file, up to 5 files — raised from the old 10 MB limit. Video, on models that take it, goes up to 200 MB.

**Q: Can AskFrnd use custom brand fonts in generated visuals?**
Not yet — the fonts from Brand IQ → Identity are currently only used by the KV Generator. For chat-based generation, you can upload custom fonts directly into your prompt or context.
