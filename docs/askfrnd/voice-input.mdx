# Talking to AskFRND with Voice

**Voice input** lets you dictate to AskFRND instead of typing. Your words appear in the composer as you speak, and when you stop talking the message **sends by itself** — you never press send.

The session also **outlives the panel**. Close AskFRND or move to another page and a small floating bar stays on screen so you can keep talking.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **A browser with speech recognition** — frndOS uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. If your browser doesn't have it, the mic simply never appears.
2. ✅ **Microphone permission** — your browser asks the first time. Blocking it stops voice input.
3. ✅ **A model that supports voice** — the mic only appears for chat models flagged for voice input. Switch models if you don't see it.

> **Rolling out:** voice input rides a kill switch (`show-voice-command`). It's on by default, but if it has been switched off for your workspace the mic, the floating bar, and the shortcut all disappear together.

## Three Ways to Start Talking

| How | What to do | Ends when |
|---|---|---|
| **Composer mic** | Click the microphone in the AskFRND composer | You stop speaking (the browser detects end-of-speech) |
| **Hold Space** | Hold the **Space** bar on an empty composer and talk | You release Space |
| **⌘⇧V** (`Ctrl⇧V` on Windows/Linux) | Press it anywhere in frndOS | You stop speaking, or press it again |

The mic tooltip says it all: _"Voice input — click, or hold Space to talk. ⌘⇧V toggles it anywhere."_

### When the mic shows up

The mic takes over the **send** slot only when a keystroke couldn't use it — so it appears when:

- the composer is **empty** (no typed draft),
- **no files** are attached,
- nothing is currently streaming,
- the composer isn't disabled (read-only shared chats are disabled).

Type a character or attach a file and the slot goes back to the normal send button.

> **Heads-up:** `⌘⇧V` is normally "paste as plain text". In ordinary text fields frndOS leaves that alone. Inside the AskFRND composer the shortcut starts voice instead — but only while the composer is **empty**, so it never overwrites a draft you're in the middle of. And if a voice round is already live, `⌘⇧V` always stops it.

## What You'll See While It Listens

On an empty thread the panel replaces the greeting with the **listening state**:

- The Ask FRND badge, animated
- A live **waveform**
- The heading **Listening…**
- Your words as they're recognised, or the prompt _"Speak now — your words appear here as you talk."_
- A **Stop** button

When your message is on its way, the heading becomes **Sending…**.

## The Floating Voice Bar

Close the AskFRND panel mid-session and the round doesn't die — a small pill appears at the bottom of the content area:

| State | What the bar shows |
|---|---|
| **Listening** | A waveform and your live transcript, or _"Listening…"_ |
| **Sending** | A spinner and _"Sending: &lt;your message&gt;"_ |
| **Idle** (between rounds) | **Voice is on — tap to speak** |
| **Error** | The error message in red |

The **✕** on the bar dismisses the whole voice session.

> **Why it survives:** the session is deliberately kept alive outside the panel, so closing AskFRND or navigating to another page doesn't cut you off mid-sentence.

## Speaking While AskFRND Is Still Answering

If a turn is still streaming, the send slot is a **Stop** button — so voice moves to a **sidecar** mic beside it. Tap it (or hold Space) and dictate your follow-up: instead of interrupting the answer, it's **parked as a queued follow-up** and sent when the current turn finishes.

See [Queueing follow-ups](./overview#queueing-follow-ups-while-askfrnd-is-answering).

## Which Language It Recognises

Voice input uses your **browser's language setting**. There's no language picker in frndOS.

## When Something Goes Wrong

frndOS shows a plain-language message and ends the session shortly after:

| Situation | Message |
|---|---|
| Mic permission blocked | _"Microphone access is blocked. Allow mic permission in your browser settings to use voice input."_ |
| No microphone found | _"No microphone found. Connect a microphone and try again."_ |
| Speech service unreachable | _"Speech service is unreachable. Check your connection and try again."_ |
| Nothing was heard | _"Didn't catch that — press the mic and try again."_ |
| Anything else | _"Voice input isn't available right now. Try again."_ |

## FAQ

**Q: Do I have to press send after dictating?**
No. When speech ends, the message sends itself. That's why there's no send button during a voice round.

**Q: I stopped talking mid-sentence and it sent anyway.**
In tap mode the browser decides when speech has ended, so a long pause can end the round. Use **hold Space** instead — capture keeps going through pauses and only ends when you release the key.

**Q: I don't see a microphone anywhere.**
Check three things: your composer is empty with no attachments, your browser supports speech recognition, and the chat model you've picked supports voice input.

**Q: I closed the panel and it's still listening.**
That's intentional — the floating bar keeps the session alive. Click the **✕** on the bar to end it.
