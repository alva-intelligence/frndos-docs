# Growth

:::info Coming soon

**Growth** is one of the five frndOS pillars, but it is **still being built**. You may see it listed in a brand's sidebar — it isn't usable yet.

This page exists so the overall map of frndOS makes sense. There is nothing to set up or try today.

:::

## What Growth Will Be For

Growth is the **distribution** pillar — the step after creative is finished in [Studio](../studio/overview). The plan is to take an approved asset and push it to your audience without leaving frndOS.

| Planned Tool | What It Will Do | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Social Ads** | Publish and manage paid social campaigns from frndOS | Coming soon |
| **WhatsApp Blast** | Send campaign messages to a customer list | Coming soon |

## What to Use Today Instead

While Growth is being built:

- **Produce the creative** in [Studio](../studio/overview), then export and publish through your existing ad platforms
- **Measure what you publish** in [Insights](../insights/overview) — Paid, Owned, Earned, and ATL performance already flow into your dashboards
- **Build the audience list** in [Research](../research/overview) via Customer Intelligence

> Growth availability will be announced in the [What's New](/blog) section. Nothing here needs to be configured in advance.
