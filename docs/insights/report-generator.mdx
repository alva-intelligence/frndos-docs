# Report Generator

**Report Generator** makes a presentation deck of your brand's Insights numbers, so you don't copy figures into slides by hand. You ask AskFRND for a report, check what it plans, and get a deck in **Files**. After you've seen the report, you can make it **automatic**, so next period's is waiting for you.

Everything happens in the chat. Every slide has a headline that states what the numbers show, the chart or table, a short **Takeaway** on what it means, and a source line.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ You can open the Insights data tabs you want in the report. A report only covers tabs you can read.
2. ✅ The brand has data connected in Insights. A tab with no connection or no numbers for the period gets a "no data" slide instead of figures.
3. ✅ To **make a report automatic** you also need the permission to schedule reports for that brand. Whoever can make a report for a brand can usually do this.

## Starting a Report

There are three ways in:

* **Generate report** is a button in the toolbar of every Insights data tab, between the filters and Settings. It opens AskFRND with that tab, the brand and the dates on screen already filled in.
* **Make a report** is a starter card at the end of the starter questions when AskFRND is open on an Insights page.
* **Type it.** Ask AskFRND, for example "make a report for October". If your request is vague, it asks one question: which type of report.

The Report Generator works on these tabs: **Business**, **Ads**, **Social Media**, **Influencers** and **ATL**.

## The Confirm Card

Before it writes anything, AskFRND shows one card with the choices for this report.

| Field | What it sets |
|---|---|
| **Report** | **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Quarterly**. **Campaign…** is shown but not available yet. A note under it explains what each type does: weeks run Monday to Sunday, monthly is the most common, and quarterly is a deeper structure |
| **Covers** | Which Insights tabs it covers. It reads like **Social Media** with **1 of 4**. Choose **All sources**, or tick tabs one by one. At least one must stay on. A tab that isn't connected is greyed with **Not connected**, and a connected tab with no numbers for the period shows **No data** with the period and is not ticked for you |
| **This report covers** | The period. The latest complete one is first, then earlier periods. Below them, a section for the month or week that isn't over yet offers **so far** (made now, marked "so far") and **when it ends** (made by itself on its own day). **The page's dates** appears last when the Insights page had other dates, and makes a one-off |
| **Numbers as of** | The day the numbers are read from |

The primary button reads **Continue**.

### The default

Type comes from where you started, the period is the latest complete one, covers is the tab you came from (a quarterly report starts with every source), and **Numbers as of** is today.

### Numbers as of

The field reads like **Today · Oct 28**, a past day such as **Sep 7, 2026**, or **Later · Nov 3**.

* **Today** uses the latest numbers, recorded up to today.
* **A past day** is available only when **Social Media** is the only cover, because only Social Media keeps daily history. The calendar offers the days after the period ends on which at least one of its posts had saved numbers. While it checks, it says **Checking which days have Social Media numbers…**, and if it can't, **Past days can't be checked right now.** A report as of a past day shows the numbers as they stood that day. A post with no numbers on that day is never filled in with today's.
* **Make it on a later day** (not offered for a "so far" report) lets you pick a day up to 60 days ahead. The card says _Not made now. This report is made by itself on_ the day you pick, _and you'll be told when it's ready._
* If you turn on another cover after choosing a past day, the field goes back to Today and a note says so (it says Numbers as of is back to Today, because only Social Media keeps daily history).

### Style

The card has a **Style** row. It says whether the deck will use the brand's own look and voice: colours, fonts and logo come from **Brand IQ**, and the words follow its voice and audience. If Brand IQ has colours but no deck style yet, one is made when you continue. With no brand colours, it says it will use the frndOS default style. **Edit brand look** or **Set up brand look** opens the brand's identity settings in a new tab.

### A report you already have

If a report for the same brand, type, period and covers already exists, the card says **You already have this one**, with its title, the date its numbers are as of, and that it is in Files. **Open it** opens it, and the button changes to **Make a fresh one**. Change any field and it's treated as a different report. A Social Media report and an All sources report of the same month are different reports.

## The Outline

The first time a brand makes a given type, AskFRND suggests a structure based on what the brand actually has in Insights: the connected tabs in the covers, the KPIs and targets, and what moved this period. It shows it as an outline in a read-only preview beside the chat, headed with the brand, the type and **report structure** (for example **Top Coffee · Monthly report structure**). A line under it counts the slides and the version, for example **Outline · v2 (v1 kept) · 8 slides · not saved yet**. There is one row per slide.

Each row shows the slide's title and its source, such as **Social Media**, **All sources**, or **AI summary** for the slides the AI writes itself. The slide kinds include:

| Slide | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Key numbers** | The headline KPIs |
| **Trend** | A metric over time |
| **Top posts**, **Worst posts** | The best and weakest posts |
| **Channel mix** | How channels split |
| **Table**, **Period comparison** | Ranked figures, or this period against the last |
| **Pillar breakdown**, **Content breakdown**, **Label breakdown** | Results per content pillar, format or label |
| **What stood out** | A short AI-written read of the period |
| **Since last report** | What changed since the previous report |
| **Things to check** | What the AI wants a human to look at before presenting |

A tab with no data for the period shows as one **no data** slide, marked in the outline.

### Changing it

Use the suggestion chips under the outline, which **Change the outline**, or type what you want. Each change makes a new version, and **Undo** steps back. When it looks right, click the button that says it looks good and to write it. After the deck is written, you edit slides in the deck editor instead.

If you suggest adding a slide, AskFRND may ask **Add to your outline?**

### Next time

Once you start writing, the agreed outline is saved for that brand, report type and set of covers. Next time, the chat asks **Want it like last time?** with the structure's name, slide count and version, and two buttons: **Write it**, or **Change the structure**. A change affects future reports only, and past reports are never overwritten.

## Writing

While it works, a short list shows the step: **Reading the numbers**, **Writing the slides**, then **Saving to Files**. The deck preview opens beside the chat and slides appear one by one. Closing the chat doesn't stop it, and you're told when it's ready.

A deck is only saved when every planned slide is written. If it can't be made, a card says **The report wasn't made** with the reason, and nothing half-finished appears in Files.

### When it's ready

The ready card shows the report with:

* **Open in Slides** (opens the deck in a new tab)
* **Share**, which uses the deck's normal sharing, and a notice reads **Deck link copied**
* **More**, with **Play** and **Edit the template**

The chat's title becomes the brand, the type and the period, for example "Top Coffee · Monthly report, Sep 2026".

## Where Reports Live

A report is a deck in **Files**, under **Decks**, with a **Report** badge, and an **Automatic** badge if it repeats. In Files, **Decks** has a **Reports only** filter. The numbers in a report are locked when it's made and never change afterwards. To use fresh numbers, ask for a new report: it is a separate, labelled deck and the old one stays as it was.

A report is written in English for now. There is no brand or workspace language setting yet, and the language you type in the chat doesn't change it.

## Making It Automatic

After a report is ready, AskFRND asks **Want this every** month, week or quarter**, automatically?** Choose **Yes, set it up** or **Not now**. You don't get the offer for a one-off (the page's dates or a past period).

You can also start it later from Files: in a report deck's menu, below **Quick Look**, **Make this automatic**. If it is already automatic, the row reads **Automatic** with its cadence, for example **Automatic · Monthly**, and opens its schedule.

### The automation card

| Row | What it sets |
|---|---|
| **Covers** | The covers the report was made with. A tab connected later is suggested to you, never added silently |
| **Report day** | Monthly and quarterly: the 1st to the 28th. Weekly: a weekday |
| **Repeat** (weekly only) | **Every week** or **Every 2 weeks** |
| **Next** | The upcoming reports |
| **Tell me when it's ready** | You, in-app and by email. Only you are notified |

It runs at **08:00 WIB** (Asia/Jakarta) for every brand. That time is shown but isn't editable, because brands don't have a time-zone setting yet. Choose **Make it automatic**, or **Not now**. When the card is for an existing schedule, **Pause** and **Resume** are there too. The first automatic report covers the **next** period, because this one already exists.

Making it automatic builds a workflow behind the scenes, and a preview of it opens beside the chat.

### One schedule per structure

Asking again for a report you already automated opens the existing schedule. It never creates a second one. The chat says when the next one will arrive, and **Edit the schedule** reopens the card.

## When a Report Is Made For You

* **Ready.** You get a notification, and an email if **Email me about reports** is on, with a short summary and **Open report**. The deck is in Files with its badges.
* **Needs attention.** A run that fails or doesn't finish is never saved or sent. You're told, and the notification offers **Retry**.
* **Waiting for data.** If a source hasn't synced, the run waits rather than sending a partial deck. The report card says **Waiting for data** and **Nothing is made until the data is back. You're told when it's ready.** It checks daily at 08:00 WIB and continues by itself.
* **A scheduled one-off.** A report you set for a later day shows **Scheduled** and **Made by itself on** that day, **08:00 WIB**, and lands in Files like any other.

In **Workflows**, an automatic report appears with its cadence, when it runs next, and when it last ran, for example **Monthly · next around Dec 5 · last run Nov 5**, with states **Needs attention** (with **Retry**), **Waiting for data**, **Paused** and **Ended**. Its menu leads with **Edit schedule**. Pausing is done in the chat, so there is no switch on the row.

Turn the email off under **Workspace Settings → Notifications**, **Reports**, **Email me about reports** (_When a scheduled report is ready, fails or pauses_).

## FAQ

**Q: I don't see Generate report.**
It shows on the Business, Ads, Social Media, Influencers and ATL tabs only when you can open that tab on the brand.

**Q: Can I make a Campaign report?**
Not yet. The **Campaign…** option is shown on the confirm card but can't be chosen.

**Q: Why can't I pick a past date?**
Past dates need **Social Media** to be the only cover, and the brand needs saved daily numbers. Other tabs don't keep daily history.

**Q: Will my numbers change later?**
No. A report keeps the numbers it was made with. Ask for a new report to use fresh numbers.

**Q: Who gets the automatic report?**
You, the person who set it up. Others can open the deck through Files, or you can share it.

**Q: How long does a report take?**
Roughly a minute for a monthly deck. It is never instant, and you're told when it's done.
