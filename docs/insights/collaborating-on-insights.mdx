# Collaborating on Insights

Insights is not just a place to read numbers — it's a place to discuss them. You can **save the exact slice** you're looking at, **follow** it so you hear when it changes, and **comment** on a page or on a single figure so the conversation lives next to the data instead of in a chat thread with a screenshot.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **Access to a brand's Insights** — see [Insights Overview](./overview)
2. ✅ **A tab with data connected** — see [Core & Extended Connections](./core-extended-connections)

## Where the Controls Are

The collaboration controls sit in the **top bar**, next to the avatars of whoever else is on the page:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **🔔 bell** | Follow this brand |
| **💬 speech bubble** | Comment on this view — the number beside it is the thread count |
| **Share** | Hand the page to someone |

One more sits in the filter row, with the date range: **Follow view**.

> **Why two places?** A control belongs with the scope it changes. What you follow as a *view* depends on the tab and the filters, so it lives with them. Discussing and sharing are things you do with people, not ways of changing what you're looking at — so they sit with the avatars, on every tab.

## Saved Views

A **view** is one exact slice of a tab: which tab, which filters, and which date window. Saving it makes it addressable — you can link to it, follow it, and comment on it.

The current view lives in the page URL, which is why **Share** hands someone the same filters you're looking at.

### Following a view

1. Set the tab, filters, and date range you care about
2. Click **Follow view** in the filter row
3. Name it (a name is suggested from the filters) and press Enter, or click **Follow**

Once saved, the control reads **Following view**. Hover it to see what you're following and over what window.

> **Heads-up:** a view follows a **rolling** window. If you're on an exact date range, it's saved as the nearest rolling one, and the panel says so — _"Dates roll forward — you'll follow Last 30 days."_ A frozen range could never notify you again, so it isn't offered.

Rolling windows available: **Last 7 days**, **Last 14 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **Month to date**, **Quarter to date**.

> **Note:** two people naming the same slice differently are still following the same thing — views are matched on the filters and window, not on the name.

## Following a Brand

Click the **🔔 bell** in the top bar to follow the whole brand. A toast confirms which way it went:

| State | Toast |
|---|---|
| Now following | **Following this brand** — _"You'll be notified when something changes on this brand."_ |
| Stopped | **Stopped following** — _"You won't be notified about changes here."_ |

The bell fills in while you're following.

> **Heads-up:** following a **brand** and following a **view** are different subscriptions. The brand bell is everything on the brand; **Follow view** is that one slice.

## Comments

### Commenting on a page

Click the **💬 speech bubble** in the top bar. If you've already saved this view, the comment rail opens. If you haven't, frndOS explains what will happen first:

> _"Commenting saves this view as &lt;name&gt; so the thread has something to hang on — and you'll follow it."_
>
> _"Anyone on this brand will be able to see the view and the discussion."_

Click **Comment & save view** to continue.

### The comment rail

The rail opens on the right with two rows of controls:

**Which pages:**

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **This page** | Threads on the view you're looking at |
| **All pages** | Every thread on this brand — how you find the one you owe someone an answer to |

**Which threads:**

| Filter | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Everything — the memory |
| **Unresolved** | The working queue |
| **@me** | Threads you were mentioned in, or are part of |

Empty states tell you which filter you're in — for example _"Nothing unresolved on this brand"_ or _"You're not in any thread here."_

Write in the box at the bottom. The label above it names what you're commenting on: **New comment on this view**, **New comment on this dashboard**, or the specific figure you have selected.

### Commenting on a single number

Some figures carry their own comment button. Use it and the thread is anchored to that datapoint — clicking the thread later takes you straight back to it, the same way a notification deep link does.

The rail hints at this when a view has no threads yet: _"Write below, or use a number's comment button to talk about that figure."_

### Mentions, replies, and resolving

- **@mention** a colleague to pull them in — mentions drive the notification
- **Reply** in the thread
- **React** with an emoji
- **Resolve** a thread when it's settled, or **reopen** it later
- **Edit** or **delete** your own comments

> **Note:** resolved threads aren't deleted. They drop out of **Unresolved** and stay under **All**.

## Getting Notified

Following something is only useful if the alerts reach you the way you want them. All of that is personal, per workspace, and lives in **Workspace Settings → Notifications** — see [Notification Settings](../settings/notifications).

## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between following a brand and following a view?**
The brand bell subscribes you to everything on the brand. **Follow view** subscribes you to one tab with one set of filters over one rolling window.

**Q: Why did my exact date range change when I followed the view?**
A frozen range can never change again, so it could never notify you. frndOS saves the nearest rolling window instead and tells you which one.

**Q: Why does commenting save a view?**
A thread needs something to hang on. Without a saved view there's nothing to attach the discussion to — or to take a reader back to.

**Q: Who can see my comments?**
Anyone with access to that brand. The confirmation says so before you create the thread.

**Q: I know there's a thread but I can't find it.**
Switch the rail from **This page** to **All pages** — that lists every thread on the brand regardless of which tab it started on.

**Q: Can I comment on one specific number?**
Yes, where a figure carries its own comment button. The thread anchors to that datapoint, and opening it later brings you back to it.

**Q: Someone shared a link and I see different numbers.**
Check the date range. A shared link carries the filters and the window it was copied with.
