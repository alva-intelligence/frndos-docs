# Collaborating on Insights

Insights is not just a place to read numbers — it's a place to discuss them. You can **save the exact slice** you're looking at, **follow** it so you hear when it changes, and **comment** on a page or on a single figure so the conversation lives next to the data instead of in a chat thread with a screenshot.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **Access to a brand's Insights** — see [Insights Overview](./overview)
2. ✅ **A tab with data connected** — see [Core & Extended Connections](./core-extended-connections)

## Where the Controls Are

The collaboration controls sit in the **top bar**, next to the avatars of whoever else is on the page:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **🔔 bell** | Follow this brand |
| **💬 speech bubble** | Comment on this view — the number beside it is the thread count |
| **Share** | Hand the page to someone |

One more sits in the filter row, with the date range: **Follow view**.

> **Why two places?** A control belongs with the scope it changes. What you follow as a *view* depends on the tab and the filters, so it lives with them. Discussing and sharing are things you do with people, not ways of changing what you're looking at — so they sit with the avatars, on every tab.

## Saved Views

A **view** is one exact slice of a tab: which tab, which filters, and which date window. Saving it makes it addressable — you can link to it, follow it, and comment on it.

The current view lives in the page URL, which is why **Share** hands someone the same filters you're looking at.

### Following a view

1. Set the tab, filters, and date range you care about
2. Click **Follow view** in the filter row
3. Name it (a name is suggested from the filters) and press Enter, or click **Follow**

Once saved, the control reads **Following view**. Hover it to see what you're following and over what window.

> **Heads-up:** a view follows a **rolling** window. If you're on an exact date range, it's saved as the nearest rolling one, and the panel says so — _"Dates roll forward — you'll follow Last 30 days."_ A frozen range could never notify you again, so it isn't offered.

Rolling windows available: **Last 7 days**, **Last 14 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **Month to date**, **Quarter to date**.

> **Note:** two people naming the same slice differently are still following the same thing — views are matched on the filters and window, not on the name.

## Following a Brand

Click the **🔔 bell** in the top bar to follow the whole brand. A toast confirms which way it went:

| State | Toast |
|---|---|
| Now following | **Following this brand** — _"You'll be notified when something changes on this brand."_ |
| Stopped | **Stopped following** — _"You won't be notified about changes here."_ |

The bell fills in while you're following.

> **Heads-up:** following a **brand** and following a **view** are different subscriptions. The brand bell is everything on the brand; **Follow view** is that one slice.

## Comments

Commenting on Insights is **anchored**: you aim at the thing you're talking about — a chart, a metric card, a table row, a creator, a post — and drop a pin on it. The thread lives next to the number instead of in a chat somewhere.

### Turning comment mode on

Click the **💬 speech bubble** in the top bar, or press **C**.

> **What that click does:** a tab isn't a row, so there's nothing to hang a thread off until the view is saved. Clicking the speech bubble **saves the current view** for you — in one click, with no confirmation step. The saved view appears in the shared list, you start following it, and it stays even if you never type anything.

The button itself tells you what's waiting before you open it:

| Button state | Means |
|---|---|
| Plain bubble | No open threads on this tab |
| Bubble **+ a number** | That many **unresolved threads** on this tab — threads, not comments, so a long discussion still counts as one |
| Small **orange dot** | An open thread **mentions you** |

Hovering names the surface the count belongs to — e.g. _"Paid Media · 3 open comments"_ — because the number changes as you move between tabs.

### Placing a pin

With comment mode armed, your cursor turns into a pin over anything you can comment on. Click it and a composer opens beside the pin.

- Clicking **empty space** places nothing — you stay in the mode.
- Clicking a **control** (a filter pill, a button) does what that control does. It doesn't leave a comment.
- After posting, you're **still in comment mode**, so a whole review pass takes no re-arming.
- **Escape** steps back out one level at a time: composer → open thread → out of the mode.

A thread with no particular target goes at the bottom of the panel instead: the composer there is labelled _"Comment on this page…"_.

When a tab has nothing on it yet, the panel says: _"No comments yet. Press C and click a number to leave one, or comment on the page below."_

### The comment dock

The dock opens on the right, headed **Comments**. It carries:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Search comments** | Finds a thread by its text — the fastest route on a busy page |
| **All / Open / Done / @me** | Filter pills, each with its own count |
| **Filter** | A popover with the extras — **Has image**, and filtering by author |
| **👁 eye** | Hide or show the pins on the page (the threads stay listed) |
| **✕** | Close comments and leave the mode |

> **One right-hand column:** the comment dock and the AskFrnd panel take the same space. Opening one closes the other.

**Desktop only.** Comment mode is aim-and-click, so it isn't offered on the phone layout.

### Finding a thread across the whole brand

The **All pages** view lists every discussion on the brand, grouped by the page it was left on — how you find the one you owe someone an answer to. It has its own filters: **All**, **Unresolved**, **@me**. It's read-only; open a thread on its own page to reply.

### Threads whose number is gone

Filters change, ranges roll, rows paginate. A thread whose target has left the page is marked **Not on this page**, with the explanation _"The number this comment was left on isn't on this page — the tab's filters or range may have changed"_. The thread is never hidden or deleted; only its pin can't be drawn.

Open a link to such a thread and you get a toast instead of a hunt: **Comment is still here** — _"The item it was left on isn't on this page — open it from the list."_

### Mentions, replies, reactions, and resolving

- **@mention** a colleague to pull them in — mentions are what drive the notification. The suggestion list is scoped to people who can actually open the page.
- **Reply** in the thread. The composer hint says it: _"@mention to notify · ⏎ to post"_, and in a reply _"⏎ to reply · Shift+Enter for new line"_.
- **React** with an emoji — 👍 ❤️ 🎉 😄 😢 👀. Hover a reaction to see who reacted.
- **Attach an image** to a comment with the paperclip.
- **Edit** your own comment — it's marked as edited afterwards.
- **Delete** your own comment. Deleting the first comment of a thread takes the whole thread with it, and the confirmation says how many replies that is.
- **Resolve** a thread when it's settled, or **reopen** it later.

> **Note:** resolved threads aren't deleted. They drop out of **Open** and stay under **All** and **Done**.

## Getting Notified

Following something is only useful if the alerts reach you the way you want them. All of that is personal, per workspace, and lives in **Workspace Settings → Notifications** — see [Notification Settings](../settings/notifications).

## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between following a brand and following a view?**
The brand bell subscribes you to everything on the brand. **Follow view** subscribes you to one tab with one set of filters over one rolling window.

**Q: Why did my exact date range change when I followed the view?**
A frozen range can never change again, so it could never notify you. frndOS saves the nearest rolling window instead and tells you which one.

**Q: Why does commenting save a view?**
A thread needs something to hang on. Without a saved view there's nothing to attach the discussion to — or to take a reader back to. That's why the speech bubble saves the view the moment you click it.

**Q: Who can see my comments?**
Anyone with access to that brand.

**Q: I know there's a thread but I can't find it.**
Use **Search comments** in the dock, or switch to **All pages** — that lists every thread on the brand regardless of which tab it started on.

**Q: Can I comment on one specific number?**
Yes. Press **C** (or click the speech bubble) and click the figure — the thread anchors to that datapoint, and opening it later brings you back to it.

**Q: A thread says "Not on this page".**
The number it was left on isn't rendered right now — the filters, the date range, or the table page changed. The thread is intact; only its pin has nowhere to sit.

**Q: My AskFrnd panel closed when I opened comments.**
They share the right-hand column, so only one can be open at a time.

**Q: I clicked a filter pill in comment mode and it filtered instead of leaving a comment.**
That's deliberate — controls keep doing what they do. Pins only land on the data itself.

**Q: Someone shared a link and I see different numbers.**
Check the date range. A shared link carries the filters and the window it was copied with.
