# Sharing Radar

You can share a whole Radar page, or a single Industry story, with a link. There are two kinds of link: a **team link** for people already on the brand, and a **public link** for people outside frndOS, such as a client or an agency, who don't need to log in.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ You are working on a **brand** and on one of the Radar tabs you can share (see [What You Can Share](#what-you-can-share)).
2. ✅ To **see** the Share button for a page, you need to be able to read every Radar tab that page carries. To **change** the public link you need write access on at least one of them.
3. ✅ For a story, you open it from Industry. Changing its public link needs write access to Industry.

## What You Can Share

| Page | Needs read access to | An outsider sees |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | Trends, Listening and Industry | The briefing, trends, conversation and industry news. Comments, and what this means for your brand, stay private |
| **Industry** | Industry | This week's industry news, competitor moves and regulators. Comments and your competitor list stay private |
| **Trends** | Trends | This week's strongest trend and the list of trends, as cards. Comments, bookmarks and the ideas drafted for your brand stay private |
| **Listening** | Listening | The topic cards and what people say, in their own words. The war room, keywords and settings stay private |
| **A single story** | Industry | The story, its sources, and what it means for your brand as text only. Your team's discussion stays private |

**Prediction** and **Explore** have no link of their own yet.

## Sharing a Page

1. Open the Radar tab you want to share.
2. Click **Share** in the top bar. It only shows once the page has something worth sharing: for Industry, a covered industry; for Overview, once it has loaded.
3. The dialog is titled **Share Overview**, **Share Industry**, **Share Trends** or **Share Listening**.

The dialog has two parts.

### Team link

**Copy link** copies the address of the page exactly as you see it now, including an open story. Anyone on the brand can follow it. It doesn't depend on the public link being on, and a notice reads **Link copied**.

### Public link

For people outside frndOS, with no login needed. You can set:

* **Access**: **Anyone with the link can view**
* **Expires**: **24 hours**, **7 days**, **30 days** or **Never**. An expired link can be renewed
* **Protection**: **Require password**, at least 8 characters with at least one uppercase letter and one number
* **Copy link**, **Save link**, and **Regenerate**, which makes a new URL and stops the old one working at once, after you confirm

These controls work the same as for a Listening topic; see [Sharing and Exporting](../insights/social-listening#sharing-and-exporting) for the password rules and for how renewing works. If you don't have write access, the dialog says **Only people who can manage Market can change the public link.**

The note under the public link repeats what an outsider will and won't see for that page.

## Sharing a Story

Open a story in Industry and click **Share story** in the pop-up header. The dialog has the same two parts:

* **Team link** opens the story's pop-up inside frndOS for anyone on the brand.
* **Public link** has the same access, expiry and password options.

People with the public link see the story, its sources and what it means for your brand, as text only. Your team's discussion never leaves frndOS.

## What an Outsider Sees

A person who opens a public link sees the page read-only, with the brand's name and logo at the top and the line **Shared via frndOS**. If the link has an expiry, it says **Link expires** and the date.

* The page shows only the tab you shared. There are no tabs to other pages, because one link opens one page.
* A password-protected link asks for the password first.
* Comments, the Share button, Settings, **Show**, **New since**, **Your competitors** and **What this means for you** are not there.
* Parts that need an account, such as links to other tabs, are shown as plain text or left out.
* A story opened from a shared page opens read-only, with no discussion, no Ask FRND and no team actions.

If a link was turned off or has expired, the visitor sees **This share link is not available**, and is told to ask the sender for a new link.

### A team link you can't open

If you open a team link to a story and your role doesn't give you Industry, the page says **You don't have access to stories on** the brand's name, and to ask a brand admin to give you access to Market › Industry.

## FAQ

**Q: Who can see a public link?**
Anyone who has it. Set an expiry and a password for anything sensitive.

**Q: I regenerated the link. What happens to the old one?**
It stops working at once. Send the new link to everyone who needs it.

**Q: Can an outsider comment or ask FRND?**
No. Comments, discussion and Ask FRND stay inside frndOS.

**Q: Where is the Share button?**
In the top bar of Overview, Industry, Trends and Listening. Prediction and Explore have no page link of their own yet.
