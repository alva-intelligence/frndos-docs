# Radar Overview

**Radar** shows what is moving in your market. Its **Overview** tab is one screen that pulls together what is rising, what people say about your brand, and what your industry and competitors did, for the brand you're working on.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ You are working on a **brand**. Radar opens for the brand in the page title.
2. ✅ Your role can read at least one Radar tab for that brand. Overview shows only the sections whose tab you can open.
3. ✅ For the conversation section you need **Listening** set up with at least one topic. For the industry section the brand needs an industry set in its settings.

## Opening Radar

Open **Radar** from the **Apps** section of the sidebar (see [Apps](../settings/apps)). It opens on **Overview**, or on the first Radar tab you can read.

The tabs along the top are **Overview**, **Prediction**, **Trends**, **Listening**, **Explore** and **Industry**. Which of them you see depends on your role and on what your workspace has switched on. Admins can rename or hide Radar tabs; see [Customizing Radar Tabs](./radar-tabs).

## What's New Since Your Last Visit

At the top right of the page, a line such as **New since Tue 18:40** summarises what arrived after you last looked: for example **1 new alert**, **2 new trends**, or a competitor's move. It only appears when something is new, and not on your first visit. Times are shown in Jakarta time.

## The Daily Briefing

The first card, marked **Daily briefing**, gives one headline and a few sentences that tie Trends, Listening and Industry together. A line under it says what it is based on, for example the number of active trends, mentions and industry articles. On a quiet day it says so.

## Front Page

**Front page** lists up to three of the biggest stories from across Radar, once a day. Each item is one of:

* **Conversation**, a topic from Listening with its mentions in the last 24 hours, shown as a bar chart where spikes stand out
* **Trends**, a rising trend
* A news story from Industry

Hover a story to see what it means for you. Click it to open it. A listening or trend item opens its own page.

## Trends, Conversation and Industry

Below the front page come up to three sections, each with a headline, a "based on" line, and a link to its full tab (**See all in Trends**, **See all in Listening**, **See all in Industry**):

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Trends** | **Your strongest trends lately**, as cards. Hover one to see how your brand can join in. If none yet: **No trends yet** |
| **Conversation** | The topics people talk about most, with their own words, based on mentions in the last 24 hours. Until Listening is set up it offers **Set up Listening**. With no data yet: **No mentions yet** |
| **Industry** | What your competitors, government and regulators, and the industry did this week. If the news service can't be reached it says **Market news isn't available right now** |

A section you can't read never appears.

## The Side Column

On a wide screen two cards sit to the right and stay in view as you scroll.

**Market pulse** has up to four rows, each with its change and a seven-day line:

* **Mentions · 24h**
* **Positive sentiment · 24h**
* **Competitor moves · 7d**
* **Active trends · 7d**

A row only shows when its source is available. Point at the line to read each day's value. If Listening isn't set up, the card suggests setting it up.

**What this means for you** lists risks and openings that need your attention, each tagged **Risk** or **Opportunity** with a short reason and, where there is one, a suggested action. When nothing needs you it reads **Nothing needs you today**.

## Your First Day

When a brand has just started, Overview shows a short checklist of what is ready instead of empty cards: **Trends** (looking for the first trends), **Listening** (collecting from your topics, or **Not set up**) and **Industry** (reading sources, or **Not set up** with **Set industry**). Numbers usually start to appear within an hour of setting up a Listening topic.

## FAQ

**Q: Why is a section missing from my Overview?**
Overview only shows the sections for tabs your role can read. A tab an admin hid, or one you have no access to, hides its section too.

**Q: What does a Sample mark mean?**
It marks parts that are sample data for the preview rather than real news.

**Q: I see a different layout, with a map of Indonesia.**
Some workspaces have a different Overview layout switched on. This guide describes the standard one.

**Q: Where do the numbers come from?**
Trends, Listening and Industry, each of which has its own tab. Open the tab for the full view.
