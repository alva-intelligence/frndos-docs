# Comments on Radar

Your team can talk about what Radar shows without leaving the page. You can leave a note on a section of **Overview** or **Industry**, start a **Discussion** on a single story, and read a feed of what changed across Radar.

Comments follow the same rules as comments in Insights: pins, the comment dock, mentions, replies, reactions and resolving. This guide covers what is specific to Radar. For the basics, see [Collaborating on Insights](../insights/collaborating-on-insights#comments).

## Before You Start

1. ✅ You are working on a **brand** and can read the Radar tab you want to comment on.
2. ✅ For story discussions, your role can read **Industry** for the brand.
3. ✅ You are on a desktop screen. Comment mode is aim-and-click, so it isn't offered on a phone.

## Where Comments Work

| Radar tab | How you comment |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Pin a note on a section of the page, or open a story's Discussion |
| **Industry** | Pin a note on a section, or open a story's Discussion |
| **Trends** and **Listening** | The same comment rail as Insights, anchored to a saved view. See [Collaborating on Insights](../insights/collaborating-on-insights#comments) |
| **Prediction** and **Explore** | No comment mode of their own. The Radar activity feed is available |

On **Overview**, you can comment on a section of the page. A note on a story needs your role to read **Industry**, because the story's Discussion lives in Industry.

## Turning Comment Mode On

Click the speech bubble at the top right of the page, or press **C**. The tooltip reads **Comment on Overview (C)** or **Comment on Industry (C)**, and **Close comments** while the mode is on.

The bubble shows how many threads are open on the page. A thread counts once however long it is. A small dot means an open thread mentions you. Hover it to read it, for example **Industry · 3 open comments**.

## Commenting on a Section

With comment mode on, your cursor becomes a pin over the things you can comment on. A section of the page, such as the daily briefing, a card in the side column, or a list of stories, takes a pin.

1. Click the section. A composer opens beside the pin.
2. Write your note. Use **@** to mention someone.
3. Post it. You stay in comment mode, so a whole review pass needs no re-arming.

A thread that doesn't belong to one spot goes at the bottom of the dock, where the composer reads **Comment on this page…**. A pin that sits on a section is labelled with that section and its page, for example **Overview · Daily briefing**.

When there are no comments yet, the dock says: _No comments yet. Write one below, or press C and click a section or a story to start one._

## Commenting on a Story

A story is a finer target than a section, so clicking a story card starts a thread for that story. Story threads draw no pin on the card. Instead the card shows a small speech bubble with the number of open comments, hidden when there are none.

Every story has one list of threads, called its **Discussion**, wherever you opened it from. It is a column beside the story in its pop-up:

* It opens by default when there is a conversation, and is collapsed when there isn't.
* Its composer reads **Start a new discussion, or @ to mention someone…**.
* A thread you open from the dock shows the line **You opened this from Comments**, with the thread highlighted.

When you start a thread on a story from the page, a notice reads **Posted to the story's Discussion**, with an **Open** button that takes you to it.

If a thread was left on a story that isn't on the current page, it is marked **Not on this page**, with the note that the story isn't on this page and to open it from Industry to see its Discussion. The thread is not deleted.

> **Your discussion stays inside frndOS.** If you share a story or a page with a public link, people outside see the story but never the Discussion. See [Sharing Radar](./sharing).

## The Radar Activity Feed

Next to the comment bubble there is an activity button. Its tooltip reads **Market activity**, and **Close activity** when it is open. It opens a feed of what changed in the brand's Radar, one feed for the whole pillar. It records changes such as a trend, a content idea, a topic, a category, a destination, a source, alerts, a shared story, or a competitor.

When nothing has changed yet, it reads **Nothing has changed in this brand's Market yet.**

The comment dock and the activity feed share the same column on the right, so opening one closes the other.

## FAQ

**Q: Why can't I comment on a story?**
Story threads need your role to read **Industry** for the brand. Without it, section notes still work on Overview.

**Q: Can people outside my team see comments?**
No. Public links show the page or story without comments or discussions.

**Q: Why is my pin missing?**
A pin belongs to a section. If a thread was left on a story, it has no pin on the card. Look for the bubble count on the story, or open the thread from the dock.

**Q: Why is there no comment bubble on Explore or Prediction?**
Comment mode is available on Overview and Industry, and Trends and Listening use the Insights comment rail. Explore and Prediction have no comment mode yet.
