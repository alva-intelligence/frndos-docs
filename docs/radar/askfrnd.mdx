# Asking AskFRND About Radar

AskFRND can read what Radar shows and answer questions about it, in plain words, with charts and maps where they help. It reads the same data your Radar tabs show, under your own access, so it never reaches further than you can.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ The Radar tab you ask about is available to you. See [Radar Overview](./overview).
2. ✅ The matching **tool** is switched on. The Radar tools are **off by default** for everyone, so an admin or you have to turn them on (see the next section).
3. ✅ Your role can read that tab for the brand. AskFRND checks this on every call. If you can't open a tab, it can't read it for you.

## Turning the Radar Tools On

Radar has three tools of its own, plus Overview and Trends which are covered by existing tools:

| Tool | What AskFRND can read |
|---|---|
| **Radar Industry** | The daily briefing, competitor moves and campaigns, government and regulator updates, this week's stories and the sources behind each |
| **Radar Explore** | The social graph: the totals, the communities ranked by member accounts, the accounts that earned a role, the emotion shifts behind the stream, and the written analysis when it is ready |
| **Radar Prediction** | The stored forecast evaluations, the baseline and any validated signal-aware estimate, the cited evidence and dated events, source coverage and the settled accuracy record |

For the **Overview** and **Trends** tabs, AskFRND reads through **Brand Data** (and also **Radar Industry** for the industry part of Overview, when it is on). For **Listening**, it uses **Social Listening**.

To turn them on:

* **For yourself:** open **Chat Settings → Tools**, find the tool under **Knowledge**, switch it on and save. See [Choosing Which Tools AskFrnd Uses](../askfrnd/tools#choosing-which-tools-askfrnd-uses).
* **For the workspace:** an admin switches the tool on under **Workspace Settings → AskFRND Tools**, so every member starts with it. See [Setting the Workspace AskFRND Tools](../settings/askfrnd-tools).

A Radar tool only appears in the list where that Radar tab exists for you. If the tab isn't there, neither is its tool.

> **Radar tools only read.** They never change anything, spend credits to refresh a forecast, or search the web for you.

## Asking From a Radar Page

Open AskFRND while you are on a Radar tab. It knows which tab you are on (**Radar - Overview**, **Radar - Industry**, **Radar - Explore**, **Radar - Prediction**, **Radar - Trends** or **Radar - Listening**), and an empty chat shows starter questions for that tab. The starters only appear when the matching tool is on. Without it, you see the general starters.

| Tab | Starters |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | **Summarise Radar**, **What needs attention?**, **Explain the conversation**, **What does this mean for us?** |
| **Industry** | **What's happening this week?**, **What did competitors do?**, **Rules that affect us**, **Explain the top story** |
| **Explore** | **Who leads this conversation?**, **Explain the biggest community**, **Why did the tone shift?**, **Who is behind these coordination signals?** |
| **Prediction** | **What is predicted today?**, **Why these numbers?**, **What could change it?**, **What's missing?** |

The **Ask FRND** buttons on the tabs themselves, such as on an action in Prediction or an item in Explore, open AskFRND with the right context attached. See [Radar Prediction](./prediction#asking-frnd) and [Radar Explore](./explore).

## What a Radar Answer Looks Like

Radar answers are meant to be quick to read:

* **A short headline.** One bold line with the answer, then a few one-line points and a next step. About 60 to 90 words by default.
* **Who is behind the movement.** Every answer names the accounts, outlets, communities or groups that drove the number, and when, instead of leaving a count without a source. Naming someone only says who posted. It never claims intent or coordination.
* **Key facts stand out.** Headline numbers are in bold with their units, and each point starts with a small icon such as 📌 what happened, 👥 who drives it, 💬 what people say, 📈 rising, 📉 falling, ⚠️ risk, 💡 an opening, 🗺️ where, or ✅ the next step. A real post is quoted with its platform and link.
* **Charts over tables.** When an answer compares several things, shows change over time or splits a whole into shares, AskFRND draws a chart from the numbers the tool returned.

For a longer answer, ask it to **explain**, give a **report** or **go deeper**. The first answer is kept short on purpose, and breakdowns by platform or period wait until you ask for them.

### Dates and windows

Radar tabs have their own time windows, and AskFRND keeps them apart:

* **Overview** is a live page that mixes windows: Listening is the last 24 hours, trends and changes 7 days, and Industry keeps its own windows. For a specific period, AskFRND goes to Listening, Trends history or Industry with the dates you gave.
* **Explore** uses a window of **24h**, **7d** or **30d**, or exact dates of up to 30 days in Jakarta time.
* **Trends and Industry** history use exact dates of up to 30 days. Missing snapshots are unknown, not zero.
* **Prediction** reads stored forecasts. It never recomputes one.

If something is still being built or isn't ready, AskFRND says so instead of guessing. For example, a map that is still building, or an analysis that hasn't been written yet.

## Maps in a Reply

AskFRND can draw a map right inside its answer.

### The Explore map

When you ask who is in a conversation, how accounts are linked or which communities formed, AskFRND can show the **live Explore map** for the brand or one of its topics, over a window of 24h, 7d or 30d.

* It is the same map as on the Explore tab, read live under your own access, so the numbers match the tab.
* You can change the lens, click a community or an account to see who drives it, zoom, and open it full screen. **Esc** clears your pick, and leaves full screen when nothing is picked.
* The card is headed **Who drove the conversation** unless AskFRND gives it another title.
* If the map is still being built, it reads **Building this window's map. It appears as soon as the first…** If you can't open the brand's Explore, it says **You don't have access to this brand's Explore map.** If there is no map for that scope, it says so, and if it can't load, it says **Couldn't load the Explore map. Your Listening topics are unaffected.**
* **Open in Radar →** opens the same view on the Explore tab.
* On a shared page, where there is no signed-in reader, the card is only a link into Radar.

### The Indonesia map

When you ask where a conversation is happening, AskFRND can draw a map of Indonesia from the province counts it read, for example mentions by province.

* The map glows by each province's share of the loudest one. A ranking shows the top provinces, with **Show all** for the rest, and you can hover or click a province, zoom and pan, and open full screen.
* AskFRND names what a number counts (mentions, conversations or posts) and the period it covers (such as yesterday or the last 7 days).
* Mentions that can't be placed on a province are counted separately and kept off the map. **No location** shows how many, and a quiet province is not zero. Mentions from outside Indonesia are counted as **Outside Indonesia**.
* A province name it can't place is left off the map and counted into **No location**, rather than put somewhere it isn't.

### Charts, and asking more

Charts AskFRND draws are clickable. Click a bar, a slice or a legend item to ask about it, and AskFRND answers with that item in mind. This works on a chart that has finished drawing. While a reply is still streaming the click is disabled, so you can't open two replies at once.

## FAQ

**Q: I don't see the Radar tools in Chat Settings.**
They only show where the matching Radar tab exists for you. If the tab is hidden or not available to your workspace, so is its tool.

**Q: AskFRND says it can't answer about Radar.**
Check that the tool is on, and that your role can read that tab. Radar tools are off by default.

**Q: Why is the answer so short?**
Radar answers are short by default. Ask it to explain or go deeper for more.

**Q: Why did it name accounts or outlets?**
A number without a source is incomplete, so Radar answers name who drove it. Naming only says who posted, never why.

**Q: Can I change the date range?**
Overview mixes windows and can't be pinned to a date. Explore, Trends history and Industry accept exact dates, up to 30 days.
