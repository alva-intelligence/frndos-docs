# Radar Industry

**Industry** is the Radar tab for what is happening around your brand: what your competitors launched, what regulators changed, and the news of your industry. It reads news for the industry set in your brand's settings and summarises it each morning.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ You are working on a **brand** whose **industry** is set in its settings. If it isn't, or it's one Radar doesn't read yet, the tab says so (see [When the Tab Is Empty](#when-the-tab-is-empty)).
2. ✅ Your role can read **Industry** for that brand. To change the settings you also need edit access to the brand.
3. ✅ Your brand has **competitors** in Brand IQ. They drive Competitor moves and the briefing.

Open it from **Radar**, then the **Industry** tab. See [Radar Overview](./overview).

## The Header

The page title is followed by the industry and how fresh the page is, for example **Skincare · FMCG (from Brand settings)** and **Updated 20m ago**.

**Settings** opens [Industry settings](#industry-settings). The **Show** menu beside it (**All sources**, **Local**, **International**) is greyed out for now and shows a **Coming soon** tooltip.

## The Daily Briefing

At the top, a short **Daily industry briefing** tells you what matters most in your industry today. It is written each morning and says what it was based on: how many articles from how many sources, and over which window. On a brand's first day it reads **Your first briefing arrives tomorrow at 07:00** and lists what is being collected (your competitors, the number of news sources, and that the first stories arrive in about an hour). If the briefing isn't ready yet, the page says so and the stories below stay live.

## Front Page

**Front page** shows the **Top stories this week**: up to five that matter for your brand, headed **Five stories that matter for your brand**. Hover a story to see what it means for you, and click it to open it. Until a few days of news have come in it reads **No stories yet**.

## Competitor Moves

**Competitor moves** covers the **Last 7 days**. It plots each competitor's moves on a seven-day timeline (**All moves**), coloured by type: **Campaign**, **Product launch**, **Press release** and **News**.

* Hover a dot to preview it, click it to open it.
* Click a competitor in the **Your competitors** card to **spotlight** that brand across the whole page. Click it again to clear.
* **Live campaigns** lists campaigns found in the news, with **Live** and **Ended** tabs. It is built from news about their campaigns, so it carries no ads or performance numbers.
* With no competitors yet, the section reads **Add your competitors to see their moves**. With competitors but no moves, **No moves spotted yet**.

A line under the section names the competitors it is based on and how many articles and press releases.

## Industry Trends

A card reads **Industry trends are coming**: ingredients, formats and packaging that are rising in your industry. It is not live yet.

## Government and Regulators

**Government & regulators** lists proposals and rule changes that affect your products, for the **last 30 days**, each with a status. If there is nothing new it says so. Regulator stories always count as important.

## All News

**All news** lists everything else from the **Last 7 days**, headed **Everything else happening in your industry**.

* Switch the type with the tabs **All**, **Competitors**, **Regulators** and **Industry**, each with a count.
* Turn on **Only important news** to keep the ones flagged **Important**.
* Rows are paged with **Previous page** and **Next page**. Each row carries a type tag.
* Click a row to open the story.

## The Side Column

On a wide screen two cards stay in view:

* **News this week** shows the total, for example **120 articles from 14 sources**, with a bar per day split into competitors, regulators and industry. Hover a day to read it.
* **Your competitors** lists each competitor with its latest move, or **Watching since today** when nothing has landed. **Edit competitors in Brand IQ** opens Brand IQ. With none, it offers **Add competitors in Brand IQ**.

## Opening a Story

A story opens in a pop-up. The header says what it is and for which brand (for example **Competitor story**, **Industry story**, **Regulator update** or **Competitor campaign**), how many sources, and when it was updated.

From top to bottom it can show:

* **Key facts** and a **Timeline** (**Done**, **Next**, **Later**) for a regulation, or key facts for a campaign
* An **AI summary** of the articles, with **Key points**
* **What it means for** your brand, with action buttons. Each opens a new AskFRND chat about that story
* What to **Prepare** for a regulation, and **Competitors already in** for an industry story
* **What else** the competitor did this week, for a campaign
* **Sources**, each with **Open article** (or **Open document** for an official source), an **Official** tag, or **Taken down** when the original is gone
* **More this week**

The pop-up header also has **Ask FRnD**, **Share story**, and a discussion column where your team can comment (see [Sharing a Story](#sharing-a-story)).

### Hiding an outlet

If you can edit the brand, each source has a **Hide stories from** button. The outlet disappears from Radar's stories and source counts for that brand. A notice names the outlet and says it is hidden for your brand, with **Undo**. A story stays while another outlet still reports it.

## Sharing a Story

**Share story** opens a dialog with two links (see [Sharing Radar](./sharing) for the full picture):

* **Team link** opens the story for anyone on the brand.
* **Public link** is for people outside frndOS, such as a client or agency, with no login needed.

## Industry Settings

**Settings** opens **Industry settings** with three tabs. Changes apply to everyone on the brand's team, and only people who can edit the brand can save them; others see a **Close** button and a note.

| Tab | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Competitors** | Which competitors Radar follows in Competitor moves, Live campaigns and the news. A competitor with no data yet shows **Collecting data**, and its first moves take about 3 days. Competitors and their accounts themselves are edited in **Brand IQ › Competitors**; a website alone is enough (see [Competitors](../brand-setup/brand-iq#competitors)) |
| **Notifications** | Which news counts as a change for the brand: **A competitor launches a campaign**, **A competitor launches a product**, **A competitor press release**, **A new rule, recall or deadline**, and **The daily briefing is ready** (every day at 07:00). The first three also offer **Only important stories**, and the rule row lets you pick statuses (**Proposal**, **New rule**, **In effect**, **Recall**). Everyone who follows the brand gets these. How each person receives them is set in **Workspace settings › Notifications** |
| **Sources** | Which outlets count. **Important stories** sets how many outlets must report a story for it to count as important (**Any source**, **2+ outlets**, **3+ outlets**, **5+ outlets**). **Muted outlets** lists what you hid, and says **No muted outlets** when empty |

A notice reads **Industry settings saved**, or **Could not save Industry settings. Try again.**

## When the Tab Is Empty

* **We don't read news for** your industry **yet**: the industry set in the brand's settings is one Radar doesn't cover. Open **Brand settings** and pick the closest suggested industry.
* **Market news isn't available right now**: the news service couldn't be reached. Your competitors and settings are safe. Click **Try again**.
* **Couldn't load industry news. Please try again.**: the page itself failed to load.

## FAQ

**Q: Why are there no competitor moves?**
Add competitors in **Brand IQ › Competitors**. New ones need about 3 days to show their first moves.

**Q: Where do the stories come from?**
From news about your industry and competitors, plus regulator lists. Each story shows its sources, and you can hide an outlet you don't trust.

**Q: Can I get an alert when something happens?**
Yes. Turn on the rows you care about under **Settings → Notifications**.
