# What the frndOS MCP Server Can Do

**Once your AI app is connected, it gets the whole AskFRND tool surface — not a cut-down copy of it.** The connect dialog sums it up as _"Every AskFRND capability, exposed to your AI: brand knowledge, audiences, KV generation, deck compose, workflows."_

And the parity line spells out the difference: _"Same tools as AskFRND — plus agents, teams, and workflow runs. Same workspace, same billing, same permissions."_

Not connected yet? Start with [Connecting Your AI App](./frndos-mcp-server).

## The Short Version

The connect dialog groups it into three headlines:

| Area | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Brand intelligence** | _"Brand knowledge, directories, creative playbooks."_ |
| **Audience & insights** | _"CI overview, segments, personas, persons."_ |
| **Create & generate** | _"KV files, artifacts, deck compose."_ |

## The Full Capability List

This is the list the approval screen shows you before you connect, under _"Through the MCP server, [your app] will be able to:"_

- ✓ Read brand knowledge, insights, and reports (owned, paid, earned media, signals, overview)
- ✓ Read customer intelligence — segments, insights, and audience personas
- ✓ List, create, and update agents and teams
- ✓ Create and update campaign plans
- ✓ Run workflows and read their results
- ✓ Generate key visuals and compose decks
- ✓ Browse creative playbooks and post-creative analyses
- ✓ Only within workspaces you belong to, with your own permissions

## Area by Area

### Finding your way around

Your AI app always starts by listing the workspaces you belong to, then the brands inside the one you pick. Everything else hangs off those two choices.

### Brand knowledge

One brand's knowledge payload: **DNA, identity and tonality** — the same material behind [Brand IQ](../brand-setup/brand-iq).

### Brand insights

Each Insights subpage is readable on its own:

| Subpage | What comes back |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Cross-channel KPIs plus top trend signals |
| **Owned media (Social Media)** | KPIs, performance, per-platform breakdown, or a post list |
| **Paid media (Ads)** | Overview and performance, or a creative-asset list |
| **Earned media (Influencer)** | Overview KPIs, or a post / influencer list |
| **ATL** | Above-the-line — TV, cinema, radio, DOOH |
| **Signals** | Trend-signal counts and breakdowns, or the signal list |
| **Campaigns** | Totals and status breakdown for the brand's campaigns |

See [Insights Overview](../insights/overview) for the same data in the app.

### Customer intelligence and audiences

The AI-generated customer-intelligence overview, customer segments, aggregated customer insights, plus audience segments, personas, and the analysed-followers table. In the app: [Customer Intelligence Overview](../research/customer-intelligence-overview) and [Audience Personas](../research/audience-personas).

### Agents and teams

List the workspace's enabled agents, create a new one from a name and instructions, update an existing one, and list or create agent teams. An agent created this way is immediately usable in chat and workflows — see [Agents (Beta)](../early-access/agents).

> **Heads-up:** updating an agent needs creator or workspace-admin rights, same as in the app.

### Campaigns

Read a brand's campaigns at every stage — drafts, plans awaiting approval, scheduled and finished — open one in full, save a new **draft** plan, or edit a plan that hasn't launched yet. See [Campaign Planner](../insights/campaign-planner).

> **Heads-up:** a launched campaign plan can't be edited through the server, the same rule as the app.

### Creative

Saved creative playbooks and their patterns, plus completed per-post creative analyses.

### Workflows

List workflows, read what one does, see its run history, **start a run**, then poll its status and collect the final output and artifacts. If a run pauses to ask a question, your AI app can answer it and let the run continue. See [Workflows (Beta)](../early-access/workflows).

### Key Visuals

List the workspace's Key Visual files, read a file's recent generations, and **queue a new render or iteration** — it returns immediately and the result appears as a new version. See [KV Generator](../studio/kv-generator).

### Decks

Read the deck composer's manual (available layouts and plan rules), list decks, open one's slide summary, and **compose a deck** from a slide plan. Composition runs in the background and hands back a deck URL. See [Decks Overview](../decks/overview).

### Artifacts

Save an HTML artifact and get back a shareable viewer URL.

## What Needs a Subscription or Credits

Reading is free of both. Making or changing something is not:

| Requirement | Applies to |
|---|---|
| **Active workspace subscription** | Creating an agent, updating an agent, creating a team, creating an artifact, composing a deck, generating a Key Visual, running a workflow, answering a paused workflow, creating or updating a campaign plan |
| **Spendable credits** (on top of a subscription) | Generating a Key Visual, running a workflow |

Credits are spent from the same pool as the rest of frndOS — see [Managing Credits](../workspace/managing-credits).

## What It Will Never Do

- **Reach outside your membership.** Every call re-checks that you belong to the workspace being used. Brand ids from other workspaces read as **not found**.
- **Exceed your own permissions.** The connection acts as you, with your role.
- **Work without a workspace.** A tool call with no workspace chosen is refused with an instruction to list your workspaces first.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this a smaller set of tools than AskFRND has?**
No. The surface is mirrored one-to-one from AskFRND, with one extra tool for listing your workspaces. It currently covers **47** tools.

**Q: Why does my AI app show a huge list of tools?**
Because the whole surface is offered in one go, so clients that only read the first page of tools still see everything.

**Q: Can I turn individual tools off for the MCP server?**
The per-tool switches in **Chat Settings → Tools** and **myFRND → Tools** are documented in [How AskFrnd Uses Tools](../askfrnd/tools).
