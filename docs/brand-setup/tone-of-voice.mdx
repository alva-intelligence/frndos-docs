# Brand IQ Tone (Tone of Voice)

**Tone** — the pillar formerly called **Tonality** — is the communication layer of [Brand IQ](./brand-iq). It defines **how your brand speaks** — the personality that comes through in words, captions, and creative direction.

On the Brand IQ Overview card it's summarized as: _Voice, personality & words to use or avoid._

> **Naming update:** the tab is labelled **Tone** in Brand IQ. The form heading inside still reads **Brand Tonality** — same pillar, same fields.

## When to Use This

Set up Tone when:
- You've configured [Strategy](./brand-profile) and [Identity](./brand-identity)
- You want AI-generated direction to match your brand's voice
- You need to override tonality for specific campaigns (see FAQ below)

## What's Inside Tone

The Tone tab has six fields:

| Field | What to Include | Example placeholder in the form |
|---|---|---|
| **Brand Archetype** | The archetype your brand plays | — |
| **Target Audience Context** | Who you're speaking to (can be copied from Strategy) | *e.g. Gen Z, Millennials* |
| **Key Phrases** | Lines your brand actually uses | *e.g. 'Stay curious', 'You've got this'* |
| **Voice Tone** | How the voice sounds | *e.g. Confident, Warm, Direct* |
| **Tone Descriptors** | Adjectives describing the writing style | *e.g. Minimalist, Punchy, Poetic* |
| **Forbidden Words** | Words the brand never uses | *e.g. cheap, aggressive* |

> **Tip:** **Target Audience Context** has a copy action that pulls the audience segments straight from your Strategy pillar, so you don't retype them.

## How to Set Up Tone

1. Log in to **frndOS**
2. Open the brand you want to configure
3. Click **Brand IQ** in the brand sidebar, then open the **Tone** tab
4. Fill in each field, then save — or upload a brand guideline in **Sources** and let frndOS extract them (see [Brand IQ Sources](./brand-iq-sources))

### Validate with a Vibe Check

At the bottom of the Tone tab is the **Generate Vibe Check** panel — _Generated from all 6 tonality fields_. It produces a sample post so you can check the voice reads the way you intended before relying on it downstream.

### Example: A Bank Brand

```
Tone Descriptors: Trustworthy, approachable, modern
Dos: Use clear, simple language. Speak as a partner, not an institution.
Don'ts: Avoid financial jargon. Don't be overly formal.
Sample Copy: "Your money, your goals, our partnership."
```

## Overriding Tonality for Specific Campaigns

Sometimes a campaign needs to **break from your usual brand voice** — for example, a Gen-Z campaign that's intentionally experimental and bold.

You don't have to change your brand's global Tonality. Instead, override at the project level:

1. Create a **Project** for the campaign
2. In the **Project Brief**, enter a custom tonality
3. Toggle off the default brand knowledge for that project
4. Generate as usual — the AI will use your campaign-specific tonality

👉 See: [Creating & Managing Projects](../projects/managing-projects)

## How Tonality Affects Your Output

Once configured, Tonality guides:

- **KV Generator** — AI suggestions and concept direction reflect your voice
- **AskFrnd** — creative ideas and copy suggestions stay on-brand
- **All Studio tools** — written guidance is tone-aware

## FAQ

**Q: Can I have different tonalities for different campaigns?**
Yes. Your brand-level Tonality is the default, but every project can override it. This is especially useful for experimental campaigns that intentionally depart from your usual brand voice.

**Q: Can I update Tonality later?**
Yes. Tonality can be edited anytime. Changes apply to future generations only.

**Q: How is Tone different from Strategy?**
- **Strategy** (Brand DNA) = strategic foundation (mission, audience, competitors, positioning)
- **Tone** = how the brand communicates (archetype, voice, key phrases, forbidden words)

Both work together, and both count toward Brand IQ readiness. Strategy defines *who* you are; Tone defines *how* you speak.
