# Brand IQ Sources — Grounding Your Brand

Instead of typing every field by hand, you can hand frndOS a brand guideline, a deck or a website and let it fill the brand's details for you. That's what the **Sources** tab in [Brand IQ](./brand-iq) is for. The tab describes itself as _"What agents search when they write for this brand."_

Sources you add during the [Brand Book setup](./brand-book#setting-up-the-brand-book) are listed here too.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **Write access to Brand IQ for this brand** — without it **Add source** isn't shown and the rows have no actions.
2. ✅ **A file or a website** — a PDF or PPTX up to 50 MB, or a website address.
3. ✅ **Enough text in the file** — scanned or image-only files give frndOS very little to read.

## Step 1 — Open Sources

Open the brand → **Brand IQ** → **Sources** tab.

Each source is one row: its name, **Added by \{name\}** with the date and page count, and either what it filled or its status. The side panel filters the list: **All**, **Files**, **Websites**, **Brand Book slides**.

With no source yet, the tab shows **No sources yet** and an **Add your first source** button.

## Step 2 — Add a Source

Click **Add source**:

| Option | What it takes |
|---|---|
| **Upload file** | PDF or PPTX, up to 50MB. You can pick several at once |
| **Add website** | A website address, for example `lumenoat.com` |

A file of another type, or over 50MB, is refused with a toast naming the file.

You can add several sources in a row. They're read one at a time, in order; a source waiting its turn says **Waiting**.

## Step 3 — Wait for It to Be Read

A source being read says **Reading…** with its progress. When it finishes, it either goes straight into the brand, or waits for you:

| Status | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Ready** | Click **Apply** to add what it found to the brand |
| **Needs mapping** | The file covers more than one brand. Click **Map sections** and say where each section goes |
| **Failed** | It couldn't be read. The row says why; click **Retry** |

### When frndOS isn't sure

A source that found things it wasn't sure about, or that disagree with what the Brand Book already says, shows **N questions**. Click it to answer them: _"Pick what the Brand Book should say."_ Where the file and the book disagree, the question says _"Your Brand Book says one thing, this file says another. Which should stay?"_

## Step 4 — See What a Source Filled

Click a row to open its detail: _"What this source filled, and where it shows in the book."_ Each field it filled is listed under its Brand Book chapter.

From the detail you can **Open file** (or **Open website**), **Read again**, and remove the source.

A source that filled nothing new says _"Filled nothing new. Agents still search it."_ Its passages are still searchable.

## Removing a Source

**Remove source** asks first: _"Removing it stops agents searching this file. Your Brand IQ and pages stay as they are. Use History to roll them back."_

Removing a source doesn't undo what it filled. To take that back, restore an earlier version from the Brand Book's **History** (see [The Brand Book](./brand-book#history-presenting-and-downloading)).

## FAQ

**Q: Which file types can I upload?**
PDF or PPTX, up to 50MB each. Websites need a normal web address.

**Q: Can I keep several sources at once?**
Yes. Add as many as you like; they're read one after another.

**Q: Will a new source overwrite what's already in the Brand Book?**
Not where they disagree. A conflict becomes a question, and you choose which value stays.

**Q: One PDF covers several of our brands.**
Click **Map sections** on that source and assign each section to a brand. Shared rules go to every brand you pick.

**Q: Reading failed. What now?**
Click **Retry**. If it keeps failing, try a text-based version of the file.

**Q: I removed a source but its content is still in the book.**
That's intended. Removing a source only stops agents searching it. Use **History** in the Brand Book to roll back what it filled.
