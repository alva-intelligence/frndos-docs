# The Brand Book

The **Brand Book** is a brand's guideline as frndOS keeps it: pages under four chapters (**Strategy**, **Identity**, **Tone & Voice**, **In use**), in the brand's own colours and fonts. It is built from the brand's data in Brand IQ, so when that data changes, the pages update.

It's the first tab of [Brand IQ](./brand-iq).

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **Brand IQ access for this brand.** Reading needs read access; setting up and editing need write access.
2. ✅ **Something about the brand, if you have it.** Guidelines, an existing brand book or a deck (PDF or PPTX), the brand's website, and logo files. You can also start with nothing and answer questions instead.

## Setting Up the Brand Book

The first time you open the tab, it walks you through setup. The setup takes the whole page, with a stepper at the top: **Add sources**, **Reading**, **Questions**, **Look**. **Finish later** takes you back to Brand Home and keeps your place.

If you can only read Brand IQ, the tab says _"This Brand Book isn't set up yet"_ and that a brand admin can set it up. **Sources** stays open to read in the meantime.

### Step 1 — Add what you have

The page asks you to **Add what you have about \{brand\}**: _"Upload anything that describes the brand. More sources mean fewer questions later."_

* **Files** — drop them on the box or **browse**. Guidelines, brand book, decks; PDF or PPTX, up to 50 MB each.
* **Brand website** — type the address and click **Add**. frndOS reads up to 20 pages: home, about, products.
* **Logo files** — **Upload** SVG or PNG files (primary, mono, icon).

Click **Read N sources** to start. Two other ways forward:

* **No files? Talk it through with Ask FRND** opens AskFRND with an interview: _"Ask FRND asks about ten questions and drafts your first book. Around five minutes."_
* **Skip, start blank** goes straight to the last step.

### Step 2 — Reading

frndOS reads each source and lists what it found as it goes. It takes a minute or two. You can **Keep going in the background** and come back; when it's done, click **Continue**.

#### A file that covers several brands

If one file describes more than one brand, the setup asks where each part belongs: _"This file covers N brands. Tell us where each one belongs."_

Each brand section shows its page range and a picker: **This brand**, another brand in the workspace, **New brand** (if you're allowed to create brands), or **Skip**. Sections of shared rules are added to every brand you pick. After applying, each brand gets a line such as `Halo → Telkomsel Halo · 14 fields · Undo`.

### Step 3 — Questions

Where your sources left gaps, or disagree, the setup asks. Each question offers drafts to choose from, for example **Draft 1 · Draft 2 · Write my own**, with a line saying where the drafts came from. Pick one and click **Use this**, or write your own.

When two sources disagree, the question says _"Your sources disagree. Pick the one that's right."_ **Finish later, pick the look** skips the rest of the questions.

### Step 4 — Choose how your book looks

_"Your own pages, in your colours and fonts. Everything stays editable after, like any deck."_ Pick an **Accent** from the brand's colours and a font pairing, and preview the cover and a couple of pages. If the brand has no colours yet, the book starts in the default look.

The step shows how complete the book is (for example `28 of 34 fields ready · 6 can be answered later`). Click **Create Brand Book** and the book opens on its cover.

## Reading the Brand Book

The book is every page stacked in order, with the **Chapters** rail in the side panel following where you are. Use **Filter pages** to find a page by name.

### Editing the brand's values in place

The words and values on a page come from Brand IQ. If you can edit, hover a value to see its outline and click it to change it in a small editor. Saving changes the value in Brand IQ, and the change shows everywhere that value is used.

Empty spots invite you to fill them:

* **Draft from sources** drafts that field from what frndOS has read. A page drafted in place shows _"Drafted from N sources"_ with **Keep**, **Undo** and **Refine with Ask FRND**.
* **Write my own** lets you type it.
* A picture page offers **Upload images**.

Some pages also carry **Change colours**, **Change fonts** or **Change layout**, which open **Edit book** on the right section.

### How complete it is

The chip at the top right shows how much of the book is filled in, for example `31 of 34 · 3 gaps`, or **Brand IQ complete**. Click it to see:

* the overall percentage and a count per chapter
* **Still missing**: each gap with **Answer** (or **Upload** for a logo or font file)
* quality notes, such as a font file that should be uploaded
* **Finish with Ask FRND · N questions**, which runs the questions for every gap at once

The questions run full screen over the book. When you're done, a summary says whether the book is complete and which gaps are left for you to fill on their pages. **Back to the book** returns you to where you were.

## Edit Book — Changing the Look and Pages

**Edit book** in the header opens a side panel with two tabs. The book stays visible and updates behind it as you apply changes.

**Style**

* **Colours** — the palette in order (drag to reorder), each colour's hex, RGB, name and role. A contrast check marks each pair as **Reads well**, **Headlines only** or **Too faint**, with **Fix** for the ones that don't pass. **Suggest a palette** reads one **From the logo** or **From an image**. Click **Apply colours**.
* **Fonts** — pick the heading and body fonts, then **Apply fonts**.
* **Type scale** — **Compact**, **Balanced** or **Expressive**, and optionally a weight for the headline and body fonts. Click **Apply type**.

**Pages**

Every page under its chapter. Show or hide a page, drag pages within a chapter, drag whole chapters (the Cover stays first), switch a page's layout, and add a page. Click **Apply pages**.

Each Apply is saved as one version. A toast confirms it (for example **Colours changed**) with **Undo**, and the change is listed in **History**.

> **Edit design** in the header opens the book in the deck editor, in a new tab, for changes beyond what Edit book covers.

## Status — Draft, In Review, Approved

The status menu in the header moves the book between:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Still being written |
| **In review** | Send to the client |
| **Approved** | Signed off |

An approved book shows a banner such as _"Approved by Rina · Sep 28"_. Only a brand admin can change an approved Brand Book; anyone else sees it read-only. Moving the book back to **Draft** opens it for editing again.

## History, Presenting and Downloading

* **Present** shows the book full screen as slides.
* The **…** menu holds **History**, **Download PDF** and **Download PowerPoint**.

**History** lists Brand IQ changes, newest first, with where each came from (an edit, a source, a chat, an agent). Pick an earlier version and click **Restore**; Brand IQ and the pages follow it. You can also open the history of one field: it's filtered to that field's changes.

## Editing the Brand From AskFRND

AskFRND can change a brand's details for you in chat. Ask it, for example, to update the tagline or add a colour, and it makes the change straight away. The reply carries a card, **Brand IQ updated · N fields**, with **Show in Brand Book** and **Undo**. The change is also listed in **History**.

AskFRND can only do this for a brand you're allowed to edit, and frndOS checks your access on every change. It's on by default for those brands. See [How AskFrnd Uses Tools](../askfrnd/tools) for how tool switches work.

## FAQ

**Q: I don't have any files. Can I still set up a Brand Book?**
Yes. Choose **No files? Talk it through with Ask FRND** and answer about ten questions, or **Skip, start blank** and fill the pages in yourself.

**Q: Our guideline covers several sub-brands in one PDF.**
Upload it once. The setup detects the brands in it and asks which brand each part belongs to.

**Q: Where do I edit the brand's strategy and tone now?**
On the Brand Book pages. Click a value to edit it, or use **Answer** in the completeness chip for an empty one.

**Q: I can't edit an approved Brand Book.**
Only a brand admin can. Ask one to move it back to **Draft**, or to make the change.

**Q: AskFRND changed something I didn't want.**
Click **Undo** on its **Brand IQ updated** card, or restore an earlier version in **History**.

**Q: Will editing the Brand Book change decks that use brand data?**
Yes. A value edited here is the brand's value, so anything that shows it from Brand IQ picks up the change.
