# Business Priorities

The **Business** tab in [Brand IQ](./brand-iq) holds a brand's business priorities: what the brand is trying to move this period, which KPI measures it, and where that KPI stands. AskFRND and agents read approved priorities, and [Proposal Scorecard](../research/proposal-scorecard) scores proposals against them.

Priorities don't count towards the Brand Book's completeness.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **Brand IQ read access** to see the tab
2. ✅ **Brand IQ write access** (or brand settings write access) to add, edit and approve priorities

## The Tab at a Glance

The side panel lists **Periods** (for example a fiscal year) and a **Show** filter:

| Filter | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every priority in the period |
| **Needs attention** | Priorities missing a KPI, a baseline or a target, or whose latest result has gone stale |
| **Drafts** | Priorities not approved yet |
| **Approved** | Approved priorities |

Each priority is a ranked row with its title, its KPI, and **Baseline**, **Current** and **Target** values. A current value says how old it is (for example `as of Sep 1 · 30 days old`) and turns amber when it's stale. Use the arrows on a row to change its rank.

## Adding the First Priorities

A brand with no priorities shows faded example rows and asks _"What does \{brand\} need to achieve this year?"_: _"Add up to three business priorities and link each to a KPI. Ask FRND and the review agent will check every proposal against them."_

* **Add first priority** opens the priority form
* **Copy request for client** copies a request you can paste to the client as is, asking them for their priorities

"Up to three" is a guide; you can add more.

If you can only read the tab, it says **No business priorities yet** and that a Brand IQ editor can add them. Until then, AskFRND reviews proposals against the brand's values only.

## A Priority's Details

Click a row (or create one) to open its form:

| Field | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **Priority** | What the brand is trying to move, e.g. _Grow ARPU without a price war_ |
| **Why it matters (optional)** | One or two lines agents can quote |
| **KPI** | Pick the KPI from the list, or **Type my own…** and give it a **KPI name** |
| **Period** | **Period start** and **Period end** |
| **Source** | Where it came from, e.g. _Aji · FY2026 planning_ (separate several with ·) |

A KPI typed in rather than picked from the list isn't linked, and the row says so.

### Recording results

**Baseline** and **Current** come from the results you record. Open the priority and use **Record a new actual**: a **Value**, an **As of** date and an optional source, then **Record actual**. Within the priority's period, the earliest reading is the baseline and the latest is the current value. A current value older than 60 days is stale. You can remove a reading, and both values recalculate.

### Approving

Approval is per priority, from its form: **Approve**, or **Move to draft** to take it back. There is no bulk approve.

> **Heads-up:** changing what an approved priority says (its title, why it matters, KPI, period or source) puts it back to draft. Reordering doesn't. The toast says _"It went back to draft — approve it again."_

## Starting a New Period

Pick **New period…** in the side panel. frndOS offers the year after the newest one, or choose **Custom dates** for a fiscal year that isn't the calendar year.

A new period that has priorities in an earlier one offers two ways to start:

* **Carry over as drafts** — copies titles and KPI links. Never numbers, never approval.
* **Start fresh** — an empty period

## FAQ

**Q: Do priorities affect the Brand Book's completeness?**
No. The Business tab is counted separately.

**Q: Who uses these priorities?**
AskFRND, agents and the Proposal Scorecard reviewer. Only **approved** priorities can be picked in a scorecard.

**Q: Why did my priority go back to draft?**
You changed something substantive after it was approved. Approve it again once it's right.

**Q: Where do targets come from?**
Targets are set in Insights, not in this form. The form shows **Target (from Insights)**, or **Not set** when there isn't one yet.
