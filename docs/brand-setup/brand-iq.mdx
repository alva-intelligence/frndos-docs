# Brand IQ — Your Brand's Knowledge Home

**Brand IQ** is the single surface that holds everything frndOS knows about a brand. It's what makes generated work sound and look like *your* brand instead of a generic one.

The brand's Strategy, Identity and Tone now live in a **Brand Book**: an agency-style brand guideline, laid out as pages, that updates when the brand data changes. See [The Brand Book](./brand-book).

> **What changed:** Brand IQ used to open on six tabs (Overview, Strategy, Identity, Tone, Sources, Templates). Strategy, Identity and Tone are now chapters of the Brand Book, and the Overview tab became the completeness chip in the header. Old links to those tabs still work and open the matching chapter.

## Where to Find It

Open **Brands** at the bottom of the sidebar and click a brand: it opens on Brand IQ. Or click the brand name in the page title and pick **Brand IQ**. The Brands page shows each brand's completeness, for example `21 of 26 · 5 gaps`, the same figure as the Brand Book's header chip.

If your role doesn't include Brand IQ access, **Brand IQ** isn't in the switcher menu, and opening the page sends you to the brand's first module you can open. See [Inviting Team Members](../workspace/inviting-team-members) for how per-brand roles are assigned.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **A brand exists.** Brand IQ is per brand. See [Managing Multiple Brands](./multiple-brands).
2. ✅ **You can read Brand IQ for this brand.** Otherwise the page sends you to a module you can open.
3. ✅ **You can write to it** to edit. Without write access the book and its sources are read-only.

## The Four Tabs

The tabs sit at the top of the page. A side panel on the left changes with the tab.

| Tab | What it's for | Side panel |
|---|---|---|
| **Brand Book** | The brand guideline itself: set it up the first time, then read and edit it | **Chapters**, with a **Filter pages** search |
| **Sources** | The files and websites the brand's knowledge came from (see [Brand IQ Sources](./brand-iq-sources)) | **Show**: **All**, **Files**, **Websites**, **Brand Book slides** |
| **Templates** | Reusable deck templates for this brand (see [Brand Deck Templates](./deck-templates)) | Template count |
| **Business** | The brand's business priorities and KPI results (see [Business Priorities](./business-priorities)) | **Periods** and a **Show** filter |

Until the Brand Book has been set up, its tab carries a lock and opens on the setup. **Sources** stays open the whole time. Once the Business tab has priorities in the period on screen, its tab reads `Business · 3`.

## The Brand Summary

The **Strategy** chapter opens with a **Summary**: a short description of the brand that frnd writes for you. It's read-only. frnd keeps it up to date as you edit Strategy, and every AI answer, persona and key visual reads it.

A tag beside the heading says where it came from:

* **Written by frnd** — written from the brand's Strategy
* **Drafted from the website** — taken from the brand's website, until Strategy has content. frnd then switches to a Brand IQ summary

If Strategy has no brand story or positioning yet, the Summary says frnd will write one once it does.

With write access to Brand IQ, click **Regenerate** to ask frnd for a new summary. It reads _"frnd is rewriting the summary…"_ while it works, and asks you to check back in a minute if it takes longer.

> **Heads-up:** the brand description is no longer edited in Brand Settings. Change the brand's Strategy instead, and the Summary follows.

## What Brand IQ Feeds

Everything in Brand IQ grounds frndOS's AI work for this brand: AskFRND answers, KV generation, campaign concepts, insight narratives and the proposal reviews in [Proposal Scorecard](../research/proposal-scorecard). Business priorities are also read by AskFRND and agents.

## Prompts You'll See Elsewhere

Brand IQ status surfaces outside the page too:

- **Brands page** — each brand's row shows its Brand IQ completeness with a ring, for example `21 of 26 · 5 gaps`.
- **Getting started** — the sidebar checklist includes **Open a brand's Brand IQ** (see [Navigating frndOS](../getting-started/navigating-frndos#getting-started)).

> **Brand Home is gone.** The brand's Home page, with its Brand IQ banner and chip, has been retired. Opening a brand lands on Brand IQ.

## FAQ

**Q: Where did the Overview, Strategy, Identity and Tone tabs go?**
Strategy, Identity and Tone are now chapters of the Brand Book (Identity's colours and fonts are edited with **Edit book**). The Overview's readiness check is the completeness chip at the top right of the Brand Book. Old links to those tabs open the matching chapter.

**Q: Is Brand IQ the same thing as Brand Knowledge?**
Yes. It's the current name for the same thing, with a dedicated page per brand. You'll still see "Brand Knowledge" in a few places, such as the AskFRND tool of that name.

**Q: My old bookmark to brand settings still works. Is that expected?**
Yes. The older settings routes for DNA, Identity, Tonality and Knowledge Vault are kept so existing deep links resolve. The Brand Book links to the Identity settings when a logo or font file needs uploading.

**Q: Can I edit the brand Summary myself?**
Not directly. It's written from the brand's Strategy, so edit Strategy and frnd updates it. To get a fresh one now, click **Regenerate**.

**Q: Do I need everything filled before generating?**
No, but partial knowledge means partial grounding. The completeness chip and the Brands page both show the gaps because output quality depends on them.

**Q: Someone on my team can't open Brand IQ.**
Their brand role doesn't include it. A workspace admin can assign a role with Brand IQ access. See [Inviting Team Members](../workspace/inviting-team-members).
