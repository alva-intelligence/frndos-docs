# Brand IQ — Your Brand's Knowledge Home

**Brand IQ** is the single surface that holds everything frndOS knows about a brand. It's what makes generated work sound and look like *your* brand instead of a generic one.

> **Naming update:** what used to be called **Brand Knowledge** — and lived scattered across brand settings — is now **Brand IQ**, with its own top-level page per brand. Old links to the individual settings pages still resolve, but they no longer appear in the Settings sidebar.

## Where to Find It

Open a brand, then click the **Brand IQ** card in the sidebar. The card shows live status under its name:

| Card status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ready · 3 pillars** | All three pillars complete (green dot) |
| **N of 3 pillars set** | Partly configured (amber dot) |
| **Not set up yet** | No pillar configured (amber dot) |
| **Not available** | Brand IQ isn't available for this brand |
| **Checking…** | Status still loading |

If your role doesn't include Brand IQ access, the card is greyed out with the tooltip _You don't have access to Brand IQ_, and opening the page sends you back to Brand Home. See [Inviting Team Members](../workspace/inviting-team-members) for how per-brand roles are assigned.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **A brand exists** — Brand IQ is per brand. See [Managing Multiple Brands](./multiple-brands).
2. ✅ **You can read Brand IQ for this brand** — otherwise the page redirects to Brand Home.
3. ✅ **You can write to it** to edit — without write access the pillar editors are read-only and the **Add source** button is disabled.

## The Six Tabs

The page header reads **Brand IQ**, with a tab bar underneath. Every tab is deep-linkable via `?section=`.

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Readiness at a glance — pillar status, knowledge chunks, what Brand IQ powers |
| **Strategy** | The strategic foundation (formerly **Brand DNA**) |
| **Identity** | Visual rules — colors, typography, visual do's and don'ts |
| **Tone** | Voice and personality (formerly **Brand Tonality**) |
| **Sources** | Reference material that grounds the AI — see [Brand IQ Sources](./brand-iq-sources) |
| **Templates** | Reusable deck templates for this brand |

> **Only the three pillar tabs save.** Strategy, Identity and Tone share a save bar that appears at the bottom once you have unsaved changes. Overview, Sources and Templates have no save action.

## Reading the Overview Tab

### Status banner

At the top, a dot plus a headline:

- **Brand IQ ready** (green) — _Every summary, KV and answer speaks in \{brand\}'s voice — grounded across all three pillars._
- **N of 3 pillars set** (amber) — _Complete each pillar so every summary, KV and answer speaks in \{brand\}'s voice._

When reference material has been processed, the banner also shows a count of **knowledge chunks** on the right.

### Pillar cards

Three cards, one per pillar:

| Card | What's inside (as shown on the card) | Tab it opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Strategy** | Positioning, single-minded proposition & benefit ladder. | Strategy |
| **Identity** | Logo, colors, typography & visual do's and don'ts. | Identity |
| **Tone** | Voice, personality & words to use or avoid. | Tone |

Each card carries a badge — **Ready** when complete, otherwise **N of M** or **Not set** — and lists up to three of its own checklist items with a tick or a dot. The button reads **Review** when the pillar is ready, or **Complete \{pillar\}** when it isn't. With read-only access, no button is shown.

### Knowledge Vault card

Below the pillars sits the **Knowledge Vault** card. It reads _\{N\} knowledge chunks grounding every answer._ once material has been processed, or _Reference material grounding every answer in \{brand\}'s facts._ when it hasn't. **Manage sources** takes you to the Sources tab.

### What Brand IQ powers

The bottom strip is labelled **Brand IQ powers** and lists exactly what a complete Brand IQ feeds:

- Daily brief
- KV generation
- Chat answers
- Campaign concepts
- Insight narratives

## The Three Pillars

Each pillar is a form with its own fields. Edit, then use the save bar.

| Tab | Fields |
|---|---|
| **Strategy** | Mission, Vision, Target Audience, Competitors, Market Positioning, Brand Purpose, Brand Pillars |
| **Identity** | Color palettes, typography (including uploaded fonts), visual do's and visual don'ts |
| **Tone** | Brand Archetype, Target Audience Context, Key Phrases, Voice Tone, Tone Descriptors, Forbidden Words |

Detailed field-by-field guidance lives in the pillar guides:

- 👉 [Strategy (Brand DNA)](./brand-profile)
- 👉 [Identity](./brand-identity)
- 👉 [Tone of Voice](./tone-of-voice)

> **Tone has a Vibe Check.** The Tone tab ends with a **Generate Vibe Check** panel — _Generated from all 6 tonality fields_ — that produces a sample post so you can sanity-check the voice before relying on it.

## Prompts You'll See Elsewhere

Brand IQ status surfaces outside the page too:

- **Brand Home banner** — when a pillar is missing you get a **Brand IQ** banner reading **Finish setting up \{brand\}**, with a **Complete Brand IQ** button that deep-links to the first incomplete pillar. Without write access the banner reads _\{brand\}'s Brand IQ is incomplete_ with a **Remind Admin** button instead.
- **Brand Home chip** — once ready, a quiet row: **Brand IQ ready** · grounded across 3 pillars, with **Manage →**.
- **Studio** — an incomplete brand shows as **Brand IQ Incomplete** / **Brand IQ — Not configured** before you generate.

## Templates

The **Templates** tab is described as _Reusable deck templates that keep this brand's decks on brand._ Click **New template** to create one (it opens as **Untitled template** in the deck editor). With none yet, the tab shows **No templates yet** — _Create one to keep your team on brand._ Click any card to open it.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Brand IQ the same thing as Brand Knowledge?**
Yes — it's the current name for the same thing, now with a dedicated page per brand. You'll still see "Brand Knowledge" in a few places, such as the AskFRND tool of that name.

**Q: What happened to Brand DNA and Tonality?**
They're still there, renamed in this surface: **Brand DNA → Strategy**, **Tonality → Tone**. The forms and fields are unchanged; the older headings still appear inside some flows.

**Q: My old bookmark to brand settings still works — is that expected?**
Yes. The old settings routes for DNA, Identity, Tonality and Knowledge Vault are deliberately kept so existing deep links resolve. They're just no longer listed in the Settings sidebar — Brand IQ is the place to go.

**Q: Do I need all three pillars before generating?**
No, but partial setup means partial grounding. The Overview banner and the Studio label both flag an incomplete brand precisely because output quality depends on it.

**Q: What counts as "ready"?**
The brand reaches **ready** once its knowledge is in place across the pillars; short of that you'll see **N of 3 pillars set**, or **Not set up yet** at zero.

**Q: Someone on my team can't open Brand IQ.**
Their brand role doesn't include it. A workspace admin can assign a role with Brand IQ access — see [Inviting Team Members](../workspace/inviting-team-members).
