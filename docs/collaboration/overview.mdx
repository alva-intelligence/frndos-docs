## Comments — Where to Find Them

Commenting is **pinned to the thing you're talking about**: a chart, a table row, a persona card, a slide element, a board on the canvas. Because it belongs to whatever tool you're in, each guide covers it where you'd actually use it:

## What's the Same Everywhere

However different the surfaces look, commenting behaves the same way in all of them:

- **Arm the mode** — the **💬 speech bubble** in the top-right actions, or the **C** key. In the KV Generator it's the **Comment** tool in the bottom toolbar instead.
- **Aim and click** — your cursor becomes a pin; click the thing you mean and write beside it.
- **Escape steps out one level at a time** — composer, then open thread, then the mode.
- **The dock** on the right holds every thread: search, **All / Open / Done / @me** filters, filtering by author or "has image", and an eye to hide the pins.
- **Per comment** — **@mention**, **reply**, **react** (👍 ❤️ 🎉 😄 😢 👀), **attach an image**, **edit** or **delete** your own, **resolve** or reopen.
- **The count on the button** is **unresolved threads**, not comments — a long discussion is still one piece of feedback. A small orange dot means an open thread mentions you.
- **Mentions are scoped** — you can only suggest people who can actually open what you're commenting on.
- **Nothing is lost when its target goes** — a thread whose row, slide, or board isn't there any more is marked and stays readable.

> **One right-hand column:** the comment dock and the AskFrnd panel share it. Opening one closes the other.

> **Desktop only:** commenting is an aim-and-click interaction, so it isn't offered on the phone layout.

> **Rolled out per tool:** each surface is switched on separately. Having comments in Insights doesn't mean you have them on decks yet, and vice versa.

## Resolving Doesn't Delete

Resolving a thread moves it out of **Open** — it stays under **All** and **Done**, with its replies intact. A resolved thread is the record of a decision, so nothing is thrown away.

> **If a thread you just resolved seems to vanish, it hasn't.** Switch the dock's filter to **All** or **Done**. (A bug that really did make resolved threads disappear from the list has been fixed — as has one that moved pins onto the wrong item.)

A link into a thread — from a mention email or a copied comment link — opens the thread itself, on the right item, whether or not it's been resolved.

## Seeing Where Feedback Is Waiting

File thumbnails carry a small blue **comment badge** with a count of **unresolved threads** — in the Library, in **Continue working**, and on recent-work cards — so you can spot files that need an answer without opening them. Deck slide thumbnails carry the same count per slide.

> **Not an inbox:** the badge means "this has open feedback", not "you have unread feedback". There's no per-person read state.

## Getting Notified

Mentions are what drive notifications, and a notification link opens **straight into the thread**. How and where you're alerted is personal and per workspace — see [Notification Settings](../settings/notifications).
