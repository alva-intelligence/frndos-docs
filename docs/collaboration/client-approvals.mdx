# Client Approvals

**Client approvals** let you send a deck, a campaign plan, or a set of KV artboards to the client for a decision with one link. The client opens it on a decision page, leaves notes, and approves or asks for changes. They don't need a frndOS account. You see their decision back in frndOS.

Decks and campaign plans are covered first. Key visuals work a little differently, because one request carries many artboards: see [Approving Key Visual Artboards](#approving-key-visual-artboards).

> **Rolling out:** features may reach workspaces gradually. If **Request approval** isn't in your share options, refresh the page.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **A deck, a saved campaign plan, or KV artboards marked ready to share** to send
2. ✅ **The client's email addresses**, if you want named approvers (optional)

## Step 1 — Request Approval

Open the item's share options:

| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| **Deck** | **Share** in the deck's top bar → **Request approval** (_"Freeze this version and send it to the client to decide."_) → **Request** |
| **Campaign plan** | The plan's **Share** → **Request approval** (_"Freeze this plan and send it to the client to decide."_) → **Request** |

Requesting approval freezes the current version. The client decides on exactly what you sent, even if you keep editing.

## Step 2 — Choose Approvers and Say What Changed

**Approvers · optional**

Type an email and press **Enter** to add it. Brand members appear as suggestions underneath. You can name up to 10 approvers per round.

* **No names** — _"anyone with the link can decide."_
* **Named approvers** — each gets a personal link by email. One approval from anyone named completes the round.

Someone who is already a member of your workspace can't be added here; they approve from inside frndOS.

**What changed · optional**

Up to 500 characters telling the client what's new in this round, for example _"Smaller logo, price tag moved per your v2 note."_

Click **Request approval**.

## Step 3 — Send the Link

The panel confirms the round is out for approval (for a deck, `v2 is out for approval`) and gives you the link three ways: **Copy link**, **WhatsApp** and **Email**. Each named approver is listed with **personal link sent** (or **mail not sent**).

_"Anyone who opens the link decides under their own name. Named approvers already have their personal link by email."_ Click **Done**.

> **Link limit reached?** An item can only have so many share links. Revoke one on the item and try again.

## What the Client Sees

The link opens a decision page showing the deck (or plan), which round it is, who has been asked, and when the link expires.

**Leaving notes**

The client can tap a spot on the deck to pin a note there (_"Your note on this spot"_). Pins are numbered and listed under the deck, and they're kept with the version they were left on.

**Deciding**

A bar at the bottom offers **Approve v2**, **Request changes**, and **I need more time**:

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Approve** | Approves this version |
| **Request changes** → **Small — approve with these changes** | _"Counts as approved."_ Your team applies the note, with no new round |
| **Request changes** → **Needs another look — send me v3** | _"Stays pending. You'll be asked again on v3."_ |
| **I need more time** | Picks a date (**Tomorrow**, **In 3 days**, or any date up to 14 days out). _"The team hears when to expect you. Nothing is decided yet."_ |

The first time someone decides on a device, the page asks **Who is deciding?** for a name, so your team knows who said it.

> **A campaign plan needs one extra step.** A decision on a plan moves budget, so before it counts the page sends the client a one-tap link by email (**Send me a one-tap link**). Tapping it on the same device records the decision. The link works once and expires in 3 days. Just looking at the plan never needs this.

Once the round is closed, the page shows what happened, for example _"Approved by Dina on v3"_. A link that's been revoked or has expired shows _"This link is no longer active."_

## Following the Decision in frndOS

The item's share options show the round's status: **Approval · round N**, with **Pending**, **Approved**, **Approved with changes**, **Changes requested** or **Withdrawn**, each approver's answer, and the decisions with their notes.

* **Resend** re-mails the round's named approvers their personal link (**Reminder sent**). A round with no named approvers has no one to remind
* **Withdraw** closes the round, after you confirm: _"The link stays open for comments; nobody can decide on it."_
* Request another round when the next version is ready

When the client decides, the person who requested approval gets an email, and brand members get a notification. On a campaign plan, every round and its decisions are listed under **Decisions** on the plan.

## Approving Key Visual Artboards

A KV file usually holds many pieces: Sketch and Craft boards, resize tiles, motion clips. An **approval request** sends a whole set of them in one link, and each artboard gets its own decision.

### Step 1 — Mark What's Ready

On the KV canvas, mark the boards, tiles or motion exports you want the client to see with **Mark ready to share** (see [Sharing a Key Visual With a Client](../studio/sharing-with-clients#step-1--mark-whats-ready)).

While something is marked that no request holds yet, the canvas shows a **Ready for client** bar with the marked artboards and **Request approval**. The top bar shows **Ready for client** with a count too.

### Step 2 — Send the Request

Click **Request approval**, from the **Ready for client** bar, the top bar, or **Share** → **Approval requests**. The form has four parts:

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Phases** | **Sketch**, **Craft**, **Resize**, **Motion**, each with a count of what's ready. Turn off a phase to leave it out. Below, the request lists how many artboards it holds |
| **Approvers · optional** | Type an email and press **Enter**. _"No names: anyone with the link can decide. Named approvers get a personal link by email."_ |
| **Message · optional** | Up to 500 characters, prefilled with what's in the request |
| **Decide by · optional** | A deadline for the client |

Artboards already approved, or still waiting in another request, are listed under **Already approved** and **Waiting** and aren't sent again. An artboard that replaces one still waiting in an earlier request is tagged **Replaces Request N**.

Click **Send request**. The panel confirms **Request N is out for approval** and gives you the link to copy or send.

> **Limit:** a request holds up to 50 artboards. Pick fewer phases if you hit it.

### What the Client Sees

The link opens the artboards grouped by phase. Each card shows a finished preview, its state, and two quick buttons: approve, or comment. A request with one artboard opens straight on it.

On an artboard, the client can:

* **Pin a note** by tapping the artwork. The note uses the same comment card your team uses in frndOS
* **Approve** it
* **Request new version**, saying what should change

**Approve all N** approves every artboard that has no decision yet. An artboard the client pinned a note on keeps its own decision, so the button reads, for example, **Approve 7 · 1 has pins**.

### The Email Check

Before a client's first decision on a device, the page asks them to **Confirm it's you**: _"We'll email you a 6-digit code. Looking and pinning never need one — only decisions do."_ They enter the code and their decision goes through.

It's once per device: _"Once per device — your decisions here count for 30 days."_ Pins placed before the check still count.

### Following the Request in frndOS

On the canvas, each artboard carries its client verdict: **Waiting**, **Feedback, no decision yet**, **Approved**, **Approved with fixes**, or **New version asked**, with its pin count.

The top bar shows the open request's status, for example **Waiting on Dina · 0/6**. Click it to see every request and each artboard's decision. Click an artboard row to jump to it on the canvas.

**Share** → **Approval requests** lists every request on the file as **Request N · N artboards**. Open a request to see its link, each artboard with its decisions and pins, and each named approver. A request's **•••** menu has **Copy link**, **Open link**, **Resend** and **Withdraw**.

You get one digest email with the decisions rather than one email per click.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the client need a frndOS account?**
No. The link is all they need.

**Q: I edited the deck after requesting approval. What does the client see?**
The version you sent. Requesting approval freezes it; send a new round for the new version.

**Q: Can I approve on the client's behalf?**
No. Workspace members can't be named approvers, and a decision is recorded under the name of whoever made it.

**Q: The client asked for more time.**
The status shows the date they picked. Nothing is decided until they come back.

**Q: Can I take a request back?**
Yes. **Withdraw** it from the share options. The link stays open for comments, but nobody can decide on it.

**Q: Why does my client have to enter a code for a KV request?**
Decisions on a KV request are confirmed by email once per device. Looking and pinning notes never need the code.

**Q: Does a client have to decide on every artboard at once?**
No. Each artboard has its own decision. **Approve all** only covers the artboards without a decision and without the client's own pins.

**Q: I marked more artboards after sending a request.**
The top bar shows a count of the new ones beside the status. Send another request; artboards already approved or waiting aren't sent twice.
