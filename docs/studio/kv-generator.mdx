# Creating Your First Key Visual (KV Generator)

The **KV Generator** is the flagship tool in Frnd Studio. It takes you from concept to finalized Key Visual — with AI as your creative partner at every step.

## Before You Start

Make sure you've completed these prerequisites:

1. ✅ **Open the right brand** — from Home, click it under **Brands** in the sidebar. Everything you generate inherits this brand's context.
2. ✅ **Brand Knowledge configured** — see [Brand Setup Guide](../brand-setup/brand-profile)
3. ✅ **A Project created** for your campaign — see [Creating & Managing Projects](../projects/managing-projects)

Without these, the AI won't have the context it needs to generate on-brand visuals.

## Step 1 — Open Your Brand and Open KV Generator

1. From Home, click the brand you want to work on under **Brands** in the sidebar (if you're already inside a different brand, click **Back to Home** first)
2. Once inside the brand, open **Studio → KV Generator** from the sidebar

****![](/img/guides-asset/20260618-162254.jpeg)

> **Heads-up:** The brand selected in the sidebar determines which Brand Knowledge applies to your generations. If your output doesn't look on-brand, check that the correct brand is active.

## Step 2 — Configure the Canvas

Choose your canvas settings:

* **Canvas size**: Landscape, Portrait, or Square
* **Canvas mode** (top nav tabs):
  * **Concept** — AI-guided creative exploration: generate multiple concept boards across creative territories, with the Conceptor agent as your partner (see [Concepting Mode](./concepting-mode))
  * **Craft** — refined output, closer to production-ready; use after choosing a concept direction

> **When to use Concept vs. Craft:** Start with Concept when exploring directions. Switch to Craft (or use **Start designing** on a concept board) once you've locked a direction and need to refine it.

## Step 3 — Explore in Concept Mode

Start by generating concept boards with the **Conceptor** agent. Two ways to begin:

**Type a direct prompt**
If you have a specific idea, type it directly into the Conceptor chat.

**Use territory chips**
If you need inspiration, click one of the six territory chips (Product Hero, Human Ritual, Unexpected Subject, Benefit Metaphor, Brand World, Graphic Device). The Conceptor will discuss the direction first (Brainstorm run mode) or immediately generate a concept board (Design run mode).

👉 See: [Concepting Mode — AI-Guided Visual Exploration](./concepting-mode) for a full breakdown of agents, run modes, territories, and style presets.

## Step 4 — Refine in Craft Mode

Once you have a concept you like, right-click the concept board → **Start designing** to promote it to Craft mode. Or click the **Craft** tab in the top nav.

### What you can do in Craft Mode:

**Edit specific areas with Bounding Box**
The **Bounding Box tool** lets you select a specific area of the canvas and request targeted changes. Example: select the background → prompt _"Change to a plastic bottle"_ → AI regenerates only that area. Stack multiple edits for incremental refinement.

**Add image references**
Attach a reference photo to guide character likeness, composition, or specific angles.

> **Example:** To change the camera angle to an extreme low-angle shot, attach a reference image with the desired angle and describe it in your prompt.

**Add static elements**
For elements AI shouldn't generate (footers, logos, brand marks):

1. Use the **Add Image** tool
2. Upload your logo or static element
3. Position it on the canvas

**Add shapes**
You can draw vector shapes directly on the canvas. Click the **Shape** icon (diamond icon) in the center tool palette at the bottom of the canvas to open the shape picker:

**Basic shapes:**
- **Rectangle** (Square icon)
- **Ellipse** (Circle icon)
- **Triangle**
- **Polygon** (Pentagon icon)
- **Star**
- **Diamond**
- **Chevron**

**Lines:**
- **Line** — a straight line segment
- **Arrow** — a line with an arrowhead at one end

After selecting a shape kind, click and drag on the canvas to draw it. The shape is placed as an overlay on the board and can be resized, rotated, recolored, or deleted just like any other overlay.

> **Keyboard shortcut:** Press **S** (Shape) on the canvas to activate the shape tool, then pick a kind from the popover. Press **Esc** to return to the Move tool.

Once placed, edit the shape's fill, stroke, arrowheads, and opacity in the properties panel. See [Overlay Properties](#overlay-properties) below.

**Use Your Own Image (No Generation Needed)**
Craft mode isn't limited to images generated inside KV Generator. If you already have a finished image — from a previous shoot, a stock photo, or any external source — you can upload it directly into Craft and work with it like any other KV:

1. Open Craft mode (without generating first)
2. Choose your artboard size (16:9, 9:16, or 1:1)
3. Use **Upload Image** to bring in your existing file (drag & drop or browse)

Once uploaded, the image behaves like a generated KV:
- **Resize it** across platforms via Resize Mode — see [Resizing Visuals for Different Platforms](./resizer)
- **Refine or edit it** using the Bounding Box tool, image references, or static elements — same as a generated KV

> **Why this matters:** You no longer need to generate something in frndOS just to use Resizer or Craft's editing tools. Bring in any image you already have and let frndOS handle the adaptation and refinement.

**Creativity level**
The **Creativity** control in the bottom bar sets how far the AI may drift from the source image during a polish or variant. Default is Low (faithful). See [Craft Mode → Creativity Level](./craft-mode#creativity-level) for details.

**Transform Panel**
Open the **Layers** panel (Stack icon, bottom nav) to bring up a **Transform** panel in the bottom-right corner. Select a single overlay to edit its exact Position (X/Y), Size (W/H with optional aspect-ratio lock), and Rotation (°) numerically. See [Craft Mode → The Transform Panel](./craft-mode#the-transform-panel) for full details.

### Overlay Properties

Selecting an overlay (text, shape, or image) on a Craft board opens its properties panel. The available controls depend on the overlay type. The panel sits in the bottom-right area of the canvas, replacing the chat panel when an overlay is selected.

#### Fill Controls (Text & Shape Overlays)

Text and shape overlays have a **Fill** section with two modes:

**Solid** — A single flat color:

- Click the color swatch to open the color picker popover, which contains:
  - A grid of preset colors (shared across all KV tools)
  - Recent colors used during this session
  - A saturation/hue picker for custom colors
  - A hex input field for exact color codes
  - An eyedropper tool to sample a color from anywhere on screen
- The color swatch updates live as you pick.

**Gradient** — A smooth transition between two colors:

- **Linear gradient** — Colors blend along a straight line at a configurable angle (0–360°, default 90°).
- **Radial gradient** — Colors radiate outward from a center point.
- Use the **From** and **To** color swatches to set the start and end colors.
- The **Angle** field (linear only) controls the gradient direction. Type a value and press Enter or blur the field to commit.

Toggle between Solid and Gradient using the segmented control at the top of the Fill section.

> **Gradient support:** Only available on **shape** overlays (rectangles, ellipses, diamonds) and **text** overlays — image overlays do not have a fill section.

#### Stroke Controls (Shape Overlays Only)

Shape overlays can have a **Stroke** — an outline drawn along the shape's edge:

- Toggle stroke on/off with the checkbox.
- **Color** — Click the swatch to open the same color picker.
- **Width** — Numeric input for stroke thickness (0–1, normalized).

Arrows and lines also have **Arrowhead** controls:

- **Arrowhead at start / end** — Show an arrowhead on one or both ends.
- **Head size** — Numeric input for arrowhead length.

#### Opacity

Every overlay has an **Opacity** slider (0–100%) in its properties panel, shared by text, shape, and image overlays:

- Drag the slider for a live canvas preview. The overlay's transparency updates in real time.
- Type an exact percentage in the number field beside the slider.
- Press Enter or blur the field to commit the value.
- The slider defaults to **100%** (fully opaque) for new overlays.

#### Text Alignment

When a text overlay is selected, the Text properties section includes an **Alignment** segmented control with four options:

- **Align left** — Text lines up against the left edge of the text box.
- **Align center** — Text is centered horizontally.
- **Align right** — Text lines up against the right edge.
- **Justify** — Text is spaced so each line fills the full width.

The default alignment for imported PSD text layers is **left** (matching the source file). The default for text placed directly on the canvas is **center**, matching common design tool conventions.

#### Custom Fonts

Text overlays can use any font — either one of the built-in fonts or a custom font you upload.

**Built-in fonts:** The canvas comes with six built-in font families:

- Geist (default)
- Neue Montreal
- Space Grotesk
- Archivo
- Funnel Sans
- General Sans

Select a font from the font dropdown in the Text properties section. A preview of each font family is shown in the dropdown menu.

**Uploading custom fonts:**

1. In the font dropdown, select **Upload font** at the bottom of the menu.
2. Choose a `.ttf` or `.otf` file from your computer (max 5 MB).
3. The font is uploaded to your brand's font library and registered on the canvas immediately.
4. The uploaded font appears under the "Brand fonts" group at the top of the dropdown in future sessions.

To **delete** a custom font, click the trash icon beside its name in the dropdown (only fonts uploaded through Studio can be deleted).

> **Font licensing:** Only upload fonts you have the rights to use. Uploaded fonts become available to all brand members across all KV files in the brand.

#### Hide & Lock Overlays

Each overlay in the Layers panel has two toggle controls on the right side of its row:

**Hide** (eye icon) — Toggle an overlay's visibility on the canvas:

- **Eye open** (default) — The overlay is visible on the canvas.
- **Eye closed** — The overlay is hidden from view but still exists in the layer list. It won't render in exports or downloads.
- Use this to temporarily remove an overlay from view without deleting it.

**Lock** (lock icon) — Prevent an overlay from being selected or moved:

- **Unlocked** (open lock, default) — The overlay can be selected, dragged, resized, and edited.
- **Locked** (closed lock) — The overlay cannot be selected on the canvas or edited in the Transform panel. The Transform panel shows "Locked — unlock in the Layers panel to edit." Click the lock icon in the Layers panel to unlock it.

Both states are immediately reflected on the canvas and persist across sessions.

> **Tip:** Use Hide to declutter the canvas while editing specific layers, and Lock to protect elements (like logos or footers) from accidental edits.

### Creating a Variant

A **variant** is an alternate version of an existing Craft board — it inherits the source's aspect ratio and generation context, but takes a new creative direction you describe.

**Two ways to start:**

- **"+" button** — When a Craft board is selected, a **`+`** button appears pinned to the right edge of the board. Click it to open the variant prompt.
- **Context menu** → **Create variant** — Right-click any Craft board and select "Create variant".

**The variant prompt popover:**

A compact popover appears anchored to the right of the source board with the header **"Describe your variant"**. Type any creative direction — examples from the placeholder: _"warmer palette, add the tagline…"_ — then press **Enter** or click **Create variant** to start generation. Press **Escape** or click **Cancel** to dismiss.

You can leave the prompt empty to generate a variant without a specific direction.

**What happens next:**

1. A ghost placeholder appears immediately in the slot adjacent to the source board — same aspect ratio as the source.
2. The Crafter generates the variant in Craft mode, using the source board as context.
3. The finished variant lands in the reserved slot.
4. The variant is auto-named from the prompt direction (e.g. "Warmer palette") or falls back to **Variant N** if the prompt is empty or uninformative.

> **Heads-up:** Variant generation is blocked while another generation is in progress. Wait for the current generation to finish before creating a variant.

> **Extreme aspect ratio warning:** If your source board is wider than 3:1 or taller than 1:3, the Crafter will generate the variant at 3:1 and extend it to fill. You'll see a notice: _"This board is [wider/taller] than the AI's 3:1 limit — the variant will be generated at 3:1 and extended to fill."_

### Viewing Version History

Every generation is saved. To browse previous versions:

1. Open the version history panel
2. Select any past iteration
3. Restore or branch from it

## Board Actions — The Right-Click Menu {#board-context-menu}

Right-clicking any board on the canvas opens a context menu with actions for that board. Right-clicking on an empty part of the canvas (no board) does nothing — the browser's native menu is suppressed.

The menu closes when you click anywhere outside it or press **Escape**.

### On a Concept board

| Action | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Start designing** | Promotes this concept to Craft mode — opens the board as the Craft master |
| **Copy image to clipboard** | Copies the board image to your clipboard (best-effort — see note below) |
| **Copy image link** | Copies a direct URL to the board image |
| **Copy prompt** | Copies the prompt that generated this board (only shown when a prompt exists) |
| **Duplicate** | Adds a copy of the board to the canvas |
| **Rename** | Opens an inline text field to rename the board |
| **Reset position** | Moves the board back to its auto-layout position on the canvas |
| **Remove from canvas** | Removes the artboard from the canvas — see note below |

### On a Craft board

Craft boards have the same Copy, Duplicate, Rename, and Reset actions above, plus:

| Action | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Set as master** | Marks this board as the master (only shown on non-master boards) |
| **Version history…** | Opens the version history panel for this board |
| **Create variant** | Opens the variant prompt popover — see [Creating a Variant](#creating-a-variant) |
| **Send to Motion** | Switches to Motion mode with this board as the source (requires KV Motion enabled in Labs) |
| **Send to Resize** | Switches to Resize mode with this board as the source |

> **"Start designing" is not shown on Craft boards.** It only appears on Concept boards (Sketch mode).

### Notes on specific actions

**Copy image to clipboard**
The image copy is best-effort. If CORS prevents a direct clipboard write, frndOS falls back to copying the image link instead and shows a toast: _"Image copy needs CORS — copied the link instead."_ A direct copy shows _"Image copied to clipboard."_

**Remove from canvas**
This action removes the artboard entry from the canvas. It does **not** delete the generation — the generation stays in your history and can be accessed via Version History. Use this to declutter the canvas without losing work.

## Import PSD Files

If you have an existing Adobe Photoshop design (`.psd`), you can import it directly into KV Generator — no need to redo your layout from scratch. Each import converts visible PSD layers into editable overlays on the canvas.

**How it works:**

All processing happens **on your device** (client-side) using the `ag-psd` library. No PSD data is sent to the server during parsing — only the raster layer images are uploaded afterward as overlay assets.

### Drop a PSD on an empty canvas

When you drop a `.psd` file onto an empty KV Generator canvas:

1. The flattened composite image is uploaded as the first base board.
2. Every visible layer in the PSD becomes an overlay on that board, preserving their positions and stacking order.
3. **Raster layers** (pixel images, photos) are converted to PNG and uploaded as image overlays.
4. **Text layers** are extracted as editable text overlays — content, color, font size, alignment, line height, letter spacing, and opacity are all preserved.

The result is a board where you can continue editing each layer independently — move, resize, recolor, or replace the text just as if you'd created it inside KV Generator.

> **Text layers stay editable.** Even when the PSD font isn't available on your canvas, the text content and style are preserved and remain editable. You'll see a font-missing prompt before import completes (see below).

### Drop a PSD on an existing board

If you already have boards on the canvas, drop a `.psd` directly onto any board. The PSD layers are placed as overlays on that specific board — useful when you want to bring PSD assets into an existing KV.

### What happens during import

1. **Parsing** — The PSD is read entirely on your device. This can take a few seconds for large, highly layered files.
2. **Font matching** — Text layers are matched against your available fonts (built-in fonts + brand fonts). Unmatched fonts are flagged.
3. **Upload** — Raster layers are uploaded to frndOS (4 at a time) as overlay image assets.
4. **Placement** — All layers are placed on the board in their original positions and stacking order.

### Missing fonts dialog

If your PSD uses fonts that aren't available on the canvas, a dialog appears listing the missing font names:

- **Upload fonts** — Select `.ttf` or `.otf` font files (max 5 MB each) from your computer. Uploaded fonts are added to the brand's font library and registered immediately.
- **Use default font** — Continue the import without the missing fonts. The text layers stay fully editable but render in the default font.

The import resumes automatically after you upload fonts or choose to skip.

> **Font licensing:** Only upload fonts you have the rights to use. Uploaded fonts become available to other brand members.

### Overlay limit

A single KV file supports up to **100 overlays**. If the PSD has more layers than the remaining space, the bottom layers win — the extra top layers are skipped and reported in the completion notice.

### File format tips

- Save your PSD with **"Maximize Compatibility"** enabled in Photoshop for the best composite preview.
- Hidden layers are skipped during import.
- Adjustment layers and empty groups (layers with no pixel data or text) are skipped and counted in the notice.
- Composite images larger than 4096 px on the longest edge are downscaled automatically.
- Each raster layer is compressed as PNG (or JPEG fallback) to stay within the 10 MB upload limit.

## Ruler, Grid & Smart Guides

Two alignment overlays and a drag-time smart guide system help you position overlays and boards precisely on the canvas.

### Ruler

Toggle the ruler on/off from the **ruler icon** (`⊥`) in the bottom toolbar, or press **Shift+R** (macOS / Windows) anywhere on the canvas (outside editable text fields).

When enabled, ruler bars appear at the **top** and **left** edges of the viewport:

- **Tick marks** show pixel positions relative to the canvas origin (image pixels).
- **Major ticks** (every 5th division) display a numeric label.
- **Minor ticks** (every division in between) provide finer reference.

The tick step adapts to your current zoom level — zoom in for finer subdivisions, zoom out for coarser ones. The ruler sits above the Konva canvas but is **pointer-events-none**, so it never intercepts clicks or drags.

### Grid

The background grid is controlled from the same bottom toolbar. Click the ruler icon to open the **Grid & Ruler popup**, where you can:

| Control | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Ruler toggle** | Show/hide both ruler bars |
| **Grid opacity** | Slider 0–100%. Default 13%. At 0 the grid is invisible; at 100 it shows full white lines. |
| **Grid size** | Cell size in image pixels. Default 35 px. Smaller values pack cells tighter; larger values space them further apart. |

The grid renders as white CSS lines behind the Konva canvas stage at `z-index: 1`. It uses a coarser step than the ruler ticks so cells are large enough to sight against.

> **Grid opacity and size persist** across page reloads and between files (saved to browser localStorage).

### Smart Drag Guides

When you drag an overlay on a Craft board, **smart guides** appear to help you align it:

**Distance measurements** — orange connector lines from each edge of the dragged overlay to the corresponding edge of its host board, with a pill badge showing the pixel distance:

- Top edge → board top
- Bottom edge → board bottom
- Left edge → board left
- Right edge → board right

**Alignment lines** — red guide lines that appear when the overlay's edge or center aligns with the board's edge/center or a sibling overlay's, within a matching threshold.

The guides are purely visual and never intercept pointer events. They track canvas pan/zoom so they stay accurate regardless of your viewport position.

> **Smart guides only appear while actively dragging an overlay.** They disappear once you release the mouse.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I change the camera angle or perspective of a generated image?**
Currently, angle adjustments are done via prompt + reference image. Describe the desired angle in your prompt and attach a reference image showing it. Example: *"Change camera angle to extreme low-angle view"* + attached reference.

**Q: Can I override the brand tone for a specific KV?**
Yes, but it's done at the **Project level**, not the KV level. In your Project Brief, set a custom tonality and toggle off the default Brand Knowledge. All KVs under that project will use the override.

**Q: What's the difference between Concept and Craft mode?**

* **Concept** = AI-guided ideation, generate multiple concept boards across creative territories, explore directions fast
* **Craft** = refinement mode, more control, production-ready output

👉 See: [Concepting Mode — AI-Guided Visual Exploration](./concepting-mode) for the full Concept mode workflow.

**Q: Can I reuse layouts across multiple KVs?**
Yes — save your layout as a **Template** in Craft Mode. Apply it to future KVs without repeating the setup.

**Q: What if the new generation is worse than the previous one?**
Use the **version history panel** to restore any previous iteration. No work is lost.

**Q: Can I use an image I already have instead of generating one?**
Yes. In Craft mode, use **Upload Image** to bring in any existing image — no generation required. From there you can resize it across platforms or refine it using Craft's editing tools (Bounding Box, image references, static elements).

**Q: What is a variant and how is it different from a duplicate?**
A variant is an AI-generated alternate version of an existing Craft board — it re-runs generation with the source as context and a new direction you describe. A duplicate copies the board as-is (no new generation). Use variants to explore alternatives; use duplicates to preserve a baseline before editing.

**Q: Can I create a variant from a Concept board?**
No. Variants are a Craft-mode feature only. To branch from a Concept board, promote it to Craft first (right-click → **Start designing**), then create variants from the resulting Craft board.

**Q: Can I create multiple variants at once?**
No. Variant generation is blocked while any other generation is in progress — finish the current one before starting the next.
