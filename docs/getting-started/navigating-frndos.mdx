# Navigating frndOS — The Sidebar

frndOS navigation lives in a **left sidebar**. This is where you move between your main areas, open a brand's modules, and start a chat. The brand you're working on is in the page title. This guide covers what's in the sidebar and how to get around.

## The Sidebar

The sidebar stays the same wherever you are. Opening a brand doesn't swap it for a different one: you reach the brand's work through **Modules**, and you switch brands from the page title (see [Switching Brands](#switching-brands)).

From top to bottom:

| Item | What It's For |
|---|---|
| **Search** | Find brands, files, projects and pages, or ask FRnD (**⌘K**). See [Quick Search](#quick-search--cmdk) |
| **Create** | The create menu, beside Search. See [Creating Something New](#creating-something-new) |
| **New chat** | Starts a new AskFRND conversation (**⌘O**) |
| **Home** | Your day at a glance. See [Home](#home) |
| **Tasks** | Your tasks as a list or a board. See [Tasks](../workspace/tasks) |
| **Calendar** | Your meetings, tasks and campaign dates. See [Calendar](../workspace/calendar) |
| **Files** | The files your team has made, scoped to your access. See [Files](../library/overview) |
| **myFRND** | Plugins, skills, agents, and the tools AskFRND may use. See [myFRND](./myfrnd) |
| **Workflows** | The agent workflow builder |
| **Modules** | **Insights**, **Research** and **Audience**, for the brand you're working on. See [Modules](#modules) |
| **Projects** | Your recent projects and [project rooms](../projects/project-rooms); **See all** opens the Projects page |
| **Chats** | Your recent AskFRND conversations. The **+** on its header starts a new one |

At the bottom of the sidebar:

| Item | What It's For |
|---|---|
| **Getting started** | A short checklist for new users (see below). It goes away when you hide it |
| **Brands** | The Brands page: every brand you can open. See [Switching Brands](#switching-brands) |
| **Settings** | Workspace Settings, opening on the first page you're allowed to see |

> **Coming from the brand sidebar?** Opening a brand used to replace the whole sidebar with a brand sidebar, with **Back to Workspace** to return. That swap is gone. Modules are in the main sidebar, and the brand switcher sits in the page title.

### Sidebar indicators

Some rows tell you when something needs you, without opening the page:

| Row | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Tasks** | How many tasks are overdue or due today. The number is red when any are overdue |
| **Calendar** | **now** while a meeting is on, or **in 12m** when the next one starts within the hour |
| **Research** | How many studies are ready to read, for example **2 ready** |

A row shows nothing when there's nothing to say.

### Getting started

The **Getting started** row shows your progress, for example **1 of 4**, and the next step. Click it to open the checklist:

1. **Ask FRnD anything with ⌘K**
2. **Open a brand's Brand IQ**
3. **Create your first KV**
4. **Invite a teammate**

Each step says why it's worth doing and how long it takes, with a button to do it. **Hide this checklist** removes the row.

### Creating Something New

**Create**, beside **Search**, opens a create menu naming the artifact you want:

| Row | Creates |
|---|---|
| **Canvas** | A KV canvas in the [KV Generator](../studio/kv-generator) |
| **Presentation Deck** | A [deck](../decks/overview) |
| **Campaign Report** | A report in Insights |
| **Survey** | A [survey](../research/creating-a-general-survey) |
| **Persona** | A persona in Research |
| **Image Generation** | An AI-generated image |

Rows that need a brand ask you to pick one first.

The **New** button on the Files page opens the same menu, without **Campaign Report** (it isn't a file) and with **Resize**, which opens a new [Resizer](../studio/resizer) canvas. It also offers **Import PowerPoint or PDF**.

### Modules

**Modules** holds the brand work you do every day: **Insights**, **Research** (marked **Beta**) and **Audience**. Each row opens that module for the brand you're working on, on the first tab you can read.

> **Heads-up:** modules are **per brand**. There's no view that works across all brands at once. Switch brands to see another brand's Insights, Research or Audience.

> **A module you can't open still shows.** If your role doesn't grant a module for the current brand, its row is greyed out instead of disappearing. Ask a workspace admin for access if you need it (see [Inviting Team Members](../workspace/inviting-team-members)).

> **No brand yet?** A module row leads to **Add your first brand** until the workspace has one.

> **Where did Studio go?** There's no **Studio** module. Your KVs and resizes are in **Files**, and you open them from there. Start a new one from **Create** (**Canvas**) or Files' **New** button (**Canvas**, **Resize**). Old Studio links open Files.

> **Note:** The sidebar is actively evolving as frndOS grows. Some entries may appear or change as new capabilities roll out. This guide focuses on the stable, everyday navigation you'll use most.

## Home

**Home** is your starting point: a calm, centred page that tells you what today holds and lets you pick up where you left off.

### The Greeting and Your Brief

Home opens with the date, a greeting (_"Good morning, Dina."_), and one line of brief under it: what needs your attention, across the brands you can see. The ask box underneath (_"How can I help you today?"_) lets you start working without going anywhere else. Press **/** to jump into it.

Your brief loads instantly. If a fresher one is being written, the page shows the last one first and then refreshes it quietly. While the brief is being read it says _"Reading your day…"_; if it can't load, it says _"Your brief isn't ready yet."_ with **Try again**.

> **The briefing covers the workspace you're in.** If you belong to more than one workspace, the brief only reads out notifications from the active one. The notification bell still lists every workspace's notifications, each labelled with its workspace (see [Notification Settings](../settings/notifications#reading-a-notification-from-another-workspace)).

In a brand-new workspace, Home greets you instead: _"Welcome. Start with a brief, a brand or a file."_ and a **Set up your day** card offers **Connect your calendar** and **Add your first task**.

### Today

Two cards side by side:

| Card | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Your tasks** | Your open tasks, with how many are overdue. Tick a task to mark it done. **Tasks →** opens [Tasks](../workspace/tasks) |
| **Schedule** | Today's meetings from your connected calendar. **Calendar →** opens [Calendar](../workspace/calendar) |

With nothing due, **Your tasks** reads **Nothing due** and tells you what's next. With no tasks yet, it offers **New task** and **Draft from a brief**, which starts a message in the ask box asking AskFRND to draft tasks from a brief you paste. If your calendar isn't connected, **Schedule** offers to connect it.

### Continue Working

The work you touched most recently: canvases, decks, research, workflows. Click one to pick up where you left off. **Files →** takes you to **Files**.

### Live campaigns

Campaigns that are running right now, each with its run rate and its headline numbers. A number that hasn't reported yet shows a dash.

### Team activity

Who changed what, and when, over the last two weeks. Filter it by person, or click **See all →** for the full feed. It only appears in an organization workspace.

Each section only shows when it has something in it, so a quiet day makes for a short page.

> **Automatic suggestion cards are gone.** Home used to push a grid of AI-generated brand and meeting suggestions at you. Those have been **retired**. The brief and its ask box replace them.

## Switching Brands

The brand you're working on is named in the page title, with its logo, for example **Acme ⌄ / Insights**. Click it to switch:

* **Search brands** finds a brand by name. With nothing typed, the menu lists your **Recent** brands
* **Brand IQ** opens the current brand's [Brand IQ](../brand-setup/brand-iq)
* **Brand settings** opens its settings, if you can manage the brand (see [Brand Settings](../settings/overview#brand-settings))
* **Manage brands** opens the Brands page
* **New brand** creates one, if your role allows it (see [Setting Up Your First Brand](./your-first-brand))

Switching keeps you on the same module and tab. Insights stays Insights, now for the other brand. If the new brand doesn't give you that module, you land on its default page instead.

Switching brands changes your whole context:
- Which projects and files you see
- Which Brand IQ applies
- Which Insights data loads
- Which credit context is active

### The Brands page

**Brands**, at the bottom of the sidebar, lists every brand you can open, with its Brand IQ completeness and your role (**Manager** or **Member**). Search with **Search brands**. Click a brand to open its Brand IQ, or the gear on its row to open its settings (managers only).

Opening a brand lands on its Brand IQ, titled **Brands / Acme ⌄**. The brand's old Home page is gone.

## Opening AskFrnd

AskFrnd opens from the **Ask FRND** button in the bottom-right corner of any page, from **New chat** in the sidebar, or from a chat under **Chats**. It opens as a floating card, docked beside the page, or full screen, and you can switch between them. See [AskFrnd Overview](../askfrnd/overview#where-to-find-askfrnd--and-what-history-youll-see).

The **Chats** list in the sidebar shows your recent chats that aren't tied to a brand. It's the same on every page. **See all** opens your full chat history.

## What's in Each Module

| Module | What It's For | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Insights** | Performance tracking and dashboards. Its tabs are Overview, Campaigns, Business, Ads, Social Media, Influencers, ATL, Dashboard, Trends, Listening ([guide](../insights/overview)) | Available |
| **Research** | Surveys, scorecards and studies ([guide](../research/overview)) | Available, marked **Beta** |
| **Audience** | The brand's people: contacts, followers, segments and personas ([guide](../audience/overview)) | Available |
| **Growth** | Distribution and campaign tools ([guide](../growth/overview)) | Coming soon, not in the sidebar yet |

Which tabs you see inside a module depends on your role and on which features your workspace has switched on, so two people can open the same module and get a different tab bar.

> **Creative tools aren't a module.** The KV Generator and Resizer open from **Files** or **Create**. See [Creative Tools Overview](../studio/overview).

See: [Welcome to frndOS](./welcome) for an overview of the modules.

## Quick Search — Cmd+K

Press **⌘K** anywhere to open the quick search, and jump to something instead of navigating to it.

With nothing typed it shows what you opened recently, so the common case is two keystrokes.

### Narrowing the search

A sigil in the **first** character narrows the search to one kind of thing:

| Type | Searches |
|---|---|
| `@` | **Brands** — jump to a brand |
| `/` | **Files** — search your files |
| `>` | **Commands** — run a command |
| `?` | **Ask FRnD** — ask anything |
| no sigil | Everything, merged and ranked |

> **Only the first character scopes the search.** A `/` or `@` later in what you type is treated as ordinary text, so a file called `kv 3/4` still finds itself.

### Creating from the search box

Typing a create intent offers to make the thing rather than look for it. **New**, **Create** or **Buat** all work, as does just naming the type:

**New Key Visual** · **New Presentation Deck** · **New Campaign Report** · **New Survey** · **New Persona** · **New Image Generation**

If what you're creating needs a brand and you aren't on one, it asks which brand first.

> **You only see what you can actually make.** The list is filtered by your permissions and by what's enabled for the workspace, so it never offers something that would fail on the next screen.

## Dropping a File Anywhere

Drag a file onto any page and drop it. Instead of guessing, frndOS asks _"What should frndOS do with this?"_ and offers up to four actions that fit the file and what you're allowed to do:

| Action | When it's offered |
|---|---|
| **Edit on a canvas** | An image, inside a brand |
| **Start a project** | A document such as a brief |
| **Import as deck** | A PowerPoint or PDF |
| **Add to \{project\}** | Inside a project room |
| **Ask frnd** | Any file; opens AskFRND with the file attached |

Drops on things that take files themselves, such as a chat box, a deck canvas or an upload area, still go there. Folders can't be dropped; drop the files inside instead.

## Appearance — Choosing Light or Dark

You can choose how frndOS looks on your device. Click the workspace name at the top of the sidebar, open **Miscellaneous**, and click **Theme**. The row shows your current theme and a **Beta** tag.

The **Appearance** dialog reads _"Choose how frndOS looks on this device."_ Four options:

| Theme | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **System** | _"Daylight or Midnight, following your device."_ |
| **Daylight** | _"Soft white canvas, dark ink."_ The light theme |
| **Midnight** | _"The classic dark frndOS look."_ |
| **Aurora** | _"Glass panels over a blue horizon."_ |

Each option shows a miniature preview. Select one and the theme applies instantly.

**Per device:** your choice is saved on the device you set it on. It doesn't sync across devices or affect other workspace members.

> **A cleaner look.** frndOS has a cleaner type and colour style across the app, with calmer notices. Midnight is now a softer charcoal, and new users start on **System**.

> The appearance picker is rolling out gradually. If you don't see **Theme** in the menu yet, it will appear once enabled for your workspace.

## FAQ

**Q: Where did the Beta tags on Tasks and Calendar go?**
Those rows now show what needs you instead (see [Sidebar indicators](#sidebar-indicators)). Research keeps its **Beta** tag.

**Q: My Home briefing mentioned a brand that isn't in this workspace.**
That was a bug, now fixed. The briefing only reads notifications from the workspace you're in. Notifications from your other workspaces stay in the bell, labelled with their workspace.

**Q: I still see the old navigation layout. Why?**
The new sidebar navigation is now in production. If you see an older layout, try refreshing or clearing your cache. If it persists, report it through your agency support group.

**Q: How do I switch brands?**
Click the brand name in the page title and pick another one, or open **Brands** at the bottom of the sidebar.

**Q: Where did the brand sidebar and Back to Workspace go?**
The sidebar no longer changes when you open a brand. Modules are in the main sidebar, and the brand switcher is in the page title.

**Q: Where did Studio go?**
It's no longer a module. KVs and resizes are in the brand's **Files**; open one there, or start a new one from the create menu. Old Studio links take you to Files.

**Q: I can't find Insights in the sidebar. Where is it?**
Under **Modules**, with Research and Audience. It opens for the brand in the page title. If it's greyed out, your role doesn't grant it for that brand.

**Q: Why don't I see a brand's chats under Chats in the sidebar?**
The sidebar's **Chats** list shows chats that aren't tied to a brand. To find a brand's chats, open the chat history in AskFrnd and pick the brand. See [AskFrnd Overview](../askfrnd/overview).

**Q: Some sidebar items look empty or unfinished.**
A few areas are still being built out. This guide focuses on the stable navigation. New capabilities will be documented as they ship.
