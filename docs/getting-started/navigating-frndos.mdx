# Navigating frndOS — The Sidebar

frndOS navigation lives in a **left sidebar**. This is where you move between your main areas, switch brands, and open AskFrnd. This guide covers what's in the sidebar and how to get around.

> **Coming from the old layout?** Navigation used to sit in a top bar with thumbnail icons. It's now consolidated into the left sidebar — brands now live in a **Brands** list there too. Everything you used before is still here — just in a cleaner, more familiar place.

## Two Sidebar States: Home vs. Inside a Brand

This is the most important thing to understand about frndOS navigation: **the sidebar changes shape depending on whether you're at Home or inside a specific brand.** Pillars (Insights, Research, Audience, Growth) only appear once you've entered a brand. They're not available at the Home level.

### Home Sidebar

When you log in, you land on **Home** with this sidebar:

| Item | What It's For |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your overview — all activity and files across the brands you have access to |
| **Tasks** _(beta)_ | Your tasks as a list or a board — see [Tasks](../workspace/tasks) |
| **Files** | The files your team has made, scoped to your access — see [Files](../library/overview) |
| **myFRND** | Plugins, skills, agents, and the tools AskFRND may use — see [myFRND](./myfrnd) |
| **Workflows** _(beta)_ | The agent workflow builder |
| **Search** | Find brands, files, projects, and pages across your workspace |
| **Projects** | A section listing your recent projects and [project rooms](../projects/project-rooms); **See all** opens the Projects page |
| **Brands** | A list of every brand you have access to — click one to enter it |
| **Chat** | Your recent AskFrnd conversations not tied to any brand, plus **New Chat** |

> **Note:** There are no pillars (Insights, Research, etc.) visible at this level. To reach them, click into a brand from the **Brands** list.

> **Heads-up:** **Playground** is listed but not usable yet.

### Creating Something New

The sidebar footer carries a **New Chat** pill and a **+** button. The **+** opens a create menu naming the artifact you want:

| Row | Creates |
|---|---|
| **Key Visual** | A KV in the [KV Generator](../studio/kv-generator) |
| **Presentation Deck** | A [deck](../decks/overview) |
| **Campaign Report** | A report in Insights |
| **Survey** | A [survey](../research/creating-a-general-survey) |
| **Persona** | A persona in Research |
| **Image Generation** | An AI-generated image |

Rows that need a brand ask you to pick one first.

The **New** button on the Files page opens the same menu, without **Campaign Report** (it isn't a file) and with **Resize**, which opens a new [Resizer](../studio/resizer) canvas. It also offers **Import PowerPoint or PDF**.

### Brand Sidebar

Once you click a brand under **Brands**, the entire sidebar transforms to that brand's context:

| Item | What It's For |
|---|---|
| **Brand name** | Replaces the workspace name at the top and confirms which brand is active. Click it to switch brands; the gear beside it opens brand settings |
| **Back to Workspace** | The link under the brand name; returns you to the Home sidebar |
| **Home**, **Files**, **Brand IQ** | The brand's home, its files, and its [Brand IQ](../brand-setup/brand-iq) (with a completeness ring) |
| **Pillars** (Insights, Research, Audience, plus Growth _coming soon_) | The brand's pillars, always in that order. Each opens a page whose own tabs sit in the bar at the top |
| **Projects** | This brand's projects; **See all** opens them |
| **Chat** | Recent AskFrnd conversations scoped to **this brand only**, plus **New Chat** |

> **Where did Studio go?** The brand sidebar no longer has a **Studio** pillar. Your KVs and resizes are in the brand's **Files**, and you open them from there. Start a new one from the **+** create menu (**Key Visual**) or Files' **New** button (**Key Visual**, **Resize**). Old Studio links open Files.

> **Heads-up:** Pillars are **brand-gated**. You must have a brand open to access Insights, Research, or Audience. There's no "pillar view" that works across all brands at once; each brand has its own Insights, its own Audience, and so on.

> **Coming soon:** **Growth** is still being built. It stays in the sidebar wearing a **Soon** chip, dimmed and not clickable, so the overall map still reads correctly.

> **A pillar you can't open still shows.** If your role doesn't grant a pillar for this brand, its row renders greyed out instead of disappearing, so every teammate sees the brand the same shape. Ask a workspace admin for the pillar if you need it (see [Inviting Team Members](../workspace/inviting-team-members)).

> **Note:** The sidebar is actively evolving as frndOS grows. Some entries may appear or change as new capabilities roll out. This guide focuses on the stable, everyday navigation you'll use most.

## Home

**Home** is your starting point. It shows all the activity and files related to the brands you have access to — so even if you've just joined a workspace, you can see what's happening across your brands at a glance.

### The Briefing Hero

Home opens with a **briefing** — labelled **Morning briefing**, **Midday briefing**, or **Evening briefing** depending on the time of day. It's a short written summary of what needs your attention, with an ask box directly under it (_"How can I help you today?"_) so you can start working without going anywhere else.

When there's nothing pressing, it says so: _"Nothing needs your attention right now. What are we working on today?"_ If you don't have brand access yet, it explains that instead — _"You don't have access to any brands yet. Ask your workspace admin to grant you brand access."_

> **The briefing covers the workspace you're in.** If you belong to more than one workspace, the briefing only reads out notifications from the active one. The notification bell still lists every workspace's notifications, each labelled with its workspace (see [Notification Settings](../settings/notifications#reading-a-notification-from-another-workspace)).

### Continue Working

A row of cards for the work you touched most recently — KVs, decks, research, workflows. Click one to pick up where you left off. **See all** takes you to **Files**.

### Team activity

A rail beside Continue Working showing who changed what, and when. Empty states are explicit: _"No activity yet — edits from your team will show up here"_, and _"Team activity shows up in an organization workspace"_ if you're in a personal one.

### Recent Campaigns

A band of campaign cards, each showing the brand, an **On Track** or **Needs Attention** pill, and its key numbers. Search the band to narrow it down.

> **Automatic suggestion cards are gone.** Home used to push a grid of AI-generated brand and meeting suggestions at you. Those have been **retired** — the briefing and its ask box replace them. If you're looking for the suggestion cards, that's why they aren't there.

> **Note:** the Home redesign rolls out per workspace. If your Home looks different from this, it hasn't reached your workspace yet.

## Entering and Switching Brands

To work on a brand, click it under **Brands** in the Home sidebar. This takes you into that brand's dedicated sidebar (Pillars, Chat scoped to that brand, etc.) — see [Brand Sidebar](#brand-sidebar) above.

Entering a brand changes your whole context:
- Which projects and files you see
- Which Brand IQ applies
- Which Insights data loads
- Which credit context is active

**To switch to a different brand**, click the brand name at the top of the sidebar and pick another, or click **Back to Workspace** and choose from the **Brands** list.

> **Creating a brand:** the **+** button beside **Brands** opens the **Add New Brand** dialog (name, description, logo — or let Smart Creation fill it from the brand's website). It only appears if your role can create brands. See [Setting Up Your First Brand](./your-first-brand).

> **Brand IQ row:** the brand sidebar's **Brand IQ** row shows the brand's completeness under its name, for example `21 of 26 · 5 gaps`. Click it to open the brand's Brand IQ page.

## Two Ways to Use Chat (AskFrnd)

One of the most useful things to understand about the new navigation: **AskFrnd has two access points, and they show different chat history.**

### Mode 1 — Side Panel (FAB)

Open AskFrnd from the **floating button (FAB) in the bottom-right corner** of any page and it appears as a panel alongside your current view. This mode is context-aware — it knows which pillar and data you're looking at. Its history view shows **every chat you've had, across every brand and pillar** — useful when you can't remember which brand a past conversation happened in.

### Mode 2 — Full Screen (Sidebar)

Open **Chat** from the **left sidebar** for a dedicated, full-screen conversation view. Its history is **scoped to where you currently are**: from Home, you'll see chats not tied to any brand; from inside a brand, you'll see only that brand's chats.

> **Which should I use?** Use the **FAB** when you want to ask about what you're currently viewing, or when you need to find a chat regardless of which brand it was in. Use the **sidebar Chat** when you want a focused session scoped to your current brand (or Home).

See: [AskFrnd Overview](../askfrnd/overview) for the full breakdown of history scope per access point.

## Pillars

A brand has four pillars, each a workspace for a different kind of work: **Insights**, **Research**, **Audience**, and **Growth**. **You can only access them from inside a brand** (see [Brand Sidebar](#brand-sidebar) above). They always render in that order, whichever ones your role opens:

| Pillar | What It's For | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Insights** | Performance tracking and dashboards. Its tabs are Overview, Campaigns, Business, Ads, Social Media, Influencers, ATL, Dashboard, Trends, Listening ([guide](../insights/overview)) | Available |
| **Research** | Surveys, personas, and studies ([guide](../research/overview)) | Available, marked **Beta** |
| **Audience** | The brand's people: contacts, followers, segments and personas ([guide](../audience/overview)) | Available |
| **Growth** | Distribution and campaign tools ([guide](../growth/overview)) | Coming soon |

Which tabs you actually see inside a pillar depends on your role and on which features your workspace has switched on, so two people can open the same pillar and get a different tab bar.

Every pillar is scoped to whichever brand is currently active. There's no cross-brand pillar view, so switch brands to switch which brand's Insights, Audience, and so on you're looking at.

> **Creative tools aren't a pillar any more.** The KV Generator and Resizer open from the brand's **Files** or the create menu. See [Creative Tools Overview](../studio/overview).

See: [Welcome to frndOS](./welcome) for an overview of the pillars.

## Quick Search — Cmd+K

Press **⌘K** anywhere to open the quick search, and jump to something instead of navigating to it.

With nothing typed it shows what you opened recently, so the common case is two keystrokes.

### Narrowing the search

A sigil in the **first** character narrows the search to one kind of thing:

| Type | Searches |
|---|---|
| `@` | **Brands** — jump to a brand |
| `/` | **Files** — search your files |
| `>` | **Commands** — run a command |
| `?` | **Ask FRnD** — ask anything |
| no sigil | Everything, merged and ranked |

> **Only the first character scopes the search.** A `/` or `@` later in what you type is treated as ordinary text, so a file called `kv 3/4` still finds itself.

### Creating from the search box

Typing a create intent offers to make the thing rather than look for it. **New**, **Create** or **Buat** all work, as does just naming the type:

**New Key Visual** · **New Presentation Deck** · **New Campaign Report** · **New Survey** · **New Persona** · **New Image Generation**

If what you're creating needs a brand and you aren't on one, it asks which brand first.

> **You only see what you can actually make.** The list is filtered by your permissions and by what's enabled for the workspace, so it never offers something that would fail on the next screen.

## Dropping a File Anywhere

Drag a file onto any page and drop it. Instead of guessing, frndOS asks _"What should frndOS do with this?"_ and offers up to four actions that fit the file and what you're allowed to do:

| Action | When it's offered |
|---|---|
| **Edit on a canvas** | An image, inside a brand |
| **Start a project** | A document such as a brief |
| **Import as deck** | A PowerPoint or PDF |
| **Add to \{project\}** | Inside a project room |
| **Ask frnd** | Any file; opens AskFRND with the file attached |

Drops on things that take files themselves, such as a chat box, a deck canvas or an upload area, still go there. Folders can't be dropped; drop the files inside instead.

## Appearance — Choosing Light or Dark

You can choose how frndOS looks on your device. Click the workspace (or brand) name at the top of the sidebar, open **Miscellaneous**, and click **Theme**. The row shows your current theme and a **Beta** tag.

The **Appearance** dialog reads _"Choose how frndOS looks on this device."_ Four options:

| Theme | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **System** | _"Daylight or Midnight, following your device."_ |
| **Daylight** | _"Soft white canvas, dark ink."_ The light theme |
| **Midnight** | _"The classic dark frndOS look."_ |
| **Aurora** | _"Glass panels over a blue horizon."_ |

Each option shows a miniature preview. Select one and the theme applies instantly.

**Per device:** your choice is saved on the device you set it on. It doesn't sync across devices or affect other workspace members.

> The appearance picker is rolling out gradually. If you don't see **Theme** in the menu yet, it will appear once enabled for your workspace.

## FAQ

**Q: My Home briefing mentioned a brand that isn't in this workspace.**
That was a bug, now fixed. The briefing only reads notifications from the workspace you're in. Notifications from your other workspaces stay in the bell, labelled with their workspace.

**Q: I still see the old navigation layout. Why?**
The new sidebar navigation is now in production. If you see an older layout, try refreshing or clearing your cache. If it persists, report it through your agency support group.

**Q: How do I switch brands?**
Click the brand name at the top of the brand sidebar and pick another one, or go **Back to Workspace** and pick from **Brands**.

**Q: Where did Studio go?**
It's no longer a pillar. KVs and resizes are in the brand's **Files**; open one there, or start a new one from the create menu. Old Studio links take you to Files.

**Q: I can't find Insights in the sidebar. Where is it?**
Pillars only appear once you're inside a brand. From Home, click a brand under **Brands**, and the sidebar switches to that brand's pillars: Insights, Research and Audience today, with Growth still coming soon. If a pillar is there but greyed out, your role doesn't grant it for that brand.

**Q: What's the difference between Chat in the sidebar and the AskFrnd panel?**
Same assistant, different history scope. The **FAB** (bottom-right) shows history across every brand and pillar. **Sidebar Chat** shows history scoped to where you are — Home-only, or that specific brand only.

**Q: Some sidebar items look empty or unfinished.**
A few areas are still being built out. This guide focuses on the stable navigation. New capabilities will be documented as they ship.
