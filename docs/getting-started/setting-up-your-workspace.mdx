# Setting Up Your Workspace and Starting Your Trial

When you create a new team workspace, frndOS walks you through a short guided setup. At the end, your **14-day trial** starts. This guide covers each screen, what you can skip, and what to do if you leave halfway.

For what the trial gives you and what happens when it ends, see [Trials, Plans and Read-Only Workspaces](../workspace/trial-and-plans).

## The Steps at a Glance

A row of dots at the bottom of the screen shows where you are:

| Dot | What it asks |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Your name |
| **Workspace** | Name, logo and website |
| **Team** | Teammates to invite |
| **About you** | Your role and where you work |
| **Brand** | Your first brand |
| **Data** | Social and ads accounts to connect |
| **Plan** | Plan choice and card |

If you already have a frndOS account and arrive from another team, the **Profile** dot is left out and the bar counts six steps.

## Before the Setup: Email and Code

1. Go to the sign-up page. You can click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Lark**, or enter your work email, shown as `name@company.com`, and click **Continue with Email**
2. frndOS sends a one-time code. The screen reads **Check your email**: _"Enter the 6 digit code sent to"_ your address. Type the code
3. If it doesn't arrive, wait for the **Resend** timer (it counts down from 30 seconds) and click **Resend**. **Change email** goes back
4. A wrong code says _"That code didn't work. Check it and try again."_

If the email already has an account, the screen says _"You already have an account. This code signs you in."_ If you are on the log-in screen with an email that has no account, it says _"There's no account for this email yet. Enter the code to create one."_

The screens say **Welcome to frndOS** for a new sign-up and **Welcome back to frndOS** when logging in.

## Step by Step

### Profile

_"Let's complete your profile."_ Enter your **Full name**. This is how your team and agents will know you.

### Workspace

Name the workspace: _"This is where your team, brands and files live."_

* **Workspace name** is required
* **Workspace logo** and **Website** are optional. The website, for example `arunika.co`, is checked before it's saved, and a name with no domain ending is refused with a hint like _"Use a web address that starts with http or https, or just the domain, like kopinusa.co.id."_
* If the workspace is made but the logo or website can't be saved, you're told you can add them in Settings. The workspace is not made twice

If you come from another team, the heading reads **Create your workspace** and says _"Your own brands, team and plan."_ Your other team stays as it is, and you can switch back any time. A **Growth free for 14 days** badge shows only when your account can still start a trial.

### Team

_"Invite your team."_ Add **Work emails**, press Enter after each, and click **Send invite** (or **Send N invites**). Teammates share this workspace, its brands and your trial. Click **Skip for now** to do it later.

### About you

_"Tell us about your work."_ _"We'll put the most useful skills and prompts first."_ Pick your role and where you work, then **Continue**, or **Skip**.

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| **Strategist** | Plans campaigns and reads the numbers |
| **Creative** | Makes the visuals, copy and video |
| **Account / Client Service** | Keeps clients and briefs moving |
| **Brand-side Marketer** | Grows one brand from the inside |

For where you work, choose **At an agency** (_"I manage clients' brands"_) or **In-house** (_"I work on my company's brand"_).

### Brand

**Add your brand**, or **Add a client's brand** if you chose **At an agency**. _"Paste the brand's website or social handle and frndOS fills in the basics. You can change anything."_

* Type the website and click **Autofill**. It reads **Reading…** while it works, and **Try again** appears if it fails
* **Brand name** is required. **Description** is optional: _"What the brand sells and who it's for"_
* The website and logo you gave for the workspace are carried over, ready to change, so you don't type them twice
* **Autofill** also picks up the website's icon as the brand's logo, shows it, and saves it with the brand. A logo you pick yourself wins over it

### Data

_"Connect your data."_ _"Add real accounts and frndOS syncs the last 30 days once, so Insights works on"_ your brand itself.

| Group | Accounts |
|---|---|
| **Social account** | Instagram (_"Posts, Reels, reach and saves"_), TikTok (_"Videos and engagement"_) |
| **Ads account** | Meta Ads (_"Ad account spend and results"_), Google Ads (_"Search and YouTube campaigns"_), TikTok Ads (_"Spend, views and conversions"_) |

A tile then reads **Finish connecting**, **Connected · syncing the last 30 days**, or **Connected · last 30 days**. Click **Skip for now** to connect later.

### Plan

**Start your 14-day trial**: _"Pick the plan you want after the trial and add a payment method now, or skip and decide later."_ Each plan shows its price per month and its credits a month. One is marked **Most Popular**. Choose with **Start (plan) Trial**, or click **Skip for later**.

### Card

**Add a card to start your trial**: _"(Plan) is free for 14 days. Your bank checks the card today."_ Enter the card number, expiry, CVV, **Name on card**, **Phone number** and the billing address (street, province, city, postal code). If the bank doesn't accept it, the screen says _"Your bank didn't confirm the card. Try again or use another card."_ **Skip for later** is available.

### Trial started

The last screen reads **Your 14-day trial is on**. With a card saved it says _"Nothing was charged today."_, when the plan starts and its price, _"unless you cancel."_ Without a card: _"Every (plan) feature is open until"_ the end date, _"We'll remind you 3 days before it ends."_

It also shows your **trial pass**, a card you can drag to swing. You can **Download** it as an image or **Share** it. **Share** opens your device's share sheet, or copies the pass image to the clipboard and reads **Copied** (**Copy failed** if the browser blocks it). Click **Go to frndOS** to start.

If the trial can't start, you see **We couldn't start your trial**: _"Nothing was charged. Try again; your setup is saved, so you can also come back to it later."_ A workspace that has no trial reads _"This workspace has no trial. Choose a plan from Settings to use AI."_

## Leaving and Coming Back

Your progress is saved on the server, so closing the tab doesn't lose it.

* **The setup banner.** Until the trial starts, the top of **Home** shows **Finish setup to start your 14-day trial**: _"Your trial starts when you finish setting up your workspace. Until then AI features are closed."_ A progress ring shows how many steps are done, and **Continue setup** takes you to the next step. Close the banner with the dismiss button. It is only closed for that page and comes back the next time Home loads
* **Admins and members** see _"Your workspace owner is finishing setup. AI features open when the trial starts."_ with no button. Only the owner can carry the setup on, because the trial is theirs
* **Opening the onboarding again.** If your workspace is waiting, you're taken to the step it waits at. Steps already saved are shown as saved and locked, and **Continue** only moves on. It never makes a second workspace or a second brand
* **One trial per account.** If you try to create another workspace while your first still waits for its trial, you are taken back to the first one instead

In the workspace list, a workspace on its trial shows **Trial · 9 days left**, or **Trial · ends today** on the last day. The **Add a workspace** row shows the trial badge only when your account can still start one.

## If You Were Invited

An invitation takes you through the sign-up or log-in screen, which reads **Join (workspace name)**, or **Log in to join (workspace name)**, with a line showing who invited you. The email is the invited one and can't be changed. After the code and your profile, you accept the invitation and join the workspace. You don't go through the workspace setup, because the workspace already exists and the owner sets it up.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I skip steps?**
Yes: **Team**, **About you**, **Data**, **Plan** and **Card** can be skipped. The workspace name is required. The trial starts on the last screen, whether or not you added a card.

**Q: Is my card charged?**
Not during the trial. The screen says your bank checks the card today and nothing is charged until the plan starts.

**Q: Why are AI features closed?**
AI opens when the trial starts, which happens at the end of the setup. See the banner on Home.

**Q: I closed the page halfway. Where do I resume?**
Open frndOS and click **Continue setup** on the banner, or open the onboarding page again.
