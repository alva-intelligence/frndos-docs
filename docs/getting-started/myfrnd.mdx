# myFRND — Making Your Assistant More Capable

**myFRND** is one home for everything that makes your assistant more capable. Connect the tools your team already uses, add skills, enable agents, and choose which tools AskFRND may reach for, all in one place instead of hunting through chat modals and workspace settings.

The page carries an **Experiment** badge next to its title: it's still being shaped, so expect it to change.

## Before You Start

1. ✅ **A workspace with AskFRND.** myFRND is in the sidebar for every workspace
2. ✅ **Nothing else.** The Discover tab walks you through the rest

## Opening myFRND

Click **myFRND** in the left sidebar (the sparkle icon). It sits under **Files**, alongside **Workflows**.

The page has five tabs: **Discover**, **Plugins**, **Skills**, **Agents**, **Tools**.

> **Tip:** each tab is linkable. The Chat Settings modal deep-links straight to a specific tab, so a bookmark to one tab keeps working.

### One layout on every tab

Every tab opens straight on the same toolbar row: a collapsed search on the left (press **/** to open it), filter chips beside it, and the tab's own actions on the right.

Items are listed as compact rows, two to a line, each with an icon, a name, a one-line description, and a switch or button on the right. Rows are grouped. A long group ends in **See N more**, and a **›** beside a group's heading jumps to its full list. Click any row to open a side sheet with the details.

### Use in your AI apps

On every tab, the page header carries a **Use in your AI apps** button. It opens the **frndOS MCP server** panel: the server URL with a copy button, and setup steps for **Claude**, **ChatGPT**, **Cursor**, and **Claude Code**.

> **Note:** this points the opposite way from the tabs. Plugins bring outside tools *into* frndOS; the MCP server takes frndOS *out* to the apps you already use.

For the full walkthrough (copying the URL, the approval screen, and what your AI app can then do), see [Connecting Your AI App](../integrations/frndos-mcp-server).

## Discover — Start Here

**Discover** is the front door. The other tabs are directories, and a directory only helps if you already know what you're looking for. Its search covers skills, plugins, and agents at once.

### Get started

A slim **Get started** strip with a progress bar (`N of 3 done`) and three steps, computed from what your workspace actually has:

1. **Connect a plugin**
2. **Turn on a skill**
3. **Enable an agent**

Each step takes you to the right tab. A finished step is crossed out, and the whole strip disappears once all three are done.

### What else is on Discover

Below the strip, Discover is a catalogue of shelves, each with a **See all** link:

| Shelf | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Picked for your brands** | Suggestions matched to your brands. The subtitle names the brands it matched on, and says _"Chosen by frndOS, not by popularity."_ |
| **Essentials for brand teams** | _"The standards most brand work starts from."_ |
| **New in frndOS** | _"Recently added to the frndOS catalog."_ |
| **Plugins** | _"Let AskFRND reach the tools your team already uses."_ |
| **Tools your studio already uses** | _"Connect them once, and AskFRND can work in them too."_ |
| **Agents that do the work** | _"One job each. Call them with @ in AskFRND."_ |

Between the shelves sit **collections**, such as **Plan a Ramadan campaign** and **Build a brand platform**: a few skills and agents in the order you'd use them. **Add all** adds every piece you don't have yet, and **See the collection** opens its own view.

Click **Get** on a tile to add or connect it in place. A tile you already have reads **Added**, and one that isn't available yet reads **Soon**.

## Plugins — Connecting Your Tools

**Plugins** holds first-party integrations and MCP servers in one list.

The toolbar has **Search plugins**, category chips (**All**, then categories such as **Productivity**, **Creative**, and **Data & Research**, each with a count), and **Add custom** on the right for adding your own MCP server by URL.

Top to bottom, the tab shows:

| Group | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Connected** | What you've already connected, with its status |
| **Popular with agencies** | frndOS's own plugins (tagged **frndOS**) and the tools agencies already pay for |
| **New & noteworthy** | Recent additions to the catalogue |
| **Creative**, **Data & Research**, **Productivity** | Six rows each; **›** opens the category's full list |

Picking a category chip, or searching, swaps the shelves for one full list that keeps loading as you scroll. Plugins marked **Soon** are greyed out and always last.

### A connected plugin

Each row under **Connected** shows its status: **Active**, **Disabled**, **Needs re-auth**, **Needs your API key**, or **Authorization not finished**. A status that needs attention is highlighted.

The **⋯** menu on the row offers **Manage**, **Reconnect** (for plugins that sign in through your account), and **Disconnect**. **Manage** and **Disconnect** open the plugin's details in a side sheet.

> **Heads-up:** disabling a plugin and disconnecting it are different. **Disabled** keeps your account connected but stops AskFRND using it.

## Skills — Teaching Your Assistant

**Skills** is one list of everything you have and everything you could add. Search with **Search skills**, or narrow by who made a skill with the chips:

| Chip | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every skill |
| **frndOS** | frndOS standards |
| **Workspace** | Added by someone in your workspace |
| **Mine** | Yours |

Skills are grouped as **Mine** and **Workspace** first, then the frndOS standards by job: **Research**, **Strategy**, **Ideas & creative**, **Planning**, and **Quality**.

A skill you have carries a switch to turn it on or off. One you don't have yet carries **Add**. Click the row to open its sheet: what it does, its instructions (editable if it's yours), **Try in AskFRND**, and **Remove skill** for your own skills. Instructions have a **Preview** / **Raw** switch, so you can read them formatted or see the markdown source. The switch remembers your last choice for the session.

### Creating or importing a skill

The toolbar has **Browse directory** and an add menu with three routes:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Create a skill** | _"Write the instructions here"_ |
| **Import from GitHub** | _"Pin to a public repo or a folder inside one"_ |
| **Browse the directory** | _"Skills shared across frndOS"_ |

## Agents — One Assistant, One Job

The **Agents** tab lists your agents and teams in the same row layout. An agent is described as: _"One assistant, one job. Give it instructions, skills, and a model, then call it with @ in AskFRND."_

The toolbar has **Search agents and teams**, scope chips (**Private**, **Workspace**, **FRnD**, **All**), plus **Browse directory** for a ready-made starting point and **Create agent** to build one from scratch. The chips filter teams as well as agents.

Rows are grouped under **Agents** and **Teams**. **Teams** are _"Several agents under a coordinator. It splits a multi-step request between them, then returns one answer."_

The right side of a row tells you what you can do with it:

* **Always on** — built by FRnD, in every workspace. You can edit it freely, but it can't be turned off, deleted, or shared
* **Ask an admin** — you aren't allowed to turn this one on yourself
* **A switch** — turn it on or off

Click a row to open its sheet: the switch, **Try in AskFRND**, what it does and what it can use, and **Manage** for the full editor.

See [Agents (Beta)](../early-access/agents) for the full walkthrough.

## Tools — What AskFRND May Use

**Tools** is the odd tab out. The other tabs are things you add; this one lists the tools frndOS already ships and lets you decide which ones AskFRND may use on your behalf.

Tools are grouped by what they do: **What frnd can look at**, **What frnd can make**, **What frnd can hand off**, and **More tools**. Most rows carry a switch. A few always-on tools say **Always on** instead.

### Your settings vs. your workspace's

Your workspace sets the defaults. The toolbar says so in one line, for example `Your workspace sets the defaults · 2 changed by you`. Anything you switch here applies to **you only**; your workspace keeps its defaults.

* A row you've changed carries a **Changed** tag. Click it (it reads **Reset** on hover) to put that one tool back to your workspace's setting
* **Reset to workspace** in the toolbar puts every tool back. It only appears once you've changed something

Click a row to open its sheet: whether it's on (**frnd can use it** or **Off for you**), what it reads, makes, or does, which brands it can read for you, and **Try asking** with example questions that open in AskFRND.

## FAQ

**Q: I don't see myFRND in my sidebar.**
It's a regular sidebar item, under **Files**. If it's still missing, refresh the page; features can roll out gradually.

**Q: What's the difference between a plugin and an MCP server?**
Plugins tagged **frndOS** are built and maintained by frndOS with fine-grained permissions and no setup. MCP servers are remote servers from the catalogue, or ones you add yourself with **Add custom**.

**Q: Where did the Added and Browse sections in Skills go?**
They're one list now. Skills you have show a switch, skills you don't have show **Add**. Use the **frndOS**, **Workspace**, and **Mine** chips to narrow it.

**Q: If I switch a tool off in the Tools tab, does it change for my whole team?**
No. It only changes for you. Your workspace's defaults stay as they are, and **Reset to workspace** brings you back in line.

**Q: What's the frndOS MCP server for?**
Using frndOS tools from Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or Claude Code. Open it with **Use in your AI apps** at the top of the page. See [Connecting Your AI App](../integrations/frndos-mcp-server).

**Q: The Get started strip vanished.**
That's intended. It disappears once all three steps are done.
