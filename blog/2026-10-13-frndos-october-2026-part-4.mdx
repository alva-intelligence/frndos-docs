**One portal for your clients, paste and undo for labels, one panel for AskFRND.**

Clients who sign in with the emailed code now see every brand they can reach, and you can edit a client or send a new link from the Clients tab. Label Posts takes copy, paste and undo, KV Studio gets a new header and chat panel, and AskFRND opens a key visual beside the chat. Listening is clearer about pausing, and social sheets carry more channels.

***

## Clients: All Their Brands in One Place

**All your brands, one portal.** A client who signs in with the emailed code sees every brand they can reach, and switches between them from the top bar. Someone who opened a personal link can choose **See all your brands**.

**Edit a client.** Open **Edit details** on a client's row to change their **Title**, **WhatsApp** and **Access ends**.

**New link.** **Regenerate link** makes a new portal link for a client. The old link stops working, and you can email the new one.

**Find clients faster.** The approver field now reads **Search a client or type an email**, so you can look clients up by name.

**A clearer review page.** Clients can filter a request by **All**, **Needs decision** and **Decided**, see artboard names in full, and see how the decisions add up.

👉 [The Client Portal →](/docs/collaboration/client-portal)

👉 [Client Approvals →](/docs/collaboration/client-approvals)

## Label Posts: Paste, Undo and Add in the Grid

**Copy and paste.** Select cells in the table, copy them, and paste into the grid. A toast with **Undo** is one click away.

**Add a label in the cell.** If a value isn't in the dropdown yet, **Register** it without leaving the grid. A value that differs only by capital letters or spacing reuses the label that already exists.

**A tidier grid.** The author shows as an avatar only, and grid view shows each post's format on its thumbnail.

👉 [Owned Media and Label Posts →](/docs/insights/owned-media)

## KV Studio, Motion and AskFRND

**A new KV Studio layout.** The canvas header, the chat panel and the bottom toolbar follow the new design, and you can resize the chat panel.

**Mark many artboards ready to share.** Select several artboards or resizes and mark them, or clear them, in one step.

**Motion transport.** Go to the start or the end and step frame by frame while a clip plays.

**A key visual beside the chat.** When AskFRND starts a key visual, a read-only preview opens in a tab next to the chat.

**One chat panel.** Chat history, page suggestions and mode switching sit in one AskFRND panel.

👉 [Sharing a Key Visual With a Client →](/docs/studio/sharing-with-clients)

👉 [Motion →](/docs/studio/motion-mode)

## Listening and Google Sheets

**Clearer pause and resume.** Pausing a topic now shows what is pending and asks you to confirm. If resuming fails, FRND says so.

**Fuller listening data.** Every topic is rechecked over a wider window every few hours, so mentions the source adds late are caught. Day windows line up and neighbouring days no longer overlap.

**Five channels from Google Sheets.** A social sheet can now carry Facebook, YouTube and X posts as well as Instagram and TikTok. Facebook, YouTube and X rows take their numbers from the sheet.

👉 [Social Listening →](/docs/insights/social-listening)

👉 [Connecting Owned Media →](/docs/insights/connecting-owned-media)

## Files, Campaigns and Fixes

**Previews in Deleted files.** Deleted files show their previews, and you can switch between grid and list.

**The campaign wizard lists every connected account.** Paid, owned, earned and ATL steps now show all the accounts connected to the brand.

* **Links to another workspace:** a link to a record in one of your other workspaces switches the workspace, or tells you which workspace or brand you need.
* **Share link passwords:** passwords up to 72 characters work, and a longer one gets a clear message.
* **Steadier share pages:** public share pages limit how fast links can be requested, so one heavy caller cannot slow them for others.
* **Lark tasks:** sync retries the connection when Lark is slow to answer.

👉 [Files →](/docs/library/overview)

## What's Next

* **Animate with AI in KV Motion:** a chat that plans an animation is built and can be turned on for one workspace at a time.
* **Comments on KV Motion clips:** client comments and smaller pins show in workspaces where comments are on.
* **Pausing a listening topic at the source:** for now, a pause stops the topic in FRND only.

Features may roll out gradually. If something looks missing, a page refresh usually brings it in.
