**Listen, plan and answer faster.**

This update brings Social Listening to Insights, gives every project its own room, and redesigns AskFRND so you can see how it works. Research now leads with the answer, clients get their own portal, and the sidebar is simpler, with one brand switcher in the page title.

***

## Social Listening

**Hear what people say about your brand.** A new **Listening** tab in Insights shows mentions, sentiment and spikes for the topics your frndOS team connects for your brand.

**Voices and sources.** See who drives the conversation in **Voices**, and which sources amplify it in **Sources & Amplifiers**.

**Alerts and a daily recap.** Get spike alerts and a morning or afternoon **Daily Recap** in a Lark group or by email. Quiet hours hold non-urgent alerts, and critical alerts are never held.

**Watchlist.** **Pin to watchlist** the accounts you want to follow, and get an alert when they post about the topic.

**Share and export.** Share a topic with a link, or **Download CSV** to take its data with you.

***

## A Room for Every Project

**One room, four tabs.** Every project opens as a Project Room with **Overview**, **Work**, **Tasks** and **Calendar** tabs.

**Project details at the top.** Set the project type, **Stage**, **Health**, **Lead** and **Due** date. Add milestones and follow the **Activity** log.

**Needs you.** The projects list puts the projects that need you first, and a room shows what is new since you last looked.

**Create into a project.** New decks, canvases, resizes and campaign reports can go straight into a project from its **New** menu.

**An Ask FRND that knows the project.** Ask FRND inside a project already knows its files, people and plans.

👉 [Project Rooms →](/docs/projects/project-rooms)

***

## A Redesigned AskFRND

**Your chat, your way.** Use AskFRND as a floating card, dock it to the side, or go fullscreen.

**See every step.** Each answer shows how long it took, such as **Worked for 14s · 2 steps**, and the steps behind it, like reading an insight page or asking an agent.

**Reply and branch.** **Reply** to a specific answer, or **Branch from here** to take a new direction without losing the thread.

**Suggested answers.** Tap a suggested answer to keep the conversation going.

👉 [AskFrnd Overview →](/docs/askfrnd/overview)

***

## A Simpler Sidebar

**Modules in the main sidebar.** Insights, Research and Audience now sit in a **Modules** section of the main sidebar. You no longer switch to a separate brand sidebar.

**One brand switcher.** Change brand from the page title, on every page.

**Indicators that need you.** Tasks, Calendar and Research show what needs your attention, such as tasks due today, a meeting coming up, and studies ready to read.

**A tidier layout.** **New chat** and **Create** are at the top. **Brands**, **Settings** and a **Getting started** guide are at the bottom.

👉 [Navigating frndOS →](/docs/getting-started/navigating-frndos)

***

## Client Portal and Approvals

**A portal for your clients.** Clients sign in with a 6-digit code sent to their email and see all their approvals in one place.

**Insights for clients.** Choose which Insights tabs clients can see, and use **Preview as client** to check the page before you share it.

**A Clients tab.** In Brand Settings, under Members, the **Clients** tab lets you add a client, set what each one can do and when their access ends, and resend an invite.

**Insights-only clients.** Give a client **Insights only** access. Their portal shows insights and nothing else.

**Approvals for decks and plans.** **Request approval** now works for decks and campaign plans, not only KV artboards. One request can hold up to 200 items.

👉 [Client Approvals →](/docs/collaboration/client-approvals) · [Customizing Insights Tabs →](/docs/settings/insights-tabs)

***

## Research That Answers First

**A new Research overview.** Start with a question. **Your studies** and the research methods sit below it.

**Answer-first study results.** Competitor and desk studies open with the answer and how confident it is, then what we found, what to do next, and the sources.

**An answer-first scorecard.** One page that starts with the verdict, then shows what to fix. Share it or export it as a PDF.

**Fixes become tasks.** **Create tasks** makes one task per fix, with a suggested owner. When the last fix is done, FRND suggests a re-score.

**Review this proposal.** Drop a proposal file and FRND offers to score it. Setup asks for the file first and keeps the rest optional.

**Rubrics and sources.** One **Review rubrics** page shows how a review is judged, and a new sources panel lets you connect the tools your studies use.

👉 [Research Overview →](/docs/research/overview) · [Proposal Scorecard →](/docs/research/proposal-scorecard)

## Also New

**Brand IQ writes your brand summary.** Brand IQ Strategy now opens with FRND's **Summary** of your brand. FRND writes it from your Strategy and keeps it up to date as you edit. Need a fresh take? Click **Regenerate**.

**KV Motion presets and Tune.** Pick from a library of motion presets, or use **Animate all** to apply a style pack to every layer at once. The Tune panel adjusts a preset's direction, distance, duration and easing without starting over.

👉 [Brand IQ →](/docs/brand-setup/brand-iq) · [Motion →](/docs/studio/motion-mode)

## What Changed

* **Tasks:** one-line rows with the status in a circle, a visible **List** and **Board** switch, and a task sheet that floats over the page.
* **Scorecard labels:** scores now read **Ready**, **Nearly ready**, **Needs work** or **Not ready**, on the scorecard, in chat and in exports.
* **Brand settings:** brand settings open inside the main app.
* **Studies list:** your studies show in one table with five clear columns and quick filters.

👉 [Tasks →](/docs/workspace/tasks)

***

## Improved and Fixed

* **Finished studies:** you get one notification when a study is ready or fails, for every study type.
* **Large approvals:** approval pages with up to 200 artboards load quickly.
* **Faster decks and resizes:** the deck slide strip scrolls sideways, and KV resize strips load in one request.
* **Deck stories stay put:** opening a deck no longer wipes its story.
* **Google Sheets:** columns lock only after you save your mapping.
* **Low-credit alerts:** the alert waits until your credits actually run low, not while Extra Credit still covers usage.
* **Pop-up menus:** menus inside pop-up windows work everywhere.
* **KV resize edits:** edits stay on the size you are working on, without flicker.

Features may roll out gradually. If something looks missing, a page refresh usually brings it in.
