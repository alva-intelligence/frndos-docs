**frnd joins the project room, study results travel, and mentions speak your language.**

Mention @frnd in a room to get an answer, a catch-up and a drafted update. Competitor and desk study results can be shared by link and exported. Social Listening can translate mentions and shows how relevant each one is. Files and KV Studio get smoother to work in.

***

## Project Room: frnd Joins the Conversation

**Ask @frnd in the room.** Mention @frnd in a post or reply and frnd answers in the room, with sources. The person who asks pays the credit.

**Since you looked.** The room opens with a short catch-up from frnd on what happened since your last visit.

**Draft with frnd.** In Update mode, **Draft with frnd** fills in health and Done, Blocked and Next. Text you already typed stays.

**Limits that do not lose work.** Each room has an hourly limit for @frnd. Past the limit your post still saves, and a message tells you frnd skipped it.

👉 [Project Rooms →](/docs/projects/project-rooms)

***

## Research: Share and Export Study Results

**Share a study.** Share the results of a competitor or desk study with **Share**. Anyone with the link sees a read-only page with the latest version.

**PDF and doc export.** **Export** offers a **Summary PDF**, a **Full report PDF**, and **Open as a doc in Files**.

**Slides from a study.** Build an editable deck from the study and open it when it is ready.

**Next steps become tasks.** Assign a next step to a teammate with a due date, and tick it off when it is done.

**Scorecards that explain themselves.** A scorecard that did not finish now says why, and **Try again** restarts it when a retry can help.

👉 [Competitor and Desk Studies →](/docs/research/competitor-and-desk-studies)

👉 [Proposal Scorecard →](/docs/research/proposal-scorecard)

## Social Listening and Reports

**Translate mentions.** Turn on mention translation per brand in settings, then press **Translate** on a mention to read it in the language you pick. It uses credits.

**Tracked keywords.** A **Tracked keywords** popover lists the keywords and accounts a topic follows, with mention counts for the period you picked.

**Relevance.** Each mention shows how relevant it is. Sort by relevance, or tick **Include low relevance**. Low-relevance mentions never count in the totals.

**Clearer sources.** Sources get a **Brand** tab, and you can correct the role of each account.

**Google Sheets as a channel.** A connected Google Sheets social sheet can be an owned channel in a report, and a sheet picker narrows the report to the sheets you choose.

👉 [Social Listening →](/docs/insights/social-listening)

👉 [Connecting Owned Media →](/docs/insights/connecting-owned-media)

## Files and KV Studio

**One menu for every file.** Files rows and cards share one menu with **Quick Look**, **Copy link**, **Move to Trash** and more.

**A selection bar.** Select several files for **Move to**, **Set status** and **Move to Trash**. Benchmarks, Audience tables and Label Posts use the same bar.

**Arrow keys in KV Studio.** Move selected layers 1 pixel with the arrow keys, or 10 with Shift, on boards and Resize tiles.

**Replaced images keep their shape.** A replaced image keeps the width you set and fits the new picture.

👉 [Files →](/docs/library/overview)

👉 [KV Generator →](/docs/studio/kv-generator)

## Fixes

* **Project deadlines:** everyone sees the same deadline day, whatever their timezone.
* **Label Posts:** pasting one blank row clears the selected cells.
* **Files header:** controls stay on one row, with the search beside the title.
