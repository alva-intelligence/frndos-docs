**Talk to your deck, trust your numbers.**

This update puts an assistant inside the deck editor that plans, writes and edits slides with you, and brings real brand numbers onto those slides in a click. Audience segments now tell you what a rebuild changed, Custom Dashboards chart Earned and ATL media, and a long list of fixes makes sure the figures in your reports agree with each other.

***

## Decks

**The deck assistant.** Chat inside the editor. It reads the deck, proposes a plan, and builds or edits slides only after you press **Go**.

**Slides appear as they are written.** No more waiting on a spinner: each slide fills in as its part finishes, and text that slightly overflows shrinks to fit.

**Live data on slides.** Put brand performance numbers, audience data, KVs and Studio files straight onto a slide, then refresh them whenever you need the latest figures.

**Templates and chapters.** Save a deck as a template, start the next one from it, and add chapters and slides as you go.

**Style builder.** Define your brand's look once with **+ New style**, and it is offered in every deck after that.

**Share, review and restore.** Share a deck by link, approve it internally, and restore an earlier version from the history.

**Present from frndOS.** Present full screen and move through slides and chapters from the keyboard.

**Video, embeds and charts.** Paste a YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Figma or article link and it lands on the slide. Funnel charts are new, and the positioning matrix becomes a bubble chart.

**Research filed for you.** Research the assistant runs for a deck is saved to your Library as a Study, so the findings outlive the conversation.

👉 [The Deck Assistant →](/docs/decks/deck-assistant) · [Live Data on Slides →](/docs/decks/live-data-blocks) · [Brand Deck Templates →](/docs/brand-setup/deck-templates)

## Audience

**See what a rebuild changed.** Segments show what is new, updated or merged after a rebuild, plus a summary of the last run. Near-duplicate segments are merged, and the counts, averages and follower totals are right.

**Generate segments your way.** Generate segments and choose where they come from.

**Search everything.** Audience search covers every column, your custom attributes and segment names.

**Ask about your audience anywhere.** AskFRND answers questions about people, segments and personas from anywhere in frndOS. In chat, "Customer Intelligence" is now called **Audience**.

👉 [Segments and Personas →](/docs/audience/segments)

***

## Reporting and Content

**Earned and ATL on Custom Dashboards.** Chart earned media and TV, radio, cinema and out-of-home metrics next to the rest of your data.

**Filter a dashboard by account.** Connected accounts are now a filter in the dashboard's **Filters** panel.

**Paid Media filters that exclude.** Filter by campaign objective, and exclude as well as match, for example campaigns that do not contain a word. Filters also stay put when the page reloads.

**Owned Media labels.** Label your posts, then filter your numbers by label.

**Google Sheets as a source.** Connect a sheet as an Owned Media source, with a mapping step. If the sheet has nothing to read, frndOS tells you why instead of waiting forever.

👉 [Insights Overview →](/docs/insights/overview) · [Connecting Owned Media →](/docs/insights/connecting-owned-media)

***

## KV Studio

**Edit part of an image.** Draw a box on a tile and regenerate only that area, leaving the rest as it was.

**Style just the words you select.** Set the size and colour of the selected words, not the whole text box.

**Motion bumpers.** Store a brand logo or short clip once and reuse it at the start or end of any video.

**Edit on the canvas.** Move, rotate and scale a layer directly on the canvas, and the keyframe is written where you drag.

👉 [Motion →](/docs/studio/motion-mode) · [Resizing Visuals →](/docs/studio/resizer)

***

## Also New and Improved

* **Credits by brand:** see usage broken down by brand, tool, action and person, and export it.
* **Quick search:** **⌘K** finds files and brands, remembers what you used recently, and offers Create actions.
* **Numbers you can trust:** each Paid Media creative appears once, an objective filter no longer doubles spend, "Worst posts" ranks the lowest performers, and ATL KPI cards match their charts.
* **Dashboards save reliably** and always match their own filters.
* **One look for charts** across decks, dashboards and generated content, and loading states that show work is still happening instead of a dash.
* **Tables** size to their rows, with sticky headers and a pinned first column.
* **KV Studio fixes:** locked layers stay locked, text edits keep their line spacing and styling, and motion exports come out with sound and animation.

***

## What's Next

Features may roll out gradually as connected accounts re-authorize. If something looks missing, a page refresh usually brings it in.
