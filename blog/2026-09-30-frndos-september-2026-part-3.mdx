**Pitch it, brand it, score it, get it approved.**

This update is about getting work out the door with confidence. Your pitch now reads like a story and dresses itself in your client's look. Brand IQ becomes a Brand Book you can edit and keep current. A proposal can be scored before it goes out, and clients can approve it from a single link, no account needed.

***

## Pitch: A Story From Brief to Deck

**Editorial step pages.** Each pitch step reads like a page in a magazine, and decisions are clear radio cards.

**Pitch slides in your client's look.** FRND reads the brief and dresses the pitch in the client's own colours, fonts and tone. Every Big Idea direction becomes a short slide set: the idea, how it works, and the concept image.

**A story-built pitch deck.** FRND turns the whole pitch into one story and builds the deck from it.

**Creative Platform.** "KV Concepts" is now **Creative Platform**: the device that makes the idea show up everywhere. The campaign step speaks agency too, with phases, campaign elements and activations. Titles you renamed yourself are kept.

👉 [Pitch (Beta) →](/docs/early-access/pitch)

***

## Brand Book: Brand IQ as a Living Guideline

**An agency-style brand guideline.** The **Brand Book** shows Brand IQ as a live guideline that updates when the brand data changes.

**Edit book.** Change colours, fonts, type scale and pages from one side panel, with undo.

**Set it up with AskFRND.** AskFRND interviews you about the gaps, reads several brands from one document, and edits brand details in chat.

**Business priorities.** A new **Business** tab in Brand IQ holds the brand's priorities and KPI results, ready for AskFRND and agents to use.

👉 [The Brand Book →](/docs/brand-setup/brand-book)

***

## Proposal Scorecard

**Score a proposal before it goes out.** Check a proposal against a rubric and get persona reactions and next steps. Scoring now takes about 1.5 minutes, down from about 3.5.

**Scorecards from chat.** Ask AskFRND to run a scorecard and read its findings. You can switch this off in **Chat Settings → Tools**.

**Your own rubrics.** Keep your own scoring rubrics and reuse them.

**Preview tabs.** Files, artifacts, decks and workflow runs open right beside the chat.

👉 [Proposal Scorecard →](/docs/research/proposal-scorecard)

***

## Client Approvals From One Link

**No account needed.** Send your client one link. They open a decision page, approve or ask for changes, and you see their answer in frndOS.

👉 [Client Approvals →](/docs/collaboration/client-approvals)

***

## Live Data on Your Decks

**Quick edit in place.** Edit brand data, charts, personas and Studio files right on any deck, from one shared editor.

**Used in.** See every deck that shows a piece of brand data. One change updates all of them.

**Insert by kind.** Add live data to a slide by kind: charts, brand data, personas or Studio files.

👉 [Live Data on Slides →](/docs/decks/live-data-blocks)

***

## A Calmer Workspace

**Files.** A calmer Files page with thumbnails, Quick Look, keyboard control, a brand tab, group by, and bulk select, move and import.

**Tasks board.** A kanban board with drag and drop, **Doing** and **Waiting for review** columns, and an archive.

**myFRND Discover.** An app store–style catalogue of plugins, skills, agents and tools, with a Workflows page in the same layout.

**Drop a file anywhere.** Drop a file on any page and FRND suggests what to do with it.

**Daylight theme.** A light appearance, beside Midnight, Aurora and follow-your-system.

**Label Posts.** Label posts in a full-screen view, with progress, account picking and inline editing in Content Posts. Owned content funnels now show comment, save, share and view totals for each stage.

👉 [Files →](/docs/library/overview) · [Tasks →](/docs/workspace/tasks) · [myFRND →](/docs/getting-started/myfrnd)

***

## Audience

**Activity feed.** See who changed what in a brand's audience: imports, edits, enrichment, segments and personas.

**Follow and notifications.** Follow a brand's audience and get notified, now also by email, with your own quiet hours.

**Enrichment quality.** Choose **High** or a cheaper **Low** quality per brand in Audience Settings. Each run shows its quality and price.

**Smarter imports and phone support.** A CSV import asks about unclear shop columns instead of guessing, and Audience now fits a phone screen.

👉 [Audience Overview →](/docs/audience/overview)

***

## KV Studio

**Resizes on the Craft canvas.** Every size sits under its board on one canvas. Select a size to edit, regenerate, upscale or chat about it. The separate Resize tab is gone.

**Version history per size.** Preview and restore an earlier version of any size, or cancel a render back to the last one.

**Edits become variants.** Editing a board that already has sizes makes a new variant, so the sizes you shipped stay intact.

**Safe zone templates.** Ready-made safe zones for each platform, opened with **Shift+S**.

**Faster Motion editing.** Drag several layers at once, paste segments at the playhead, and add a second run of an effect.

👉 [Resizing Visuals →](/docs/studio/resizer)

***

## Also New and Improved

* **For admins:** creating a Motion and a layer-composed resize now have a fixed price, shown before you start. Superadmins can invite new people and manage pending and declined invites, and reset a member's credit limit.
* **Clearer invoices:** one Platform Fee line, billed to your workspace's billing details, with the right billing period.
* **One Filters panel** for Owned and Paid Media accounts and labels, and a Paid Media funnel that matches the Owned Media and Campaign design.
* **Big Idea generation** is reliable and stays on the brief.
* **Facebook and YouTube reach** shows a dash instead of 0, and brand totals count only platforms that report reach.
* **AskFRND attachments:** if AskFRND can't read a file, it says so, and PDF text reads cleanly.
* **Fixes:** the home briefing shows only your active workspace, revoked invite links show a dead-link screen, and duplicated KV boards show at once.
