**frndOS now opens on chat, and your tools sit in one place.**

Projects and chats live in the sidebar, and every tool opens from the Apps launcher. Radar, our market view, is live with six tabs. New owners get a guided setup and a 14-day free trial. Docs, Sheets, the Studio canvas and the Report Generator join the workspace, and AskFRND runs on newer models.

***

## Workspace: A Chat-First Sidebar and Apps

**Projects and chats in the sidebar.** New and Search rows sit at the top, your projects and chats below them, and your workspace at the bottom.

**Apps replaces Modules.** An **Apps** section and an **All apps** launcher hold your tools. Admins turn apps on or off in **Workspace Settings → Apps**.

**Pin your brands.** Pin the brands you use most so they stay at the top of the brand switcher.

**Invitations in the app.** Workspace invitations show up inside frndOS. Accept or decline them there.

👉 [Navigating frndOS →](/docs/getting-started/navigating-frndos)

👉 [Apps →](/docs/settings/apps)

***

## Radar: Your Market in Six Tabs

Radar is live for everyone, with **Overview**, **Prediction**, **Trends**, **Listening**, **Explore** and **Industry**.

**Overview.** The daily briefing, the front page, trends, conversation and industry news for your brand on one screen.

**Industry.** Competitor moves, regulator updates and industry news, summarised each morning.

**Prediction.** A ranked list of suggested actions for the next 30 days, each with a date and a confidence level.

**Explore.** A full-screen map with a timeline. Ask FRND about any item, and use **Export CSV** to take the raw data with you.

**Share and comment.** Share an Overview page, an Industry page or a single story by public link, and comment on stories.

**Your tabs.** Rename Radar tabs, and show or hide them, for the whole workspace or per brand.

👉 [Radar Overview →](/docs/radar/overview)

👉 [Radar Prediction →](/docs/radar/prediction)

## AskFRND: Radar Answers and Newer Models

**Ask about Radar.** Ask FRND reads Radar Explore maps and counts and Prediction forecasts, and answers questions about them.

**Newer models.** The AI features and the Ask FRND system agents run on newer Claude and OpenAI models.

**Your language.** Ask FRND replies in the language you last typed.

👉 [Asking AskFRND About Radar →](/docs/radar/askfrnd)

## Onboarding: Guided Setup and a 14-Day Trial

**Step by step.** New owners go through sign-up, workspace setup and brand setup one screen at a time.

**Email code.** A new account confirms its email with a 6-digit code.

**14-day free trial.** A new owner workspace starts a free trial, with a downloadable trial pass and a **Trial** badge in the workspace switcher.

**Setup banner.** A banner on Home shows your next setup step. You can close it.

**Setup resumes.** If you leave halfway, setup picks up at the right step instead of sending you back.

👉 [Setting Up Your Workspace and Starting Your Trial →](/docs/getting-started/setting-up-your-workspace)

## Create and Report: Docs, Sheets, Studio and Reports

**Docs.** Write documents inside frndOS. Ask FRND can read and use them.

**Sheets.** Work in spreadsheets inside frndOS. Ask FRND can read and edit them.

**Studio canvas.** A node-based canvas for building images step by step, with an agent that builds and changes the steps for you.

**Find objects.** In the Region tool, find the objects in an image, pick one and give it its own prompt.

**Report Generator.** Build a report from a brief, and schedule it to run again.

**Research Studio.** A workspace for research studies, from brief to findings.

**Numbers as of a past day.** Pick which day a report reads its numbers from. The report says how old the numbers are.

## Pitch and Appearance

**Decisions.** What the team chose, such as the winning strategy, is saved and used by every step and chat.

**Team review.** Each step has an owner and a reviewer. Use **Request review** in the step toolbar.

**Orion theme.** A calm theme with clean panels and one accent colour. Pick it under **Appearance**.

👉 [Pitch (Beta) →](/docs/early-access/pitch)

## Changed, Improved and Fixed

**Plans.** Features that spend credits, and scheduled runs, need an active plan. A workspace with no plan, or with an ended trial, is read-only. Billing users can still choose a plan.

**Ended trials.** A workspace whose trial ended says when it will be deleted and how to keep it. You get a warning 7 days before.

**Radar competitors.** A competitor needs only a website.

**Safer share links.** Public share links are harder to guess.

**Fixed.** Logo search for a new brand finishes instead of hanging. Unfinished report drafts stay private until they are ready. Muse Spark models show the Meta logo.

👉 [Trials, Plans and Read-Only Workspaces →](/docs/workspace/trial-and-plans)
