**Write it, send it, score it, automate it. All in fewer steps.**

This month brings four new ways to move work forward in frndOS. Your deck story gets its own calm writing page. Clients can approve a whole set of key visuals from a single link. A mixed proposal file can be scored part by part. And AskFRND can now build a workflow right inside the chat.

***

## Story: Write It Page by Page

**A quiet writing mode for your deck.** Switch the editor dock to **Story** and you get one calm page for your story: a card for every slide, grouped into chapters. Use **+ Add a card** between cards, move a card, or remove it, and undo if you change your mind.

**AI talk tracks beside every slide.** FRND drafts a talk track for each card, and the slide sits right next to the words you'll say over it.

**Build a slide per card.** Pick a layout for a card and FRND builds the slide for you. Cards without a slide yet show a suggested slide in their empty spot.

**Chapter menu and quick peek.** Jump between chapters from the chapter menu, and peek at any slide without leaving the page.

👉 [Writing the Story →](/docs/decks/story-view)

***

## Client Approvals From One Link

**Send a whole set of KV artboards in one request.** Mark the artboards that are ready, click **Request approval**, and send one link. Each artboard gets its own decision, so the client can approve some and ask for changes on others.

**Pins and notes on any artboard.** Clients pin notes exactly where they mean them, using the same comment card your team already knows.

**A quick email check.** Before they decide, clients confirm their email with a short code. Pins they placed earlier still count.

**Finished previews and one digest.** Artboards show a finished preview, and you get one digest email with the decisions. Changed your mind? You can withdraw a request.

👉 [Client Approvals →](/docs/collaboration/client-approvals)

***

## Score a Proposal Part by Part

**Scorecard Parts.** Drop in a mixed file and FRND finds its parts. You confirm them, each part gets its own score, and you can re-score one part without redoing the rest.

**One report you can share.** Every file gets one scorecard report with live charts and evidence pages. Share it with a link, save it as a **PDF**, or turn it into slides with **Present as slides**.

**Easier setup.** Set up a scorecard in minutes, or ask AskFRND to build it for you.

👉 [Proposal Scorecard →](/docs/research/proposal-scorecard)

***

## AskFRND Builds Workflows in Chat

**Build a workflow by asking.** Ask AskFRND to create a workflow and watch it appear in a live preview beside the chat. When you want the full picture, **Open builder canvas** takes you to the builder.

**Turn tools on with one click.** If a tool you need is switched off, AskFRND shows a card for it. Turn it on and your request carries on from there.

**Rich answers for everyone.** AskFRND answers can now include cards, charts and tables, not only text.

👉 [Workflows (Beta) →](/docs/early-access/workflows)
