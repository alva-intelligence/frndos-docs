**Learn what works, talk it through, comment on the moment.**

This update brings a Playbook to Owned Media that reads your own posts and says what works and what does not. Project rooms get a place to post updates and decisions, and motion clips get comments pinned to an exact moment. Tables read more clearly, the workspace and brand switchers share one new menu, and a few things that kept going wrong now work.

***

## A Playbook From Your Own Posts

**Know what works.** A new **Playbook** card on Owned Media reads your labelled posts and says what works and what does not, with the numbers behind it.

**Read it your way.** Read the playbook **By objective**, or **By pillar** when your brand has content pillars.

**Create it once, refresh it later.** Build a playbook for a brand, then **Refresh** it when you have posted more. When a chapter needs a longer history, FRND says so.

**Lessons you can act on.** Each chapter opens with a summary and a finding. Lessons are written as do and do not, and each one points at the posts it came from.

**Turn it into a deck.** Choose **Generate deck** from the **More** menu, and AskFRND builds a deck from the playbook.

👉 [Creating and Refreshing the Playbook →](/docs/insights/owned-media)

***

## Updates and Decisions in the Project Room

**Write to the team inside the room.** Post a note, share a progress **Update** with **Done**, **Blocked** and **Next**, or log a **Decision**. Teammates can reply.

**Client decision-makers on the project.** The people who decide on a project now sit at the project, under **Client side**, not on each proposal. Every proposal in the room shares one list, and **Add decision-maker** adds someone.

👉 [Project Rooms →](/docs/projects/project-rooms)

***

## Comments on a Motion Clip

**Comment on an exact moment.** In Motion, leave a comment at a point on the timeline. Each note keeps its size and layer, so the next person knows what it was about.

**See what is still open.** Size tiles show how many comments are open, and you can narrow the list by size or layer.

👉 [Motion →](/docs/studio/motion-mode)

***

## Clearer Tables and One New Menu

**Format, at a glance.** **Content Format** is now **Format**. It shows as an icon with a tooltip in the posts table, the post detail and the labelling table.

**Label Posts loads every account you tick.** It also shows each post's author, and has the same **Group by** as the insight tables, up to three levels.

**One new menu.** The workspace and brand switchers use the same menu, and it looks the same in Midnight, Aurora and Daylight.

***

## Improved and Fixed

* **Faster pages:** flag checks and cross-origin checks are cached, so pages answer sooner.
* **Playbook keeps up:** refresh it when you have posted more, and it says when it needs a longer history.
* **Lark plugins:** all 17 plugins are back on, and a token refresh can no longer switch off a plugin that works.
* **Safari:** motion clips show, play and export in Safari instead of showing black or failing.
* **Gemini image models:** some newer Gemini image models failed to run. They work again.

Features may roll out gradually. If something looks missing, a page refresh usually brings it in.
